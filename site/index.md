## Finding a rule

- **Search** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>, or <kbd>/</kbd>) by topic: *close assault*, *water*, *breakdown*, *Tobruk*.
- **Browse by system** in the rules sidebar: each page covers one part of the game, in the order you play it.
- **Coming from the rulebook?** Every block is tagged with the SPI case numbers it restates (for example
  *SPI 8.37*), so you can match it to your printed copy. The [glossary](/rules/glossary) explains the game's terms.

## What this is, and what it isn't

- It is a **restatement** of the rules in our own words, organised by system. It is not SPI's text, and
  no map art or counter art is reproduced. The map and tables are game facts, redrawn and retyped.
- **SPI's September 1979 errata are applied.** Each correction is marked where it applies, and the
  [changes page](/rules/changes) lists them all.
- **Ambiguities are settled as rulings** you can dispute. Every ruling records the options and the
  reasoning, and anyone can [propose a change](https://github.com/basmith7/cna/issues).
- It is written to be precise enough that a computer could enforce it, as groundwork for an
  open-source digital version of the game.
- The rules are **provisional**: they will be revised as that engine is built against them.

Not affiliated with SPI or its successors. The game's name is used only to say which game these rules describe.
