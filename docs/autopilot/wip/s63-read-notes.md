## Printed oddities needing a ruling
1. 63.2 start "Game-Turn 1OZ" (= 102?). The end of The Last Chance reads "3rd OpStage of Game Turn 102 1st OpStage of Game-Turn 111", which looks like two clauses run together. Encoded 102/1 to 102/3, unconfirmed. The Long Retreat ends with the "December 3/IV, 1942" Game-Turn. I set 111/3 as a placeholder; it needs checking against the turn calendar.
2. 63.41 12th Bersaglieri printed at E3133, inside the Commonwealth area. Probably D3133.
3. 63.31 the 50th Inf Div is listed at "2501" with no map letter; read as E2501.
4. 63.74 prints Tobruk at Level Five. Ruling R-027 sets 2.
5. 63.82 B: the "Map D Dumps" supply units are placed on maps A-C. That contradicts their name.
6. 63.83 B/C refer to "63.72 B/C". Read as 63.82.
7. 63.43: the scan shows "nine Airfields" with "eight" struck through or inserted in the OCR. Check this.
8. 63.31 7th Armd Div gives the 4th Armd Bde as Royal Scots Greys (8/10), but the 10th Armd Div is "Less: SctsGray". The same unit is listed twice.
9. 63.91 the tactical victory is a TOE-on-enemy-map comparison; the schema can't express it, so it's in notes.
10. 63.92 uses the level "Substantive", and the schema's points victory has no thresholds field.
11. 63.32 footnotes */†/‡ attach the Stuarts to the 1st SA and 2nd NZ. The parent column marks are garbled ("9/+‡", "-†").
