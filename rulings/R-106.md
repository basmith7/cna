# R-106 — Two Italian tank battalions with strengths but no starting hex

## Problem

62.42 gives tank strengths to the I(L) and II(L) light tank battalions, but
62.41 never places them.

## Options

1. **With Ariete.** Both start attached to the Ariete division, as their
   sheets say they arrived, at the strengths 62.42 gives.
2. **Not in play.** The strengths are a leftover; the battalions are absent.

## Decision

Option 1. Both battalions start attached to the Ariete division at Bir el Gubi, at the strengths 62.42 gives.

## Rationale

62.42 gives them strengths, which only matters if they are in play, and
their own OA sheet says they arrived attached to Ariete; Ariete is on the
map in the Crusader set-up. Dropping them (option 2) would discard printed
figures to explain a missing line. The data now places them with Ariete.

Decided under Brian's delegation of 2026-09-26; open to dispute and
supersession like any ruling (see [rulings/README.md](README.md)).
