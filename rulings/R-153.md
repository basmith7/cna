# R-153 — The *San Giorgio* needs no ammunition

## Problem

30.17 has the half-sunk *San Giorgio* operate "as if" it were an artillery
unit, and [R-151](R-151.md) gives it 2 barrage and 6 AA points. Artillery
pays for every barrage: under abstract supply (32.21) a fixed amount per
battalion or company equivalent from a supply unit in range, and in the
Logistics Game ammunition per TOE strength point that fires. The wreck has
no ID code, TOE, size or strength points, so neither charge can be worked
out, and an engine needs to know whether it must have Axis supply in range
to fire at all.

## Options

1. **No ammunition.** It is a naval unit: like the Commonwealth ships, its
   gun rating is used as artillery points that need no ammunition, fuel or
   water. Its barrage, its fire at ships (30.39) and its AA draw on no
   supply unit, in either supply system, and it can never be out of
   ammunition. Since it owes no ammunition it is not a defender that holds
   Tobruk against an out-of-ammunition surrender ([R-129](R-129.md)); land
   units cannot attack it in any case.
2. **Charge it as one artillery battalion-equivalent.** Under abstract
   supply each barrage or anti-ship shot costs 2 ammunition, doubled when
   the Axis player is phasing, from an Axis supply unit in range of
   Tobruk; in the Logistics Game it spends ammunition for 2 gun strength
   points. With none in range it is out of ammunition and silent.
3. **Charge it as a company-equivalent**, half of option 2 (rounded up),
   with the same supply-in-range test.

## Decision

Option 1. The *San Giorgio* fires its barrage, anti-ship and AA points
without ammunition and needs no supply unit in range, under abstract supply
and in the Logistics Game alike.

## Rationale

The printed text supports it. 30.16 frees naval units from fuel,
ammunition and water, and 30.22 says a ship's gun rating counts as actual
artillery points "except" that it needs no ammunition; the *San Giorgio*
is introduced in the same section as the Italians' one ship, and 30.39 has
it fire with its gun rating, the very figure 30.22 exempts. "As if it
were an artillery unit" in 30.17 describes how it fires (barrage into
adjacent hexes, the 30.35 shot at ships), not what feeds it, just as
30.22's "as if they were artillery units" does for the Commonwealth guns.
Options 2 and 3 would invent a size and a per-point charge for a piece
that prints neither, and the §32 list of what needs no supply already
names the fleet. No exploit follows: the wreck cannot move, fires only
while an Axis land unit shares Tobruk ([R-151](R-151.md)), and its 2
points are the weakest gun on the map. It is also the simplest rule to
enforce: one fewer supply trace. This settles the ammunition question
R-151's Rationale left to the general artillery rules.
