# R-083 — Are Tobruk's blocking and clearing costs swapped?

## Problem

Blocking a port costs 25 Ammunition and 10 Stores Points per level (55.22),
but Tobruk costs 50 and 25 (55.23). Clearing costs 50 and 25 per level
(55.26), but Tobruk costs only 25 and 10. So Tobruk alone is dearer to block
than to clear, while every other port is the reverse. That may be design
(Tobruk's harbour is open and hard to choke) or a transposition.

## Options

1. **As printed.** Consequence: Tobruk is hard to deny to an enemy and quick
   to restore; the other ports are cheap to wreck and dear to repair.
2. **Tobruk costs follow the general pattern** (block 25/10, clear 50/25, the
   Tobruk exception dropped). Consequence: a retreating side can block
   Tobruk as easily as any other port.

## Decision

Option 1. Tobruk's costs stand as printed: 50 Ammunition and 25 Stores to block a level, 25 and 10 to clear one.

## Rationale

The Tobruk figures are stated twice, once as a blocking exception (55.23)
and once as a clearing exception (55.26), each explicitly naming Tobruk; a
transposition would need the same error made in two separate cases. The
result also fits the port: the options note Tobruk's harbour as wide and
open, hard to choke and easy to reopen.
Neither reading creates an exploit, so the printed
text governs; the Construction Chart and the tables here already carry the
printed figures, so nothing in the data changes.
