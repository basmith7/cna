# R-116 — Commonwealth squadron size before July 1941

## Problem

35.23's text gives a Commonwealth squadron a ready capacity of 15 (20 with
reserve) until June 1941 and 18 (24) from July 1941. The Squadron Capacity
Chart printed with the shared charts (35.23, chart page 10) gives 12 (16)
for "1940-41" and 18 (24) for "1942-43", leaving the second half of 1941
unclear. Both scans of the chart agree; the Italian and German rows match
the text.

## Options

1. **The rules text.** 15/5 to June 1941, 18/6 from July 1941; the chart's
   row labels and first row are taken as an older draft.
2. **The chart.** 12/4 through 1941, 18/6 from January 1942.
3. **The chart's figures on the text's dates.** 12/4 to June 1941, 18/6
   from July 1941.

## Decision

Option 1. The rules text: a Commonwealth squadron is 15 ready and 5 reserve to June 1941, 18 and 6 from July 1941.

## Rationale

The text gives both figures and a date, and adds a sentence pointing out
the July 1941 increase, so it is a deliberate statement rather than a slip.
The chart's rows are labelled by whole years, leave the second half of 1941
unassigned in effect, and are at odds with that explicit date; they read as
an earlier draft that was not updated. Option 2 would also leave the
Commonwealth with a smaller squadron than the Germans' (12) for the
1940–41 campaigns, though RAF squadrons were the larger formation.
Option 3 mixes the two sources without support from either. For an engine
the text gives one date switch.

Decided under Brian's delegation of 2026-09-26; open to dispute and
supersession like any ruling (see [rulings/README.md](README.md)).
