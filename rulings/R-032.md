# R-032 — Convoy bombing and fleet bombardment without the Air Game

## Problem

Without the Air Game, 48.0 skips only segments 1 and 2 of convoy resolution, so segment 3 (convoy bombing) still runs; §58 says convoys are then attacked by the §32 abstract procedure and that the Axis may attack the Commonwealth fleet by that procedure too. The §32 procedures had their own timing in the Land Game outline (convoy attacks at the convoy stage; fleet bombardment with plotting and execution segments). The Logistics sequence names no place for the abstract fleet bombardment, nor says whether the abstract convoy attack is made in segment 3.

## Options

1. **Convoy attack in segment 3, fleet bombardment where §32 put it.** Consequence: minimal change; the engine reuses the §32 hooks unchanged.
2. **Both in segment 3 of convoy resolution, once per turn.** Consequence: one attack slot per turn; fleet bombardment becomes weekly rather than per stage, which weakens it.
3. **Convoy attack in segment 3; fleet bombardment in the Commonwealth fleet phase (E) of each stage.** Consequence: bombardment falls next to fleet assignment, where the ships' positions are fixed; it stays per stage as in §32.

## Decision

Option 3. Without the Air Game the abstract convoy attack is made in convoy-resolution segment 3, and each Axis strike on the fleet is plotted during fleet assignment and resolved in the Commonwealth fleet phase (E) of that operations stage.

## Rationale

48.0 skips only the first two convoy-resolution segments when the Air Game
is absent, so segment 3 still runs; it is the natural and only slot for the
convoy attack that 58.1 hands to the §32 procedure. For the fleet, 32.73
plots the strike during the Commonwealth fleet assignment segment and
resolves it in the tactical naval movement segment of the same stage. The
Logistics sequence keeps the fleet assignment segment (phase E) but has no
tactical naval movement segment, so option 1 cannot be applied as written.
Option 3 keeps everything 32.73 fixes — secret plotting against fixed ship
positions, one strike per operations stage — and only moves the resolution
to the end of the phase where the plot is made. Option 2 would cut the strike
rate to one a turn, which 32.73 does not allow for. An engine needs one hook
in convoy segment 3 and one at the close of phase E.

Decided under Brian's delegation of 2026-09-26; open to dispute and supersession like any ruling (see [rulings/README.md](README.md)).
