# R-003 — When is a unit "in Contact"?

## Problem

Four cases describe the moment a unit becomes liable for the break-off cost,
and they do not name the same moment. 8.62 defines Contact by where a unit
stands *when a movement segment opens*: inside an enemy ZOC then, it is in
Contact. 8.24 instead charges the phasing unit whose *phase* opens with it
inside an enemy ZOC. 8.61 speaks of a *friendly* movement segment. 8.64 adds
the retreat-before-assault step and excludes reaction, and 13.22 makes a
non-phasing unit retreating before assault pay the same cost. NJHarman's
reading:[^nj-mov]

> Units are in Contact (only) if under an EZOC at the beginning of any
> Movement Segment [8.62]
>
> *[8.24] says any Movement Combat Phase. [8.61] says Friendly Movement
> Segment. [8.64] confuses things. [13.22] implies Contact can be also be
> applied during RTR. Beginning of Movement Segment [8.62], implying some
> sort of previous combat between units, makes most sense to me.*

[^nj-mov]: NJHarman, *CfNA House Rules & Interpretations*, § Movement,
  <https://friendorfoe.com/war/cfna/houserules/#movement>, CC-BY-SA 4.0
  (tagged REMINDER/CLARIFICATION). See `ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## Options

1. **Contact is tested at the start of every movement segment, either
   side's** (NJHarman). A unit in an enemy ZOC at that instant is in
   Contact until it breaks off; a unit that enters an enemy ZOC mid-segment
   is not yet in Contact and is stopped by the ZOC rules instead.
   Consequence: one test, one marker, and the retreat-before-assault and
   reaction cases fall out of 8.64 as stated (RBA pays, reaction does not).
2. **Contact is tested only at the start of the phasing player's own
   segments** (8.61 literally). A non-phasing unit is never "in Contact"
   during the enemy half, so 13.22's break-off charge would have to rest on
   the Engaged state alone. Consequence: retreat before assault out of an
   enemy ZOC becomes free unless Engaged, which 13.22 plainly does not
   intend.
3. **Contact is continuous — any unit in an enemy ZOC at any instant is in
   Contact.** Consequence: a unit that moves adjacent and is then forced to
   move again in the same segment would owe a break-off cost that no case
   charges; the marker becomes redundant with the ZOC itself.

## Decision

None yet — proposed.

## Rationale

The restated rule already follows option 1 ([Movement — Breaking
off](../rules/40-movement.md#breaking-off)): Contact is a state fixed at the
start of a movement segment and shown with a marker. 8.62 is the definition;
8.24 and 8.61 describe when the *phasing* player pays, and 8.64 and 13.22
extend the charge to the non-phasing player's retreat before assault while
exempting reaction. Read that way the four cases agree, and only option 1
gives 13.22 something to bite on. Option 3 is not supported by any case; the
one-sentence definition in 8.62 would be pointless if Contact were simply
"in a ZOC".
