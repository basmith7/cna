# V-007 — Italian squadrons in Sicily at the start of Group One

## What the printed rules say

In Group One every Italian plane starts in Libya; none in Italy or Sicily
([Group One](../rules/scenarios/10-the-italians.md#axis)).

## The change

NJHarman's house rule:[^nj]

> CHANGE  Ignore [60.32] Italy/Sicily basing prohibition. Start with 8x
> SM.79, 2x CR.42 and 2x CR.32 twelve plane squadrons based in Sicily. Treat as
> if they were assigned to Strategic Air for GT1. No refit until GT2.
> I read forum arguments, forget where, about no way were there this many
> aircraft in Africa.

[^nj]: NJHarman, *CfNA House Rules & Interpretations*, § Scenario Notes, 64.0 The Campaign For North Africa,
  <https://friendorfoe.com/war/cfna/houserules/#the-campaign-for-north-africa>, CC-BY-SA 4.0 (tagged CHANGE). See `ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## Effect on play

Part of the Italian air force starts in Sicily on strategic duty, so fewer
planes face the Commonwealth in Africa at the start.

## Why it is recorded as a variant

60.32 forbids the basing outright; moving squadrons is a change.
