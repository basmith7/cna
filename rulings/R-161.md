# R-161 — Whether a player's set-up fortifications are optional

## Problem

In The Last Chance, 63.75 lets the Axis player add three level 1
fortifications among the four land hexes next to Bardia, worded as a
permission. The typed row gave a bare count of three, so an engine required
all three before set-up could end. Rommel's Arrival (61.6) words its four
Commonwealth fortifications near Tobruk the same way, and its row had the
same bare count. A row needs to say whether its count is a target or a
ceiling.

## Options

1. **Exactly the count.** The player must place every level the row gives.
2. **Up to the count.** The player places any number from none to the
   count, in the row's area.
3. **All or none.** The player places every level or skips the row.

## Decision

Option 2. Where the printed text says the player *may* place them, the row
is a maximum: Bardia's three (one level per hex) and Rommel's Arrival's
four near Tobruk may be placed in any number from none up, and the row
carries `up_to: true`.

## Rationale

Both cases grant the placement as a choice and give "a total" as the
amount, which reads as a limit, not a quota; the rows that fill every hex
next to a place (R-158) are worded as facts and stay exact. Fewer
fortifications only weaken the owner, so the choice opens no exploit. It
matches the minefield sets, whose `up_to` already marks the optional
fields of the same scenario, and an engine needs only to stop counting a
row as unfinished. Option 3 is a reading the text does not suggest.

Decided under Brian's delegation of 2026-09-26; open to dispute and
supersession like any ruling (see [rulings/README.md](README.md)).
