# R-127 — Abstract convoy attacks: what a 5 % result destroys

## Problem

The Axis naval convoy rows of the Air Bombardment Table (41.51) give
results of 0, 5, 10, 20, 25, 30, 40 and 50 %, and 5 % is common: it is a
possible result in every column from 21–40 to 261–320 bomb points. 32.65
says what a 10 % result destroys (motorisation and replacement points only)
and what 20 % or more destroys (everything, with a minimum of one), but
never mentions 5 %. 47.65, the Air Game's version, has the same gap, and so
does 58.1 under [R-093](R-093.md). cna-engine asked whether a 5 % result,
like 10 %, hits only motorisation and replacement points.

## Options

1. **As 10 %.** A 5 % result destroys 5 % of the motorisation points
   (fractions down) and of the replacement points (fractions up), and
   nothing else.
2. **No effect.** Only the results the case names do anything.
3. **As 20 %.** Every item loses 5 %, with the minimum of one of each.

## Decision

Option 1. Every result below 20 % follows the 10 % rule: that share of the
motorisation points (trucks in the Logistics Game), fractions down, and of
the replacement points, fractions up, and nothing else.

## Rationale

32.65 draws its line at 20 %: "20 % or more" destroys every item, so a
smaller result is the light kind, and the only light rule it gives is the
10 % one. Applying it to 5 % keeps the table's ordering intact: 5 % never
does more than 10 %, which option 3 would break by taking a supply unit on
5 % but not on 10 %. Option 2 would throw away a printed result that
appears in seven columns. With fractions down, 5 % of fewer than twenty
motorisation points destroys none, while the replacement points, rounded
up, always lose at least one when any are carried, so the result is mild
but real. The same reading carries over unchanged to the Air Game's 47.65
table and to the Logistics Game's item-for-item bands (R-093). For an
engine: treat any percentage under 20 as the 10 % case.
