# V-013 — Air-to-air combat sequence

## What the printed rules say

Air combat follows the printed engagement and firing procedure of §45.

## The change

NJHarman's house rule:[^nj]

> Only fighters on OCAP may initiate air combat and they must.
> In each air combat hex:
>
> All OCAP conduct air combat with all enemy OCAP/DCAP in hex, if any
>
> Surviving OCAP in excess of surviving enemy CAP may (must if there was no
> enemy CAP) conduct air combat with any aircraft including fighters on
> non-CAP missions in hex,
>
> A fighter may jettison its bombs or drop tanks at any time during air combat.
> Potentially providing better stats and an unparenthesized Tac Air.
> Fighter vs Fighter:
>
> At most four fighters may gang up on one defender
>
> Shots are sequential; first better pilots, then the outnumbered fighter, then better maneuver, then better Tac Air differential
>
> Fighter vs Non-Fighter:
>
> At most four fighters may gang up on one defender
>
> Non-Fighter fires first at each attacking fighter

[^nj]: NJHarman, *CfNA House Rules & Interpretations*, § Air War, Air to Air,
  <https://friendorfoe.com/war/cfna/houserules/#air-to-air>, CC-BY-SA 4.0 (tagged CHANGE ADDITION, for the whole Air War section). See `ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## Effect on play

Only OCAP starts air combat, against enemy CAP first and then any aircraft; at most four fighters attack one defender, firing order is set by pilot quality and other ratings, and fighters may jettison bombs or drop tanks mid-combat.

## Why it is recorded as a variant

It rewrites who fights whom and in what order.
