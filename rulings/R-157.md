# R-157 — The transport that leaves with a withdrawal

## Problem

Every Commonwealth withdrawal prints "Tpt: a/b". The schedule's key says a
withdrawn unit must be accompanied by truck value points, or motorisation
points if the Logistics Game is abstracted, at least equal to the figure,
counting a light truck point ½, a medium 1 and a heavy 2; so a is the
truck value and b the motorisation points. It does not say what happens when
the Commonwealth player cannot find them, and a return printed "Tpt: See
2/44" brings back "the same distribution of trucks as it left".

## Options

1. **Part of the requirement.** The transport must be in Cairo or
   Alexandria at the check like the unit's strength; short of it, the
   withdrawal fails and the units are eliminated (20.83).
2. **Take what there is.** The unit leaves with whatever trucks or
   motorisation points are on hand, up to the figure.
3. **Ignore the figure** under abstract logistics.

## Decision

Option 1. At the check the Commonwealth player removes truck points worth at
least `transport.truck_value` (Logistics Game) or at least
`transport.motorisation` motorisation points (§32) from Cairo or Alexandria
with the unit; without them the withdrawal fails as if under strength. A
return comes back with exactly what left on its `transport_from`
withdrawals.

## Rationale

The key states the figure as a minimum the unit "must be accompanied by",
the same language as 20.83's strength condition, and 20.83's own reason
(forestalling procrastination) applies as well to trucks as to men: Option 2
would let the player keep his trucks at the front by leaving them behind.
Option 3 drops a figure the chart prints for the abstract game in particular.
The player chooses which truck points go, light, medium or heavy, so long as
their value reaches the figure; under §32 the points come from those in
Cairo or Alexandria, as 32.5x keeps motorisation points with units and hexes.
Returns follow the key word for word: the trucks or motorisation points that
left come back with the unit; returns that print their own truck figures use
those instead.
