# V-005 — Air attack does not reveal dummy dumps

## What the printed rules say

Case 54.12: a dummy supply dump is revealed, and removed, when an enemy unit
enters its hex or when it is bombed or strafed. The restated rule is in the
Logistics Game's trucks and dumps file
(`rules/logistics/40-trucks-and-dumps.md`).

## The change

NJHarman's house rule:[^nj]

> [54.12] Dummy dumps are not revealed by bombing or strafing.
> Revealing by air is so trivial as to make subterfuge pointless.

[^nj]: NJHarman, *CfNA House Rules & Interpretations*, § Logistics,
  <https://friendorfoe.com/war/cfna/houserules/#logistics>, CC-BY-SA 4.0 (tagged CHANGE). See `ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## Effect on play

- Only a ground unit entering the hex unmasks a dummy dump.
- Dummy dumps become a far stronger deception tool against the air game.

## Why it is recorded as a variant

The printed trigger list is explicit; removing one trigger is a change, so
under the [rulings policy](README.md) it is a variant.
