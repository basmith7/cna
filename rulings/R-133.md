# R-133 — Abstract supply: construction costs no supplies

## Problem

Every Construction Chart item (24.17) costs supplies that must be in the
hex at the start of the construction segment (24.13): a real minefield 15
stores and 15 ammunition (24.33), a fortification 30 stores, an airfield 50
fuel and 100 stores, a port block 25 or 50 ammunition, and 10 water extra
per site in hot weather. With the Logistics Game off, case 32.11 says to
ignore every reference to the use and expenditure of ammunition, fuel,
stores and water, yet a §32 supply unit does carry ammunition and fuel.
Does a real minefield, a port block or an airfield draw its ammunition or
fuel from a supply unit in the hex? cna-engine asked, and meanwhile draws
the minefield's 15 ammunition.

## Options

1. **No supply cost.** Under §32 construction needs builders, stages,
   terrain and weather as usual, but no supplies, and no supply unit in
   the hex.
2. **Ammunition and fuel from a supply unit.** The chart's ammunition and
   fuel figures are drawn from a supply unit in the hex; stores and water
   are ignored. A fortification (stores only) is free; a real minefield
   costs 15 of a supply unit's 40 ammunition.
3. **Every figure from a supply unit.** Stores and water are converted to
   ammunition or fuel at some rate.

## Decision

Option 1. With §32 in force, construction spends no ammunition, fuel,
stores or water and needs no supply unit in the hex; everything else about
construction is unchanged.

## Rationale

Case 32.11 is plain: all references to the usage and expenditure of the
four supply types are ignored, and only the §32 supply rules apply. Those
rules spend a supply unit's load in exactly two places, ammunition in
combat and fuel for land movement (32.2x), and construction is neither.
Option 2 picks out the two supply types a supply unit happens to carry and
spends them on a purpose §32 never names; it would also make a real
minefield cost three-eighths of a supply unit, a price no printed rule
sets. Option 3 needs a conversion rate the text never gives. Option 1 is
the printed reading, agrees with [R-131](R-131.md) (24.9's supply dumps,
also a supply rule, are off under §32), and is the simplest for an engine:
under §32 it skips the chart's supply columns. Construction still ties the
builders down for whole stages in which they spend no CP (24.12), so
engineers stay the limit on how many minefields a side lays. The Logistics Game keeps every
chart figure.
