# R-163 — What units hold at the start of a Logistics Game

## Problem

The scenario set-ups list dumps and their contents (59.5) and trucks, which
may start loaded for free (59.4). No case says what a unit holds itself
when play begins. Two cases give a unit somewhere of its own to keep
supplies: 49.14 rates the fuel a vehicle Strength Point holds in its tanks,
and 50.17 lets a combat unit carry the ammunition for a single fire without
trucks. Nothing gives a unit its own store of water or stores; those travel
only in trucks and dumps (51.15, 52.45). An engine has to set every
holder's stock before the first Operations Stage, so it needs a number for
each.

## Options

1. **Tanks full, one fire, one stage's water.** As option 2, plus each unit
   holds the water it needs for one Operations Stage (52.41–52.42).
   Consequence: a grace stage for water, but a unit stock of water that no
   case provides.
2. **Tanks full and one fire; nothing else.** Every fuel-using Strength
   Point starts with its tanks full and every TOE Strength Point with one
   fire's ammunition; units hold no water or stores of their own. Water and
   stores at the start are the scenario's dumps, wells and oases, and what
   the owner loads onto trucks under 59.4. Consequence: units hold only what
   a case lets them hold.
3. **Empty.** Units hold nothing; only dumps and loaded trucks have
   supplies. Consequence: a vehicle unit away from a dump or loaded trucks
   cannot move in the first stage, and a gun cannot fire.
4. **Fully supplied.** As option 1, plus one Game-Turn's stores.
   Consequence: generous, and gives units a stores holding no case provides.

## Decision

Option 2. At set-up, free and on top of the supplies the scenario lists,
every vehicle Strength Point and truck point starts with full tanks and
every TOE Strength Point with one fire's ammunition; no unit holds water or
stores of its own, in every scenario.

The quantities, for an engine:

- **Fuel.** Each fuel-using TOE Strength Point (49.12: vehicles, gun-class
  units, HQs with unbracketed TOE) holds its fuel capacity: its CPA in
  whole blocks of five, times its `fuel_rate` in
  `data/tables/weapon-systems.json` ([R-043](R-043.md)). Each truck point,
  first-, second- or third-line, holds the `fuel_capacity` printed for its
  class in `data/tables/truck-characteristics.json`. This is fuel in tanks,
  not cargo: it does not use the truck's supply capacity.
- **Ammunition.** Each TOE Strength Point of a land unit, HQs included,
  holds the largest *Logistics Game Played* rate in
  `data/tables/ammunition-consumption.json` among the actions it can
  perform: 4 if it has a barrage rating, else 3 if it has an anti-armour
  rating, else 2 if it has an anti-air rating or belongs to a class that
  pays for close assault; 0 otherwise. A point never holds more than this
  without trucks.
- **Water and stores.** None. Trucks may still be loaded with either for
  free at set-up (59.4).
- Supply units and motorisation points are not in the Logistics Game, so
  they hold nothing; planes start fuelled and armed under 59.32 already.

## Rationale

The printed text gives a unit two places of its own to hold supplies, its
tanks and its one fire, and no third. Filling those two at the start is the
reading the rest of the set-up rules point to: 59.32 starts every plane
fuelled and armed at no cost to the listed supplies, and 60.31 calls the
Italian armoured cars that may start as replacement points "fuelled" as a
matter of course, so a regular unit's tanks are full. Option 3 would leave
nearly every vehicle unit in the game unable to move in the first stage
unless it happened to start on a dump, which no scenario's notes expect.

Water and stores differ: no case gives a unit a holding of either, so
options 1 and 4 would invent one. Neither is needed. Every scenario starts
at an Operations Stage, so the first stores distribution is the next
Game-Turn's stage IV, by which time the owner has had a stage to move
stores forward. For water, the set-ups place truckless groups in
named towns, forts, oases and boxes, most of them wells, or leave the hex
to the owner (in Graziani's Offensive every truckless group is one or the
other), and groups
with first-line trucks may start them loaded with water at no cost under
59.4, which is the designers' own tool for the opening supply. Option 2
asks an engine for no new holding: a unit's starting water and stores are
zero, and its fuel and ammunition come from tables it already reads.
No scenario prints anything that overrides this, so it applies in all.
