# R-123 — Abstract supply: motorisation points per unit

## Problem

32.54 lets any foot infantry be motorised by motorisation points, and
32.53 lets them carry any gun printed with CPA 0+, but §32 never says how
many points one unit takes. 32.51 makes a motorisation point a medium truck
point in every respect §32 does not change, so the count comes from the
truck rules: 8.91 asks for enough vehicles to carry the whole unit, 8.95
counts the load in TOE strength points, and the Truck Characteristics Chart
(54.2) gives one medium point one infantry, one artillery or two anti-air
TOE points. Two things are left open. 6.17 says an anti-air unit printed
0+ takes one medium or heavy point per strength point, where the chart's
anti-air column would let one medium point carry two. And neither rule says
whether a unit with too few points moves at the truck CPA, its own, or
not at all. cna-engine needs a count per unit to motorise foot infantry.

## Options

1. **One point per TOE strength point, all or nothing.** Every TOE
   strength point of the unit (infantry, artillery, anti-tank or anti-air)
   takes one motorisation point. With fewer points than that the unit is
   not motorised and moves at its own CPA.
2. **The chart's columns, anti-air at two per point.** As option 1, but an
   anti-air TOE point takes half a motorisation point, a unit's total
   rounded up.
3. **Partial motorisation.** As option 1, but the points ride with
   whatever share of the unit they cover, and the unit moves at its own
   CPA.

## Decision

Option 1. A unit needs one motorisation point for every TOE strength
point it has, of any kind; with fewer it is not motorised.

## Rationale

The printed rules agree on one point per point for every load that
matters to a unit. The chart puts one medium point to one infantry and one
artillery TOE point; 8.95's example (four light, two medium and one heavy
point fully motorise a six-point battalion: two, two and two) checks that
reading; and 6.17 says the same for anti-air guns in so many words. 6.17
is the rule written for motorising a 0+ anti-air unit, so it wins over the
chart's anti-air column, which we read as the lift for anti-air points
carried as a load (replacements), not as a unit being motorised. Anti-tank
guns are guns (32.53) and take the artillery rate. Points absorbed into an
infantry battalion (19.9x) are TOE strength points of that battalion and
count too, which is why they move at the infantry's CPA.

8.91 defines a motorised unit as one that can carry all of itself at once,
so a unit short of points is not motorised (against option 3); the points
it does have may still be assigned and simply wait. Motorisation points are
only medium trucks, so no mixing of truck classes arises. For an engine:
points needed = the sum of the unit's TOE strength points; at or above it
the unit uses the medium-truck CPA (20 for infantry, 15 for guns, from
`data/tables/truck-characteristics.json`), below it its own. 32.54's order
(historically motorised infantry first) is unchanged.
