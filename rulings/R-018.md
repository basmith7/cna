# R-018 — The CP cost of building a supply dump: 24.9 text or the Construction Chart?

## Problem

Case 24.9 says any single TOE point builds a supply dump (or a dummy dump)
for **3 CP**, plus 20 stores in the Logistics Game. The Construction Chart
(24.17, on the chart sheet) also lists supply dumps, and according to
NJHarman the number printed there differs from the one in the rules
text.[^nj-words]

> Dump construction cost is listed in [24.9 Chart] not number noted in
> [24.17] summary.

[^nj-words]: NJHarman, *CfNA House Rules & Interpretations*, § More Words, Is
  Bad Words, <https://friendorfoe.com/war/cfna/houserules/#more-words-is-bad-words>,
  CC-BY-SA 4.0 (tagged CORRECTION). See `ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## Options

1. **The rules text (24.9) governs** (NJHarman). Dumps cost 3 CP; the chart
   entry is treated as a summary that mis-states the specific rule.
   Consequence: the restated rule stands as written; the chart transcription
   in `data/tables/construction.json` will carry an errata-style overlay or
   a note pointing here.
2. **The chart governs.** The chart sheet is the reference players use at
   the table and, in the CP cost table of 6.3, other costs are given by
   chart alone. Consequence: the 24.9 figure is the misprint and the
   restated rule changes once the chart is captured.

## Decision

None yet — proposed.

## Rationale

Option 1, provisionally. 24.9 is the case that defines the action and gives
its cost in a full sentence; the Construction Chart is a summary of costs
set out elsewhere in §24, and where a summary and its source disagree the
source is the better evidence of intent. The September 1979 errata does not
touch either figure. The ruling cannot be settled until the chart is
captured (Part C of the current mission) and the two numbers are actually
compared; the restated rule keeps 3 CP ([Engineering — Supply
dumps](../rules/80-engineering.md#supply-dumps)).
