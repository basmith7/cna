# R-018 — The CP cost of building a supply dump: 24.9 text or the Construction Chart?

## Problem

Case 24.9 says any single TOE point builds a supply dump for **3 CP** plus
**20 stores** (Logistics Game), and a dummy dump for **3 CP**. The
Construction Chart (24.17, chart sheet jp2 103, read after this ruling was
opened) gives a real dump as **3 CP and 10 stores** and a fake dump as
**2 CP** — and the Capability Point Expenditure Summary (6.3,
`data/tables/cp-costs.json`) agrees with the chart's CP figures ("3/2").
So the rules text and the charts differ twice: the stores cost of a real
dump (20 vs 10) and the CP cost of a dummy (3 vs 2). NJHarman:[^nj-words]

> Dump construction cost is listed in [24.9 Chart] not number noted in
> [24.17] summary.

[^nj-words]: NJHarman, *CfNA House Rules & Interpretations*, § More Words, Is
  Bad Words, <https://friendorfoe.com/war/cfna/houserules/#more-words-is-bad-words>,
  CC-BY-SA 4.0 (tagged CORRECTION). See `ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## Options

1. **The rules text (24.9) governs** (NJHarman's reading). Real dump 3 CP
   + 20 stores, dummy 3 CP. Consequence: the restated rule stands; both
   charts get a note pointing here, and 6.3's "3/2" is read as a misprint.
2. **The charts govern.** Real dump 3 CP + 10 stores, dummy 2 CP.
   Consequence: two independent charts agree with each other against one
   sentence of rules text; the restated rule changes for the dummy cost
   (and the stores figure, which only matters in the Logistics Game).
3. **Split the difference by kind.** CP from the charts (they agree with
   each other and are what is read at the table), stores from 24.9 (the
   only place the Logistics Game cost is explained). Consequence: dummy 2
   CP, real 3 CP + 20 stores.

## Decision

None yet — proposed.

## Rationale

With both charts now read, the CP question is not one sentence against one
chart but one sentence against two charts that agree (6.3 and 24.17): the
2 CP dummy cost is the better-attested figure and option 2 or 3 is the
stronger reading for CP. For stores only 24.9 and 24.17 speak, and they
disagree with nothing to break the tie; a stores cost affects only the
Logistics Game and can wait for that spec. The September 1979 errata
touches neither figure. The restated rule presently follows 24.9 (3 CP for
either kind — [Engineering — Supply
dumps](../rules/80-engineering.md#supply-dumps)) and the CP cost table
follows the chart (`construct-supply-dump`: 3, alt 2); one of the two must
change when this is decided.
