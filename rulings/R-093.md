# R-093 — The abstract convoy attack's result bands against itemised cargo

## Problem

Case 58.1 has Axis convoys attacked by the Land Game's simplified procedure
(32.6) when the Air Game is not played, the only difference being that the
convoy now carries itemised cargo. The 32.6 results are written for the
abstract cargo: a 10 % result touches only motorisation points (fractions
down) and replacement points (fractions up), while 20 % or more touches
everything, fractions down, with at least one point of each item and one
supply unit lost. A Logistics Game convoy carries fuel, ammunition, stores,
water, trucks and replacement points instead, and 58.1 does not say which
of those each band reaches.

## Options

1. **Map item for item.** Trucks stand where motorisation points stood,
   and the four supply types where supply units stood. A 10 % result hits
   trucks (fractions down) and replacement points (fractions up); 20 % or
   more hits every item, fractions down, with at least one point lost of
   each item carried. Consequence: the 32.6 bands keep their meaning; a
   light attack spares the supplies, as it spared supply units.
2. **Every band hits every item.** Consequence: simpler, but a 10 % result
   would then destroy supplies that 32.6 protects from it, which 58.1's
   "same procedure" does not allow.

## Decision

Option 1. Trucks take the place of motorisation points and the four supply types the place of supply units, so each 32.6 result band hits what it hit before.

## Rationale

Case 58.1 keeps the Land Game's procedure and changes only how the cargo is
described, so the bands must keep their meaning: a light (10 %) attack
touches transport and replacements, a heavy one touches everything. Trucks
are what motorisation points abstract, and supply units abstract the
supplies themselves, so the mapping is one to one. Option 2 would let a
light attack destroy supplies that the printed procedure shields from it.
The "at least one supply unit" floor becomes at least one point of each
supply type carried, the nearest equivalent that stays in whole points.
Opened and decided in the same PR: the question was left as an unnumbered
note when §58 was restated.
