# R-002 — Road stubs and other map-art inconsistencies

## Problem

The printed maps carry small drafting artefacts: a road or track that pokes
a few millimetres past a hexside into the next hex without running through
it, and similar slivers of other features. The rules (4.1, 8.31, 8.37) define
terrain per hex and per hexside and say nothing about how much of a feature
must be present for the hex to count as having it. NJHarman flags the
question:[^nj-map]

> Various map inconsistencies (e.g. tiny bits of road extending into adjacent
> hex) will be ignored. *Borrowed from OCS, nubs don't count*

[^nj-map]: NJHarman, *CfNA House Rules & Interpretations*, § Map,
  <https://friendorfoe.com/war/cfna/houserules/#map>, CC-BY-SA 4.0
  (tagged INTERPRETATION). See `ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## Options

1. **Stubs do not count** (NJHarman's reading). A hex has a road, track or
   other linear feature only when the feature runs through it to at least
   one other hexside, or terminates in the hex at a marked point (a town,
   fort, airfield or the map edge). Consequence: a redrawn map for an engine
   can drop every stub; movement along a road never gains or loses a hex to
   drafting.
2. **Any printed art counts.** If any part of a road symbol appears in a hex,
   the hex is a road hex for the road rate, provided the connecting hexside
   is a road hexside. Consequence: matches the paper literally; a handful of
   hexes gain a road rate the designers plainly did not intend, and the
   answer differs between printings and scans.

## Decision

Option 1. A drafting stub that does not carry a road or track across the hex to another hexside, or to a marked end point, is ignored.

## Rationale

Case 8.31's road rate applies to movement *along* a road between adjacent
road hexes joined by a road hexside (restated in
[Movement — Roads and tracks](../rules/40-movement.md)); a stub that stops
short of the far side of the hex joins nothing and so cannot be moved along.
Option 1 is the only reading under which the road rule has a determinate
answer independent of the printing, which the map sub-project will need when
it redraws the hex grid. Nothing in the printed text supports option 2 beyond
the literal ink.

Decided under Brian's delegation of 2026-09-26; open to dispute as described in
[rulings/README.md](README.md).
