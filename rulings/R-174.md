# R-174 — Refit and repair costs with the Air Game and abstract logistics

## Problem

In the full Air Game every squadron that tries to refit spends one stores
point, which must be present (38.36), and each aircraft repaired after
strafing (45.62) or after limping home (45.64) costs 1 stores and 1 fuel.
With the Logistics Game off, 47.0 keeps §32, whose supply units hold only
ammunition and fuel and whose 32.11 sets aside every rule on spending
supplies except §32's own. 47.2 replaces the costs of rearming and
refuelling and says nothing of refitting or repairing. cna-engine asked
what each of these costs, and what stands in for "present", under §47.

## Options

1. **Free, but only at a supplied SGSU.** Refits and repairs spend
   nothing. Where 38.36 needs stores present, and 35.14 needs the SGSU
   supplied to repair, the SGSU must be able to draw supplies under
   47.11–47.13 ([R-173](R-173.md)). Rolls, limits and delays are as
   printed. Consequence: no new charge; one supply test, the same as for
   refuelling.
2. **Stores dropped, repair fuel kept.** Refits are free; each repaired
   aircraft draws 1 fuel from a supply unit within 15 CP (none at an
   unlimited source). Consequence: repairing one fighter costs as much
   fuel as refuelling its whole squadron (47.23).
3. **Stores converted.** Each stores point becomes a point of fuel (or
   ammunition) from a supply unit. Consequence: a conversion rate no case
   gives.

## Decision

Option 1. With the Air Game and abstract logistics, a refit roll and a
repair spend no stores and no fuel; an SGSU may refit or repair only while
it can draw supplies under 47.11–47.13, and everything else in 38.3x and
45.6x applies as printed.

## Rationale

This is the reading the printed text gives. 32.11 sets aside every
expenditure of ammunition, fuel, stores and water that §32 does not make
itself, 47.0 keeps that, and §47's own costs (47.2) are flak ammunition,
rearming and refuelling. A refit's stores point and a repair's stores and
fuel appear in neither, so they are ignored, as construction's are under
[R-133](R-133.md). Option 2 keeps the repair fuel only because a supply
unit happens to carry fuel, the reasoning R-133 turned down, and at a
scale §47 rejects: 47.23 refuels a whole fighter squadron for one fuel
point, so one point per repaired aircraft would make a repair dearer than
a sortie. Option 3 needs a rate no case supplies.

The printed costs also carry a condition: stores must be at hand to refit
(38.36), and an unsupplied SGSU cannot repair (35.14). §47 says how an
SGSU is supplied (47.11–47.13), so option 1 keeps the condition in that
form, matching [R-173](R-173.md). The repair roll (45.62), the free repair
of a limped-home fighter (45.64), the full Game-Turn before a repaired
aircraft flies, and the refit roll and its additions are untouched.
