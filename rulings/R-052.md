# R-052 — Stores attrition: timing, base and small units

## Problem

Case 51.22 takes 2 % of TOE Strength Points from units in a hex for every
two consecutive Game-Turns without stores, rising to 4 % at the end of the
fourth turn, 6 % at the sixth and so on, rounded to the nearest whole
number, from infantry-type points only. It does not say:

- whether a loss is taken only at the end of each even-numbered turn of the
  run, or every turn once the run reaches two;
- whether the percentage is of the unit's infantry-type points or of all
  its TOE Strength Points (when only infantry-type points can be removed);
- whether it is figured per unit or for the hex as a whole;
- whether a unit of fewer than 25 points, where 2 % rounds to 0, loses
  anything.

## Options

1. **Even turns, per unit, infantry base, nearest rounding.** At the end of
   turns 2, 4, 6 … of a run, each unit loses the current percentage of its
   infantry-type points, rounded to nearest; a result of 0 means no loss.
   Consequence: simple; small detachments can starve a long time without
   attrition.
2. **Even turns, per hex, minimum 1.** Total the infantry-type points of all
   unfed units in the hex, take the percentage, round to nearest but never
   below 1, and let the owner choose which units lose them. Consequence:
   small units always suffer; the owner picks who pays.
3. **Every turn from the second.** As option 1, but the current rate is
   applied every turn once the run reaches two. Consequence: much harsher;
   losses mount quickly.

## Decision

Option 1. At the end of the second, fourth, sixth and later even turns of an unfed run, each unit loses the current percentage of its infantry-type points, rounded to nearest, and a result of 0 means no loss.

## Rationale

Case 51.22 charges the loss "for every two" unfed turns in a row and names
the steps at the fourth and sixth turn, which reads as a loss at the end of
each even-numbered turn of the run, not every turn (option 3 would roughly
double the printed attrition). Since only infantry-type points can be
removed, the percentage is taken of those points, per unit, as each unit
keeps its own record of TOE Strength Points. The case says to round to the
nearest whole number, and we follow it even where that gives 0; option 2's
minimum of 1 and pooled hex totals are not in the text and add an owner
choice an engine would have to prompt for. That small detachments starve
slowly is a direct result of the printed rounding. See [Ammunition and
stores — Going without stores](../rules/logistics/20-ammunition-and-stores.md).
