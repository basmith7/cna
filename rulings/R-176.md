# R-176 — Which O/A notes are historical shifts, and in which month

## Problem

19.24 lets the owner make a historical change of parent that an O/A Chart
note describes, within 19.21, but only in the month the note gives; a
shift not made then is never made. The notes do not fit that wording neatly. A few print a month (the
22nd Guards Brigade with the 70th Infantry Division from February to April
1941; the 161st Indian Motor Brigade coming under the 5th Indian Division in
August). Others tie the change to an event the sheets date without naming a
month (the 3/1 Punjab joining the 25th Indian Brigade when the 25th
arrives; the Littorio's V and VI Articelere Battalions going to the Ariete
as soon as they land; the 5th Indian Brigade replacing the 20th, destroyed
in June 1942). The rest say only "later", "eventually" or "at various
times" (the 1st RTR to the 22nd Armoured Brigade, the 22nd Armoured Brigade
to the 7th Armoured Division, the 90th Light's III/255 and III/347 to the
200th Regiment). 19.24's own example, the 104th Regiment's move to the 21st
Panzer, rests on notes that date it only by the 5th Light's reorganisation.

## Options

1. **Printed month only.** A shift may be made only in a month the note
   prints. Shifts tied to a dated event, and undated ones, may never be
   made.
2. **Printed or dated month.** A shift may be made in the month the note
   prints, or, where it ties the change to an event the O/A sheets date (an
   arrival, a reorganisation, a destruction), in that event's month. A note
   that gives neither names no month, so its shift may not be made under
   19.24.
3. **Any month.** Every shift a note names may be made at any time; a
   printed month is history, not a limit.

## Decision

Option 2. Each shift is typed on the moving unit in `data/oa/` as
`shifts` (the parent it leaves, the parent it joins, the month); an undated
shift is listed with `month: null` and may not be made under 19.24.

## Rationale

19.24 makes the month the condition, so option 3 drops
half the rule. Option 1 keeps the words but not the example: the case's
own illustration is a shift its notes date only by an event, and a note
that ties a move to the 25th Brigade's arrival pins one month as firmly as one
that names it. Option 2 follows the example and still refuses a month the
sheets do not give. Three points follow from 19.24 and 19.21 without
needing options of their own. A shift to no parent (the 22nd Guards leaving the 70th Division in April 1941)
is made the same way and leaves the unit independent. A shift is made
within 19.21, so the unit must be attached to the parent it joins and a
slot must be free. A unit already independent may be assigned under 19.21
whenever those conditions hold, dated or not, so its shift only records
history. The compulsory changes stay out of `shifts`: the 21st Panzer's
replacement of the 5th Light, with the 104th and 155th Regiments, is
`replaces`/`assigns` under the replacement-counter rule.
