# R-070 — What the Equivalent Weights Chart's "Unit of Stacking Points 1/2" row means

## Problem

The stacking-point half of the Equivalent Weights Chart (54.5, chart sheet
jp2 109) lists what each item counts as when moved by rail or interport
transfer: a truck point 1/10, a replacement point 1/5, a squadron ground
support unit 1/2, and a last row, "Unit of Stacking Points", also 1/2. Both
scans print the same. No case explains the last row: it may halve the
stacking points of every unit moved this way, or it may be a misprint for 1
(a unit counts its own stacking points), or the row may belong to the SGSU
line above.

## Options

1. **Units count half their stacking points by rail or interport.**
   Consequence: a 2-SP rail stack (8.72) could carry 4 SP of printed units;
   rail and interport lift double what the Land Game allows, a large change.
2. **Read the row as 1: a unit counts its printed stacking points.**
   Consequence: rail and interport limits match the Land Game; the chart's
   1/2 is treated as a misprint repeated from the SGSU row.
3. **The row qualifies the SGSU row (an SGSU counts 1/2 whatever its
   printed value) and adds nothing for other units.** Consequence: same play
   effect as option 2 for ordinary units.
