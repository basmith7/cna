# R-099 — Tobruk's repair facility

## Problem

The repair rules (22.31) list Tobruk among the major repair facilities; the
Group One set-up gives the Italians only a temporary one there. NJHarman:[^nj]

> INTERPRETATION  [22.31] says Tobruk is Major Repair Facility. But, [60.33]
> says it’s temporary. I rule it’s temporary. Only major facilities in game are
> Tripoli, Alexandria, and Cairo.

[^nj]: NJHarman, *CfNA House Rules & Interpretations*, § Scenario Notes, 64.0 The Campaign For North Africa,
  <https://friendorfoe.com/war/cfna/houserules/#the-campaign-for-north-africa>, CC-BY-SA 4.0 (tagged INTERPRETATION). See `ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## Options

1. **Temporary.** Tobruk is a temporary facility; the only major ones are
   Tripoli, Alexandria and Cairo.
2. **Major.** 22.31 governs everywhere; the scenario's "temporary" is a slip.
3. **Each scenario says.** A scenario's set-up names the repair facilities
   that exist when it starts, as 22.31 itself provides for temporary ones,
   and that list governs that scenario; 22.31's list of major facilities
   holds wherever a scenario does not say otherwise.

## Decision

Option 3. In Graziani's Offensive and the Italian Campaign Tobruk is a temporary facility, as 60.33 says; 22.31's list of major facilities stands wherever a scenario's set-up does not say otherwise.

## Rationale

The scenarios are not careless about this: they set Tobruk differently on
purpose. 60.33 makes it temporary for the Italians in 1940, 62's Crusader
set-up gives the Commonwealth a temporary one there and the Axis a major one
at Benghazi, and 63 gives the Axis a major facility at Tobruk in 1942. Read
together they describe facilities growing and changing hands, which a single
answer for all games (options 1 and 2) would flatten. 22.31 itself says the
facilities in existence at a scenario's start are designated by the
scenario. Treating each set-up as governing its own scenario is the same
principle R-096 applies to arrivals: the specific set-up beats the general
chart. The Land Game's repair rule is unchanged; only the scenario files say
what exists at the start.
