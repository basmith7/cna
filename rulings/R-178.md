# R-178 — Abstract supply: dummy tank battalions cost no stores

## Problem

A dummy tank battalion (16.4) is brought into play in the construction
segment by spending 10 stores that must be in its hex (16.42). With the
Logistics Game off, case 32.11 says to ignore every reference to the use and
expenditure of ammunition, fuel, stores and water, and §32's supply units
carry only ammunition and fuel. What does a dummy cost under §32, and where
is it paid from? cna-engine asked before building 16.4.

## Options

1. **No supply cost.** Under §32 a dummy is placed in the construction
   segment as usual, with no stores and no supply unit in the hex; the
   limits of 16.43 (three per player at once, one per hex, none in an
   Italian formation) and everything else in 16.4 stand.
2. **Paid from a supply unit.** The 10 stores become 10 ammunition or fuel
   from a supply unit in the hex.
3. **Left out.** Dummy tank battalions exist only in the Logistics Game.

## Decision

Option 1. With §32 in force a dummy tank battalion costs nothing and needs
no supply unit in its hex; it is still placed only in the construction
segment and every other part of 16.4 applies.

## Rationale

The 10 stores are a reference to the expenditure of stores, which 32.11
says to ignore; §32 spends a supply unit's load only on combat ammunition
and land-movement fuel (32.2x), and placing a dummy is neither. Nothing in
§32 turns 16.4 off, so option 3 would drop a printed rule on no authority,
and option 2 needs a conversion from stores the text never gives. Option 1
is the printed reading and matches [R-133](R-133.md), which reads the
Construction Chart's supply costs the same way, and [R-131](R-131.md),
under which §32 builds no supply dumps. It is also the simplest for an
engine: under §32 it skips the stores check. The cap of three per player
(16.43) keeps free dummies from flooding the map, so no exploit follows.
The Logistics Game keeps the 10 stores.
