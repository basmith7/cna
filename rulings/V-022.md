# V-022 — Unlimited flak ammunition in the Tripoli and Tunisia boxes

## What the printed rules say

Inherent flak in the Tripoli and Tunisia boxes uses ammunition like other flak.

## The change

NJHarman's house rule:[^nj]

> ADDITION  Tripoli/Tunisia boxes have unlimited ammo to fire inherent Flak
> points. In addition to, as per rules; unlimited fuel/ammo/stores for aircraft
> maintenance and unlimited water for any purpose.

[^nj]: NJHarman, *CfNA House Rules & Interpretations*, § Logistics,
  <https://friendorfoe.com/war/cfna/houserules/#logistics>, CC-BY-SA 4.0 (tagged ADDITION). See `ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## Effect on play

The boxes' inherent flak never runs short of ammunition.

## Why it is recorded as a variant

It adds a free supply source.
