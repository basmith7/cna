# R-140 — Rommel when an assault destroys his stack

## Problem

[R-138](R-138.md) says a Rommel stacked with combat units is never captured
under 10.29, and that "if his stack is destroyed or retreats he may retreat
with it". A stack that retreats takes him along. But when a close assault
destroys every combat unit in his hex there is no retreat to join, and
R-138 gives neither a distance nor a moment. Left where he is, he stands
alone next to the assaulting units. cna-engine asked.

## Options

1. **Captured then and there.** He chose to stay instead of retreating
   before assault; once his stack is gone he is alone in an enemy ZOC and
   10.29 takes him as soon as the losses are applied.
2. **He retreats at once, as a forced retreat.** Right after the losses
   that destroy his stack, before the next assault is resolved, he makes a
   forced retreat on his own, through enemy ZOC with impunity. If it does
   not end in a safe hex, he is captured.
3. **He stays put.** Nothing happens until the next 10.29 check, at the
   start of the next enemy movement/combat phase.

## Decision

Option 2. When an assault's losses destroy every combat unit in Rommel's
hex, he at once makes a forced retreat of his own, through enemy ZOC with
impunity, and is captured only if it cannot end in a safe hex.

## Rationale

R-138 is accepted and already promises him a retreat when his stack is
destroyed; option 1 would take that back, and option 3 leaves a piece with
no strength standing next to the enemy for the rest of the phase, which
10.29 forbids ("at any time" during the enemy phase). Option 2 is what his
stack would have done had any of it survived a retreat result, and 31.0
gives him free passage through enemy ZOC on any retreat. It costs the
attacker little: the hex is still taken and the Axis lose the stack. The
choice to stay and lend the stack his morale bonus (31.0, point 3) keeps a
real price, since a retreat that cannot reach safety ends in capture.

What counts, for an engine:

- **When.** Immediately after the defender's losses from that assault are
  taken, before the attacker's next assault is resolved and before any
  advance into the hex.
- **How far.** Follow the forced-retreat path (toward the nearest friendly
  supply dump or city by the easiest path, paying CP as for any move) for
  as many hexes as the assault's retreat result called for, at least one.
  He then keeps going, along the same path, until he stands in a hex with a
  friendly combat unit or outside every enemy ZOC. A major city does not
  stop him early unless it is safe in that sense.
- **ZOC.** He pays no break-off cost and may pass from one enemy-controlled
  hex to the next. He may not enter a hex holding an enemy unit.
- **Capture.** If no path reaches a safe hex, or he runs out of CP before
  reaching one, he is captured where he stops: removed for good, with no
  prisoner points, as in R-138. He has no TOE points, so the 10 %-per-hex
  loss for hexes not retreated never applies to him.
- **Other cases.** A stack that survives and retreats takes him with it
  (R-138). A stack that survives and stays leaves him stacked and safe.
