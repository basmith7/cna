# R-026 — Where is the Commonwealth's unlimited supply: Cairo, or Cairo and Alexandria?

## Problem

Case 57.0 gives the Commonwealth unlimited supplies of every type in Cairo.
The Italian campaign's set-up (60.44) says the unlimited supply is in Cairo
and Alexandria. An engine needs one list of source hexes. NJHarman:[^nj-unl]

> [60.44] says “Unlimited Supplies in Cairo/Alexandria”. But,
> [57.0] says only Cairo.

[^nj-unl]: NJHarman, *CfNA House Rules & Interpretations*, § Unlimited
  Supplies, <https://friendorfoe.com/war/cfna/houserules/#unlimited-supplies>, CC-BY-SA 4.0 (tagged
  CLARIFICATION). See `ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## Options

1. **Cairo only** (57.0; NJHarman's leaning). He adds: [^nj-unl]

   > I rule only Cairo. Delta Railroad exists for reason!

   Consequence: supplies must be carried from Cairo to Alexandria by rail or
   truck, so the Delta railway and its capacity matter.
2. **Cairo and Alexandria** (60.44). Consequence: the Commonwealth's supply
   source sits on the coast, nearer the front; the Delta
   railway carries almost nothing.
3. **By scenario.** Cairo and Alexandria in the Italian campaign (60.44 is
   that scenario's own set-up); Cairo only elsewhere. Consequence: the
   scenario text is a deliberate exception rather than an error.
