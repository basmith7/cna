# R-017 — Can a Tank Delivery Squadron react, retreat before assault, or retreat with a stack?

## Problem

22.63 says a TDS may never enter an enemy ZOC and is eliminated if an enemy
combat unit puts it in one while it is alone. The reaction list in 8.53a
names truck convoys but not TDS; the retreat-before-assault rules (13.2x)
speak of non-phasing *units*; and a CRT retreat (15.82) moves everything in
the hex. Whether a TDS stacked with a combat unit may move with it under any
of these, and whether one alone may, is not addressed. NJHarman:[^nj-tds]

> Must be stacked with a combat unit to use Reaction, Retreat Before Assault
> or perform a CRT mandated Retreat. *[22.63] "If alone in hex…" implies not
> restricted when not alone.*

[^nj-tds]: NJHarman, *CfNA House Rules & Interpretations*, § Tank Delivery
  Squadrons, <https://friendorfoe.com/war/cfna/houserules/#tank-delivery-squadrons>,
  CC-BY-SA 4.0 (tagged INTERPRETATION). The quoted item is the second
  INTERPRETATION under that heading. See `ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## Options

1. **Only when stacked with a friendly combat unit** (NJHarman). A TDS in a
   stack with a combat unit may react, retreat before assault and retreat
   with the stack; alone it may do none of these, and 22.63 eliminates it if
   an enemy moves adjacent. Consequence: the same treatment 8.53a gives
   truck convoys, extended to the two retreats.
2. **Never.** A TDS is not a combat unit and 22.63 gives it no escape
   clause; alone or escorted it stays put and is eliminated (or, in a stack,
   shares whatever befalls the stack). Consequence: harsh, and a CRT
   retreat would leave it behind in a hex the enemy is entering.
3. **Always, as a vehicle with a CPA.** It may react and retreat on its
   own, subject to the ZOC ban. Consequence: 22.63's "if alone" clause
   would rarely bite, since a lone TDS could react away from any
   approaching enemy with a CPA under 31.

## Decision

Option 1. A TDS stacked with a friendly combat unit may react, retreat before assault and retreat with its stack; a TDS alone may do none of these.

## Rationale

Option 1. 22.63's condition "if alone in a hex" only has meaning if a TDS
that is *not* alone has some way of avoiding elimination, which is what the
three moves provide; option 3 would make the clause nearly dead, and
option 2 makes it entirely so. Treating a TDS as 8.53a treats a truck
convoy — mobile only in company — is also the reading closest to the
counter's other properties (0 SP, no ratings, CPA 25 "as vehicles"). See
also R-006 for the reach of 8.53a's stacking condition.
