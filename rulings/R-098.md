# R-098 — Commonwealth major repair facilities in Group One

## Problem

The Group One set-up names Alexandria as the Commonwealth's major repair
facility, while the repair rules (22.31) make every hex of Alexandria and
Cairo one. NJHarman:[^nj]

> INTERPRETATION  [60.44] says “Major Repair Facility in (only) Alexandria”.
> But, [22.31] says all hexes of Alexandria and Cairo are Major. I rule all
> hexes of Cairo and both hexes of Alexandria are Major Repair Facilities.

[^nj]: NJHarman, *CfNA House Rules & Interpretations*, § Scenario Notes, 64.0 The Campaign For North Africa,
  <https://friendorfoe.com/war/cfna/houserules/#the-campaign-for-north-africa>, CC-BY-SA 4.0 (tagged INTERPRETATION). See `ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## Options

1. **22.31 governs.** Every hex of Cairo and both of Alexandria are major
   facilities.
2. **The scenario governs.** Alexandria only, for this scenario group.

## Decision

Option 1. Every hex of Cairo and both hexes of Alexandria are major repair facilities in Group One, as 22.31 makes them everywhere.

## Rationale

22.31 is the general rule and names every hex of both cities. Group Two's
set-up (61.36) says the same in its own words, so Group One's naming of
Alexandria alone reads as shorthand rather than a deliberate exception;
nothing in Group One explains why Cairo should lack a facility it has in
every other set-up. Keeping one rule for every scenario is also simpler
for an engine.

Decided under Brian's delegation of 2026-09-26; open to dispute and
supersession like any ruling (see [rulings/README.md](README.md)).
