# R-094 — Italian artillery listed twice "anywhere in Libya"

## Problem

The Italian set-up for Group One (60.31) places a list of corps and
grouping artillery anywhere in Libya. In the printed line two of the
units, 4/1AR and XXI Corps artillery, appear twice, once in the run of the
list and again at its end. Each is a single counter.

## Options

1. **Each unit once.** The repeats are a typesetting slip; one counter each.
   The data already lists them once.
2. **A missing unit.** One of each pair stands for a different counter
   that dropped out; leave a gap until a printed copy or errata settles it.

## Decision

Option 1. Each of the two units is one counter, placed once; the repeats are a typesetting slip.

## Rationale

The OA sheets print exactly one 4/1 artillery battalion and one XXI Corps
artillery regiment, and no other counter carries either name, so a second
copy has nothing to stand for; option 2 would need a counter the game does
not contain. Placing a unit once is also what every other set-up does, and
it is the simpler reading for an engine. The scenario data already lists
each once.

Decided under Brian's delegation of 2026-09-26; open to dispute and
supersession like any ruling (see [rulings/README.md](README.md)).
