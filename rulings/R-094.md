# R-094 — Italian artillery listed twice "anywhere in Libya"

## Problem

The Italian set-up for Group One (60.31) places a list of corps and
grouping artillery anywhere in Libya. In the printed line two of the
units, 4/1AR and XXI Corps artillery, appear twice, once in the run of the
list and again at its end. Each is a single counter.

## Options

1. **Each unit once.** The repeats are a typesetting slip; one counter each.
   The data already lists them once.
2. **A missing unit.** One of each pair stands for a different counter
   that dropped out; leave a gap until a printed copy or errata settles it.
