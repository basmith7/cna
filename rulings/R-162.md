# R-162 — Whether works placed at set-up obey the construction site rules

## Problem

A fortification may not be started in an enemy zone of control (24.44),
and a minefield may not be laid in an enemy-controlled hex (24.36); both
also have terrain limits (24.35, 24.44). Several scenarios put works on
the map at the start: Rommel's Arrival gives the Commonwealth four level 1
fortifications to place within two hexes of Tobruk (61.6); The Last Chance
fixes fortifications round Tobruk, lets the Axis choose three of the four
hexes next to Bardia (63.75), lists each side's minefield hexes and adds
some the owner places (63.76, 63.77). Only 63.77 says anything about
eligibility: the Axis's optional minefields go in hexes where a minefield
could be laid under the normal rules. Is set-up placement construction, so
that the enemy-ZOC and terrain limits apply to every work placed at the
start, to none, or to some?

## Options

1. **Set-up is never construction.** Every work goes where the set-up
   puts it, whatever the terrain or the enemy units nearby. Simplest, but
   it ignores 63.77's own "eligible" and lets Rommel's Arrival's four
   fortifications sit in Tobruk's major-city hex, which no fortification
   may be built in.
2. **Placed, not built; chosen hexes must be eligible.** No set-up work is
   construction: none costs supplies, needs builders or takes stages. A
   work the scenario puts in named hexes goes there as printed. A work
   whose hex the owner picks must go in a hex where that work could be
   started under §24 (terrain, one minefield per hex, no major city, no
   enemy ZOC for a fortification, no enemy-controlled hex for a
   minefield), judged once both sides' units are placed.
3. **Set-up is construction everywhere.** Every work, printed hexes too,
   must meet §24's site rules; a printed hex that fails loses its work.

## Decision

Option 2. Works a scenario places at the start are already built, at no
cost; those in named hexes stand as printed, and those whose hex the owner
chooses go only where §24 would let that work be started, enemy ZOC
included, checked against the finished set-up.

## Rationale

The scenarios describe works that "are placed" or "have been built", not
projects, so §24's costs, builders and stages have no part in set-up, and
a hex the scenario names is the historical position: the designer chose
it, and option 3 would let a unit placed next to it erase it (The Last
Chance's listed fields sit on the El Alamein front, beside enemy units). Where the
owner chooses, though, 63.77 says outright that eligibility means the
normal construction limits, and the printed text gives no reason for the
other owner-chosen works (61.6's four fortifications, 63.75's three at
Bardia, 63.76's five extra Commonwealth fields) to follow a looser rule.
Reading them all alike keeps a fortification out of Tobruk's own
major-city hex (C4807) and the sea round it in Rommel's Arrival, and stops
either side planting a field or a fort under an enemy unit's nose at the
start. Checking against the finished set-up, rather than whichever side
places first, makes the result independent of placement order. An engine
needs one check, the one it already has for construction, applied to the
owner-chosen rows only. Consistent with R-158, which reads the printed
Tobruk and Bardia rows as fixed hex lists, and with R-133, under which
construction can cost nothing yet still obey §24's site rules.
