# R-114 — Anti-air units with the Air Game and abstract logistics

## Problem

The Air Game played without the Logistics Game uses §32 "except as
modified" by §47. §47 never mentions §32's anti-air modifications (32.8),
which strip most AA units and AA points from both sides. §32's own general
rule says it applies when the Land Game is played without the Air *and*
Logistics Games, and the stripping only makes sense when nothing flies.
Read literally, though, §47 carries 32.8 along with the rest of §32, and
the Air Game's flak (§46) would be fought with almost no AA guns.

## Options

1. **Ignore 32.8 with the Air Game.** §47 borrows §32 for supply only; every
   AA unit and point stays, so flak works as §46 describes.
2. **Apply 32.8 as written.** §47 says "except as modified" and does not
   modify 32.8, so the AA units go even with the Air Game.

## Decision

Option 1. With the Air Game, 32.8 does not apply: every AA unit and AA point stays in play, abstract logistics or not.

## Rationale

§32's general rule (32.0) limits the whole section to the Land Game played
without the Air *and* Logistics Games, and 32.8 exists only because, in
that game, AA guns have nothing to shoot at. §47 borrows §32 for *supply*:
its own headings (supply units, expenditures, motorisation points, convoys,
the fleet, stacking) are all logistics, and it adds the cost of flak
ammunition (47.21), which assumes the gun-class AA units are there to fire.
Option 2 would have the Air Game's flak (§46) fought by the few heavy AA
points the stripping leaves, which no Air Game rule expects. Option 1 also
keeps the switch simple for an engine: 32.8 is on exactly when the Air Game
is off.
