# R-136 — Set-up areas "between" two places and "inside" a triangle

## Problem

Three set-ups place units in an area named only by its edges. Rommel's
Arrival puts the Pavia, Bologna, Brescia and Savona divisions "anywhere
between" Ras el Ali (A2010) and Nofilia (A2703), none less than two hexes
from another (61.41), and the Axis dumps in "any hex between" El Agheila
(A1816) and Nofilia (61.44). It puts the 9th Australian Division's brigades
in "any hex within the triangle" of Tocra (B5501), Er Regima (A4830) and
Benghazi (A4827) (61.31), and Operation Crusader puts the Savona Division
in the triangle C3916, Sidi Omar (C3618), C3922 (62.41). Which hexes are
those? cna-engine asked: its set-up held only the corner hexes, and two
hexes cannot hold four divisions two hexes apart.

## Options

1. **The coast road.** "Between" two coastal towns means the road hexes
   joining them, as 61.44 says for the supply units.
2. **The hexes on a shortest path.** A hex is between two places when going
   through it costs no extra distance: its distance to one plus its distance
   to the other equals the distance between them. A hex is inside a
   triangle when its centre lies inside or on the triangle drawn between
   the three corner hexes' centres. Sea hexes are left out.
3. **The columns between.** Any land hex whose column lies between the two
   places' columns, however far inland.

## Decision

Option 2. "Between" two places is every land hex on a shortest path from
one to the other, and "inside a triangle" every land hex whose centre lies
inside or on it; the data lists the hexes, and the four Italian divisions
carry `apart: 2`.

## Rationale

The printed text separates the readings. In the same scenario the supply
units go on "the road hexes between El Agheila and Nofilia", while the
dumps go on "any hex between" and the divisions "anywhere between": had the
designer meant the road he would have said so, as one paragraph later he
did, so option 1 reads a restriction into two sentences that do not have
it. Option 3 would let infantry divisions without trucks start thirty
hexes south in the desert, which "between" two coastal places does not
suggest. Option 2 gives a band along the coast four or five hexes deep
(29 land hexes from Ras el Ali to Nofilia, 44 from El Agheila), enough for
four divisions two hexes apart, and needs nothing but a hex distance,
which works across sheet seams. The triangle test is the plain geometry of
the printed figure; counting a hex whose centre is on an edge keeps the
corners, which the text names, inside. "No division less than two hexes
from another" is a hex distance of at least two between any hex holding
part of one division and any holding part of another: two divisions may
not share or touch hexes.
