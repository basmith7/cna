# R-107 — "Active" and "inactive" dumps in the Crusader set-up

## Problem

Each side is given more dump sites than real dumps and told that only some
are active. 59.53 speaks of active and non-active dumps as real and dummy
ones, but it is not said whether an inactive site here is a dummy counter
or an empty real dump that can be filled later.

## Options

1. **Dummies.** Inactive dumps are dummy dumps (59.53); the data records
   them so.
2. **Empty real dumps.** They start empty but are real and can be stocked.

## Decision

Option 1. An inactive dump is a dummy dump.

## Rationale

59.53, the general rule for reading set-ups, says in terms that a
scenario's non-active dumps are dummies (and so may not share a hex with
an air facility). Crusader's set-up uses the same words and gives no sign
of meaning anything else. Option 2 would invent a kind of dump the game
does not otherwise have. The scenario data already records them as dummy
dumps.

Decided under Brian's delegation of 2026-09-26; open to dispute and
supersession like any ruling (see [rulings/README.md](README.md)).
