# R-001 — The −26 collapse threshold: cohesion level or disorganisation points?

## Problem

Two cases name the number 26 as the point at which a unit collapses, but they
name two different quantities. Case 6.26 sets the floor at a **cohesion level**
of −26: a unit whose level reaches −26 loses the ability to move, attack or defend and surrenders to
any adjacent enemy combat unit. The general rule of Case 17.5, listing the ways
a unit can be captured, instead names "26 or more **Disorganization Points**."

Cohesion level and disorganisation points are not the same quantity in this
ruleset. DP are earned and then spent to lower the cohesion level (6.22); RP
raise it (6.23). A unit can hold a very negative cohesion level without having
26 DP outstanding at any single instant, and — because DP are applied
immediately and not banked — "26 or more DP" has no persistent meaning once
they have been applied to the level.

## Options

1. **Threshold is cohesion level −26** (follow 6.26). The 17.5 mention is a
   loose summary using "DP" where it means the cohesion level DP drive.
   Consequence: one clear, testable collapse condition; 17.5's wording is
   treated as informal.
2. **Threshold is 26 DP accumulated** (follow 17.5 literally). Consequence:
   requires tracking a running DP total separate from the cohesion level, which
   6.22 says DP are immediately applied to and not otherwise retained; needs an
   invented reset rule.

## Decision

Option 1. The operative collapse condition is a **cohesion level of −26 or
worse**, as stated in the specific rule 6.26. The word "Disorganization Points"
in the 17.5 general rule is read as shorthand for the disorganised cohesion
level, not a second counter.

## Rationale

6.26 is the specific, procedural case; 17.5 is an introductory summary of an
unrelated topic (voluntary surrender). Where a specific case and a general
summary conflict, the specific case governs. Option 2 would also contradict
6.22's rule that DP are applied immediately and not held as a standing total,
so it cannot be implemented without inventing rules the text does not give.
