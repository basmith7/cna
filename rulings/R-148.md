# R-148 — Under abstract supply (§32) desert raiders need no supply calls

## Problem

27.35 makes a desert raider call once a game-turn at a friendly dump, oasis
or well: it takes 1 ammunition and 2 fuel from a dump, waits where none is
to be had, and is eliminated if it has drawn no water by the turn's end.
27.38 says those 2 fuel are the only fuel it uses. Under §32, 32.11 says to
ignore every ammunition, fuel, stores and water rule, and 32.2x lists the
fuel each kind of unit pays instead. Does a raider still make its weekly
call under §32, and still die without water? cna-engine asked.

## Options

1. **No calls.** Under §32, 27.35 does not apply: a raider draws nothing,
   never waits for supply and is never eliminated for want of water. It
   pays no 32.2x fuel either.
2. **Calls at supply units.** Once a game-turn a raider passes through a
   friendly supply unit, oasis or well; at a supply unit it takes 1
   ammunition and 2 fuel; with no water drawn by the turn's end it dies.
3. **Water only.** The ammunition and fuel draw lapses, but a raider must
   still reach a supply unit, oasis or well each game-turn or die.

## Decision

Option 1. Under abstract supply a desert raider makes no 27.35 call: it
draws no ammunition, fuel or water, never waits for them, is never
eliminated for want of water, and pays no 32.2x fuel.

## Rationale

The printed text decides it. 27.35 is an ammunition, fuel and water rule,
which 32.11 tells players to ignore, and 32.11 adds that nothing suffers for
want of them; a raider dying of thirst is exactly that. 32.0 replaces every
supply expenditure of the Land rules with the costs of 32.2x, and those name
battalion- and company-equivalents; a raider has no stacking points (27.14)
and is neither, so it owes nothing there. Option 2 also needs the dumps
27.35 names, and under §32 none is built ([R-131](R-131.md)); it would
invent a draw on supply units that §32 never prints. Option 3 keeps a
water rule that §32 switches off for every other unit. Option 1 matches
[R-133](R-133.md), where construction under §32 spends no supplies, and is
the simplest for an engine. A raider's reach is still checked by its
CPA (27.37) and by spotting (27.4x), so going without supply calls opens no
exploit beyond the endurance §32 already gives every unit.

supersession like any ruling (see [rulings/README.md](README.md)).
