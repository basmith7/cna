# R-119 — Supply units, real or dummy, in a supply trace

## Problem

The supply trace in 32.16 (and, borrowed from it, the Land-only victory
trace of R-110) fails at an enemy ZOC "unoccupied by Friendly units", and
R-110 also refuses a route through a hex holding an enemy unit and a source
hex holding one. Nothing says whether a supply unit counts as a "unit" for
these three tests: blocking the route, denying the hold, or lifting an enemy
ZOC. 32.18 adds dummy supply units, which look the same on the map until
they are found. cna-engine counts them, real and dummy, as units everywhere.

## Options

1. **Combat units only.** A supply unit, real or dummy, neither blocks a
   route, denies a hold nor lifts a ZOC; only combat units do.
2. **Every unit.** Real supply units count (they are counters on the map);
   a dummy counts as a real one until found.
3. **Real supply units count; dummies do not.** A real one is an ordinary
   unit, a dummy is a bluff.

## Decision

Option 1. In every supply trace and victory trace, only a combat unit is a
"unit": enemy combat units block and deny, friendly combat units lift an
enemy ZOC, and supply units of either kind, real or dummy, do none of these.

## Rationale

32.13 says a supply unit has no combat capabilities and is captured, not
fought, when an enemy combat unit enters its hex, so it cannot hold a hex
against anything. 10.26 is the general ZOC rule: only a friendly *combat*
unit cancels an enemy ZOC for movement; 10.29 uses the same test to let a
non-combat unit into a ZOC; and 60.82 scores places held "by combat units".
32.16's phrase "unoccupied by Friendly units" is the loose wording of the
same test, not a second, wider one: a dump that could cancel ZOC would let
a player march a dump to the front and open his own supply line. Option 3
would make dummies act differently from real dumps, which would give a
dummy away to the opponent and defeat 32.18's purpose (to confuse). Option 2
makes a bluff counter hold ground. For an engine: when tracing or checking a
hold, filter pieces to combat units first; never look at supply units, real
or dummy.

R-110 cited 32.17 for the tracing standard; the tracing is in 32.16 (32.17 is
destroying supply units). The citation there is corrected.
