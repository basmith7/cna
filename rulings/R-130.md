# R-130 — Maximum attachments for parents the chart does not name

## Problem

19.41 caps a parent's non-assigned attachments by the Maximum Attachment
Chart (19.5), which "applies to every unit of a given size and type". Three
kinds of German and Commonwealth headquarters have no row by name. The
German table lists Armor and Infantry Divisions, Infantry or Armor
Regiments, Battle Groups and the Artillery Brigade HQ, but two German
parents are brigades (the 15th Schützen Brigade and the Ramcke Brigade)
and four artillery HQs are regiments (the 155th, 220th, 1st and 2nd Afrika
Artillery Regiments) beside one true artillery command (ArKo 104). The
Commonwealth table has Tank Brigade and "other Brigade" rows, and the
Commonwealth fielded army tank brigades (the 1st and 32nd) as well as
armoured brigades. cna-engine asked for each HQ's row.

## Options

1. **Nearest row by size and type.** German infantry brigades take the
   Infantry or Armor Regiment row (one unit); German artillery regiment HQs
   take the Artillery Brigade HQ row (one artillery unit); army tank
   brigades take the Tank Brigade rows, as armoured brigades do.
2. **No row: the O/A notes govern**, as for parents with singular
   structures (19.2), so these HQs take no non-assigned attachments.
3. **Division rows for the German brigades** (a 3 SP brigade is half a
   division), and "other Brigade" for army tank brigades.

## Decision

Option 1. German brigades use the Infantry or Armor Regiment row, German
artillery regiment HQs the Artillery Brigade HQ row, and Commonwealth army
tank brigades the Tank Brigade rows.

## Rationale

9.22 counts regiments as brigade-equivalents, so the German table's
regiment row is its brigade row in all but name, and a 3 SP German brigade
is a brigade-equivalent. An artillery regiment HQ is the same kind of
parent as an artillery command, a brigade-equivalent artillery HQ, and the
row's limit (one artillery unit) is the type restriction that matters.
An army tank brigade is a brigade of tank battalions, which is what "Tank
Brigade" describes, and the chart has no separate army tank row. Option 2 would leave the 15th
Schützen Brigade unable to take any attachment while every regiment under
it can take one, an inversion of size. Option 3 gives a brigade a
division's allowance, which no row supports. The data carries the choice
as each HQ's `attachment_parent` (rules/70-organisation.md).

Decided under Brian's delegation of 2026-09-26; open to dispute and
supersession like any ruling (see [rulings/README.md](README.md)).
