# R-135 — Rommel: his own counter, and where he starts

## Problem

Section 31 gives the Axis "a counter representing General Rommel": a
4-wheel-drive medium truck with CPA 60 and no combat ratings. No OA sheet
lists such a counter, but the German Unassigned Infantry sheet abbreviates
the Deutsches Afrika Korps HQ counter "Rommel" (ID code a, CPA 60, no
combat ratings, arriving 2/20). Is that HQ the Rommel counter, or is Rommel
a separate piece? And the scenario set-ups never place Rommel: Rommel's
Arrival places the DAK HQ, Operation Crusader and The Last Chance place
neither, though Rommel commanded in all three.

## Options

1. **The DAK HQ is Rommel.** One piece, an HQ with the 31.0 abilities.
2. **Rommel is a piece of his own.** He arrives with the DAK HQ (2/20) and
   is placed where the scenario's German units are.
3. **Rommel is a marker** on whichever German unit the Axis player names,
   with no hex of his own.

## Decision

Option 2. Rommel is a separate piece, `unit:de:rommel`, arriving with the
DAK HQ at 2/20; a scenario that starts after that places him in any hex
holding a German unit, within the set-up's limits where it groups the
German units (Rommel's Arrival).

## Rationale

The Raid on Rommel Table's key (27.92) eliminates the raiding LRDG "unless
there are no combat and/or HQ units in the hex", and its 12 removes "the
Rommel counter" from the map. If Rommel were the DAK HQ, his hex would
always hold an HQ and the key's exception could never apply, and a lucky
raid would eliminate a corps HQ the table never mentions; option 1 makes
printed text dead. 31.0 also describes him as a truck, not an HQ, and the
initiative rule (7.1x) asks only whether Rommel is on the map. The DAK HQ's
"Rommel" abbreviation is read as the counter's label, as other HQ counters
carry a commander's name. Option 3 cannot be raided in a hex of his own,
which 27.6x requires. He arrives with the DAK HQ because that is the only
arrival the sheets give for him, and it matches his landing in February
1941. The scenarios' silence is a gap, not an absence: each starts after
2/20, and placing him with any German unit is the least the set-up must
add. For an engine he is one more counter with `commander` values in place
of a unit-characteristics row.
