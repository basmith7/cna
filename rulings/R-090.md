# R-090 — Axis fuel lost on landing: rounding and which landings count

## Problem

Without the Air Game, 58.3 takes three-quarters of the fuel the Axis brings
into a port the moment it is unloaded. It gives no rounding for amounts not
divisible by four, and does not say whether only fuel arriving by naval
convoy from Europe counts, or also fuel moved between African ports by
coastal or tactical shipping. If the latter, the same fuel could be cut
again at each port it is moved through.

## Options

1. **Convoy fuel only; loss rounded up.** Only fuel landed from the
   European convoys is cut; the loss is rounded up (the Axis keeps the
   rounded-down quarter). Consequence: fuel is cut once; slightly harsher
   on small cargoes.
2. **Convoy fuel only; loss rounded down.** Consequence: fuel is cut once;
   the Axis keeps a point on odd cargoes.
3. **Every unloading in a port, any shipping; loss rounded up.**
   Consequence: literal reading; coastal hops become very costly and the
   Axis will avoid moving fuel by sea along the coast.

## Decision

Not yet decided (Task 10 of the Logistics Game plan).
