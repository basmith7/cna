# V-004 — Stores may be paid in instalments

## What the printed rules say

Case 51.1 sets each unit's stores requirement per Game-Turn (four Stores
Points per TOE Strength Point, 51.11), paid from stores in its hex when it
falls due; stores cannot be used straight off
third-line trucks (51.15) and cannot be left on the ground outside a dump
(54.13). The restated rule is in the Logistics Game's ammunition and stores
file (`rules/logistics/20-ammunition-and-stores.md`).

## The change

NJHarman's house rule:[^nj]

> [51.1] Unit’s Stores requirements may be “paid” in “installments”
> throughout the previous OpStages.

[^nj]: NJHarman, *CfNA House Rules & Interpretations*, § Logistics,
  <https://friendorfoe.com/war/cfna/houserules/#logistics>, CC-BY-SA 4.0 (tagged CHANGE). See `ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## Effect on play

- Units may bank stores toward their Game-Turn requirement in any earlier
  Operations Stage of the turn, instead of needing the whole amount in hand
  at once.
- Removes the scramble to attach and detach trucks with stores in the third
  Operations Stage.

## Why it is recorded as a variant

The printed timing is clear; the item changes it to ease play. Under the
[rulings policy](README.md) that is a variant.
