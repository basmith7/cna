# R-016 — When a Tank Delivery Squadron moves

## Problem

A Tank Delivery Squadron (22.61–22.66) is a Commonwealth vehicle counter with
a CPA of 25, no combat ratings and no stacking points; it repairs tanks, holds
reserves and tows. The cases give its CPA and its ZOC restrictions but never
say in which phase it moves: the movement/combat phase with combat units, or
the truck convoy phase with the other non-combat vehicles. NJHarman:[^nj-tds]

> [22.6] TDS move during Truck Convoy Movement Phase, even when not towing.
> It's not stated when they move. *Based on [22.63] intent was probably
> during Truck Convoy if not towing. Cleaner to always move during same
> phase.*

[^nj-tds]: NJHarman, *CfNA House Rules & Interpretations*, § Tank Delivery
  Squadrons, <https://friendorfoe.com/war/cfna/houserules/#tank-delivery-squadrons>,
  CC-BY-SA 4.0 (tagged INTERPRETATION). See `ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## Options

1. **Truck convoy phase only** (NJHarman). A TDS, towing or not, moves in
   phase H with the convoys and prisoners. Consequence: one phase, no
   interaction with continual movement, and it can never be part of a
   phasing cycle.
2. **Movement/combat phase, like a combat unit.** Consequence: it can keep
   pace with the armour it serves during a cycle, but as a 0-SP non-combat
   counter it can neither exert nor tolerate a ZOC and would be caught by
   the two-hex rule.
3. **Either, at the owner's choice each stage, not both.** The rule used for
   Land Game supply units (32.3x). Consequence: flexible, and consistent
   with the closest analogue in the rules; requires tracking which phase it
   used.

## Decision

None yet — proposed.

## Rationale

The cases treat a TDS as a vehicle rather than a combat unit: no ratings, no
SP, a ban on entering enemy ZOC, elimination if caught alone in one. Those
are the properties of a convoy, and 22.65's towing role puts it in the same
phase as the repair-and-tow traffic. Option 1 is the reading the restated
rule would need if one phase must be named; option 3 is what the designers
chose for the other non-combat mover they wrote rules for in the Land Game,
the supply unit, and may be the better analogy. The restated text
([Engineering — Tank delivery squadrons](../rules/80-engineering.md#tank-delivery))
does not currently name a phase.
