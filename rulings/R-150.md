# R-150 — A raider may be formed from any unit at its assigned basic morale

## Problem

27.12 forms a desert raider only from a unit that has reached its maximum
morale "through training", and bars untrained units. Under 17.3 only some
Commonwealth units ever train: those whose OA sheet prints an untrained
rating in parentheses (`basic_morale_alt` in the data). German and Italian
units never train. Read word for word, the DSA, which 27.2 says is formed
"in the same way" from German units only, could never be formed, and
nearly every Commonwealth armoured-car unit could never form an LRDG. Which
units qualify?

## Options

1. **At its assigned basic morale.** A unit qualifies when its current
   basic morale equals its assigned (printed) basic morale: every unit whose
   OA entry has no untrained rating, from the start, and a unit that has one
   once 17.34 training has raised it to its assigned rating. A unit still
   below its assigned rating may not form a raider.
2. **Trained units only.** Only a unit that started below its assigned
   rating and has trained up to it under 17.3x qualifies.
3. **Any unit.** The morale condition is ignored.

## Decision

Option 1. A raider is formed from a unit at its assigned basic morale; only
a unit still below it, with an untrained rating it has not yet trained out
of, is barred.

## Rationale

27.12's purpose is to keep raw troops out of the raiders, and its second
sentence says so: untrained units may never be used. A unit that never
needed training is not untrained; 17.3 explains that the Germans and most
Italians come already seasoned, which is why they have no untrained rating.
Option 2 reads "through training" so strictly that the DSA could never exist,
contradicting 27.2, and it would leave the LRDG to the handful of
Commonwealth formations that print a parenthesised rating. The rules
themselves treat the two cases alike elsewhere: 17.35's instruction
battalion is one that "has (or has reached)" its designated morale. Option 3
drops a printed restriction that does bite on the units that arrive green
(the 1st South African and 6th Australian Divisions among them). For an
engine without training, Option 1 means: a unit qualifies when neither it
nor the formation whose morale it uses has a `basic_morale_alt`; one that
does qualifies only once it is tracked as trained up. [R-147](R-147.md)'s
remark that no set-up unit is "shown as trained" read the condition
narrowly; its decision stands on its other grounds.
