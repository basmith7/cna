# R-081 — Which ports are major ports?

## Problem

Case 55.11 splits ports into major ports (troops and supplies in) and minor
ports (supplies only in), but no list says which is which. The Port
Capacity Chart (55.3) instead gives each port an incoming stacking-point
figure: a number for Tripoli, Bizerta, Alexandria, Tobruk, Benghazi and
Mersa Matruh; zero (with one zero-stacking-point unit allowed) for Bardia
and Sollum; "na" for Derna and all others.

## Options

1. **The chart decides.** A port with an incoming stacking-point figure
   above zero is major; Bardia and Sollum are minor but may land the one
   zero-point unit the chart allows; Derna and the rest are minor.
   Consequence: no separate list is needed; the two classes are just a
   summary of the chart.
2. **Only the three level-10 ports are major** (Tripoli, Bizerta,
   Alexandria). Consequence: Tobruk, Benghazi and Mersa Matruh may not land
   troops at all, which makes their chart figures for stacking points in
   meaningless.

## Decision

Option 1. The chart decides: a port with an incoming stacking-point figure above zero (Tripoli, Bizerta, Alexandria, Tobruk, Benghazi, Mersa Matruh) is major; Bardia and Sollum are minor but may land one zero-stacking-point unit; Derna and all others are minor.

## Rationale

This is not a question about the physical printing: the chart's incoming
stacking-point column is transcribed and cross-checked in
`data/tables/port-capacity.json`, and it settles the matter. Case 55.11
defines a major port by its ability to take troops in, and the chart says
exactly how many stacking points of troops each port may take in, so the
two classes are a summary of that column rather than a separate list. The
one wrinkle, Bardia and Sollum's zero figure with a single zero-point unit
allowed, is kept as the chart prints it. Option 2 has no textual basis and
would make three printed chart figures meaningless. An engine needs no
extra field: it reads `sp_in`.

supersession like any ruling (see [rulings/README.md](README.md)).
