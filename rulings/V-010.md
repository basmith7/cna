# V-010 — No Flak Destruction missions

## What the printed rules say

Bombers may fly a Flak Destruction mission against Flak in a hex (41.33), separate from reducing a fortification (41.37).

## The change

NJHarman's house rule:[^nj]

> [41.33] No Flak Destruction missions.  Is too easy to wack TOE Pts.  Instead,
> successful [41.37] Fortification reduction also destroys 1 pure Flak TOE pt,
> if any, in target hex. May fly that mission vs non-fort to target Flak.

[^nj]: NJHarman, *CfNA House Rules & Interpretations*, § Air War,
  <https://friendorfoe.com/war/cfna/houserules/#air-war>, CC-BY-SA 4.0 (tagged CHANGE ADDITION, for the whole Air War section). See `ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## Effect on play

The Flak Destruction mission is dropped. A successful fortification-reduction mission also removes one pure Flak TOE point in the hex, and may be flown at a hex with no fortification purely to hit its Flak.

## Why it is recorded as a variant

It removes a printed mission type.
