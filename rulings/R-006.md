# R-006 — Who may never react: the reach of "not stacked with friendly combat units" in 8.53a

## Problem

Case 8.53a names three kinds of unit that can never react — foot units,
squadron ground-support units, truck convoys — and ends the list with a
condition about being stacked alongside a friendly combat unit. Because the
condition comes last, it can be read as binding the whole list or the
convoys alone.
NJHarman:[^nj-react]

> Non-motorized units and SGSUs may not React. Truck Convoys and Tank
> Delivery Squadrons may not react unless stacked with a friendly combat
> unit [8.53a]. *Is unclear if "… not stacked with Friendly combat units"
> applies to the whole list or just Truck Convoys. SGSU and non-motorized
> are not agile enough to GTFO.*

[^nj-react]: NJHarman, *CfNA House Rules & Interpretations*, § Reaction,
  <https://friendorfoe.com/war/cfna/houserules/#reaction>, CC-BY-SA 4.0
  (tagged INTERPRETATION). See `ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## Options

1. **The qualifier binds truck convoys only** (NJHarman). Non-motorised
   units and SGSUs never react, stacked or not; a truck convoy reacts only
   when stacked with a friendly combat unit. Consequence: the exemption
   protects supply columns caught with their escort and nothing else.
2. **The qualifier binds the whole list.** A non-motorised unit or SGSU
   stacked with a friendly combat unit may react. Consequence: foot infantry
   sharing a hex with a tank battalion could react out of contact, which
   contradicts the design commentary's point that reaction models the
   agility of motorised troops, and would make the CPA test in 8.53b
   meaningless for foot units (their CPA of 10 is always six or more below
   a motorised attacker's).

## Decision

None yet — proposed.

## Rationale

Option 1. Reaction is movement, and a non-motorised unit moving during the
enemy's half would be spending CP it cannot recover for its own half —
possible in principle, but the 8.53b test already bars any foot unit from
reacting to a motorised attacker who declares an assault, so option 2 would
let foot units react only against slower foot attackers, an odd design.
The restated rule reads the list as option 1 ([Movement —
Reaction](../rules/40-movement.md#reaction)). NJHarman's extension of the
same treatment to tank delivery squadrons rests on 22.63, which is not part
of 8.53a; it is taken up in the engineering rulings.
