# R-091 — How many trucks leave the initial set-up without the Air Game?

## Problem

58.41 reduces each player's initial set-up by the Truck Points that would
supply air facilities, but gives no figure, and the scenario set-ups do not
mark which trucks those are. An engine cannot build a set-up without a
number.

## Options

1. **10 % of set-up Truck Points, rounded up, in proportion by type.**
   Mirrors 58.43's cut on arriving trucks. Consequence: consistent with the
   rest of §58; a single rule for every scenario.
2. **No reduction until a figure is found.** Treat 58.41 as unusable and
   rely on 58.42 and 58.43. Consequence: both sides start slightly
   truck-rich compared with the designer's intent.
3. **A per-scenario figure written into each scenario file.** Consequence:
   most faithful if a source is found; needs research and scenario data work
   before play.

## Decision

Option 1. Each player removes 10 % of the initial set-up's Truck Points, rounded up, in proportion by truck type and between convoy and attached trucks.

## Rationale

58.41 clearly means the set-up to shrink, so option 2 (no reduction) runs
against the text. It gives no figure, though, and nothing in the rules or
scenario set-ups supplies one; this is a gap in the rules, not a question
about the physical printing. The nearest printed guide is 58.43, which
withholds 10 % (fractions up) of trucks arriving later for exactly the same
reason, the trucks that would serve air facilities. Using that rate for the
starting fleet makes the two cases agree, needs no per-scenario research
(option 3), and gives an engine one formula. Splitting the cut in proportion
borrows the method 58.42 already sets for truck losses. If a scenario figure
is found later, a new ruling can supersede this one.

Decided under Brian's delegation of 2026-09-26; open to dispute and supersession like any ruling (see [rulings/README.md](README.md)).
