# R-080 — Is a port's capacity per Operations Stage or per Game-Turn?

## Problem

The Port Capacity Chart (55.3) and its key give stacking points and tonnage
per Operations Stage, and 55.14's Benghazi example speaks of tons per
OpStage. Case 55.16 says a port's capacity applies to everything received in
a Game-Turn, OpStages included, and warns players to adjust capacities for
ports used only in the Strategic Phase. An engine needs one period to reset
the counter.

## Options

1. **Per Operations Stage** (chart and 55.14). Each stage's receipts are
   capped separately; 55.16 is read as saying that Strategic Phase
   deliveries count against the stage they fall in. Consequence: a port
   handles up to three times the chart figure per Game-Turn; Axis convoys,
   which arrive once a turn, still meet only one stage's cap.
2. **Per Game-Turn** (55.16). The chart figure is the whole turn's
   allowance, shared by convoy arrivals and all stage-by-stage traffic.
   Consequence: much tighter ports; Commonwealth coastal transfers during
   the stages eat into what a convoy could land.
