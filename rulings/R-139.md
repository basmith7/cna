# R-139 — Whose ZOC pins in 8.53c

## Problem

8.53c bars a reaction by a non-phasing unit that is "already" in an enemy
ZOC, and names 8.53b as the usual way it gets there. Reaction (8.51) is triggered
by an enemy combat unit moving next to the reacting unit, and almost every
such unit exerts a ZOC into the hex it has just moved next to. If that
mover's own ZOC counts, then only a mover too small to exert a ZOC ever
draws a reaction. cna-engine asked which ZOC the case means.

## Options

1. **Any enemy ZOC, the mover's included.** The unit's hex is checked after
   the mover arrives. A mover that exerts a ZOC never draws a reaction.
2. **Only an enemy ZOC the unit stood in before the mover arrived.** The
   unit's hex is checked as it was just before the mover entered the hex
   next to it; the ZOC the mover now throws into the hex is ignored for
   this check.

## Decision

Option 2. A unit is pinned by 8.53c only if its hex was already in an enemy
ZOC before the moving unit entered the hex next to it; the mover's own new
ZOC never pins it by itself.

## Rationale

The printed case says "already", and names its usual cause: a unit that
could not react under 8.53b, because a faster unit announced an assault,
is left in that unit's ZOC and so cannot react to anything that arrives
later. Under option 1, 8.53b would do nothing, since the fast unit's ZOC
would pin the target anyway, and the CPA comparison, the assault
announcement and the size limit of 8.54 would all be dead text. Option 1
would also leave the reaction of 8.51 to only those movers that exert no
ZOC, which turns the game's main defensive tool into a rarity. R-138
already reads 8.53c this way for Rommel; this ruling makes it the rule for
every unit.

What counts, for an engine:

- **When to look.** Judge the unit's hex in the position just before the
  moving stack entered the hex that puts it adjacent: the mover at the hex
  it left, everything else where it stands. If the hex was in an enemy ZOC
  then, the unit may not react to this move.
- **What pins later.** A unit that could react and did not (by choice or
  by 8.53b) now stands in the mover's ZOC. When any enemy unit, the same
  mover included, next moves adjacent to it, it is "already" in an enemy
  ZOC and may not react.
- **Size limit.** 8.54 still applies: a ZOC that pins under 8.53c must come
  from a unit able to pin by size (no battalion pins a division, no company
  a brigade or larger). A ZOC from a unit too small to pin the reactor is
  ignored for this check.
- **Contact.** A unit in an enemy ZOC at the start of the movement segment
  is in contact and barred by 8.53d anyway ([R-008](R-008.md)); 8.53c adds
  the units that came under an enemy ZOC during the segment.
