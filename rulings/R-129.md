# R-129 — Abstract supply: surrender of a hex whose only armed defenders owe no ammunition

## Problem

[R-120](R-120.md) makes a unit that cannot pay 32.21's ammunition out of
ammunition, and the Land Game makes a hex surrender when every defender in
it is out of ammunition and it is assaulted. Some defenders owe nothing
under 32.21: HQs, as cna-engine reads the case, and anything else that is
not charged as a battalion- or company-equivalent. Such a unit can never be
"out". So when every unit that owes ammunition is out, does a hex that also
holds an HQ surrender? cna-engine #40 says yes and asked for confirmation.

## Options

1. **Yes.** The surrender test looks only at defenders that owe ammunition:
   if at least one does and every one of them is out, the hex surrenders,
   the units that owe none included.
2. **No.** A unit that owes nothing is never out, so any HQ in the hex keeps
   it from surrendering.

## Decision

Option 1. An assaulted hex surrenders when it holds at least one defender
that owes ammunition and every such defender is out; HQs and other units
that owe none surrender with them. A hex with no defender that owes
ammunition never surrenders for want of it.

## Rationale

The surrender rule describes a garrison with nothing left to fight with;
under §32 being out of ammunition only means being unable to pay 32.21's
cost, so units that pay nothing add no fighting power to the test either
way. Option 2 would make every HQ an anchor against surrender, so stacking
an HQ in a doomed hex would save its whole garrison, which no reading of
the Land Game supports: an HQ does not supply the units with it. The
"at least one" clause stops the test from passing vacuously, so a hex of
HQs alone is assaulted in the ordinary way. This confirms the reading
cna-engine #40 already builds on. For an engine: among committed and
uncommitted defenders, take those that owe ammunition; if that set is
non-empty and all are out, the hex surrenders.
