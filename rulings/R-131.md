# R-131 — Abstract supply: no supply dumps are built

## Problem

Case 24.9 lets any TOE point build a supply dump for 3 CP (stores in the
Logistics Game only) or a dummy dump for 2 CP ([R-018](R-018.md)). With the
Logistics Game off, §32 shows supplies only as **supply units**, each a
supply dump counter with its own load (32.12), and caps dummies at one per
three real supply units received or started with (32.18). It does not say
whether 24.9 still applies. If it does, what does a real dump built for
3 CP hold, and may 2-CP dummy dumps go past 32.18's cap? cna-engine asked.

## Options

1. **24.9 does not apply.** Under §32 no dump is built, real or dummy.
   Supply units come only from the supply schedule (32.4x), and dummies
   only under 32.18's cap.
2. **Dummies only.** Real dumps are meaningless, but a player may still
   build dummy dumps for 2 CP each, beyond 32.18's cap.
3. **A real dump is a new supply unit.** Building one in a hex splits part
   of a supply unit's load there into a new counter; dummies are built as
   in option 2.

## Decision

Option 1. With §32 in force, 24.9 is off: no supply dump or dummy dump is
built, supply units arrive only by the supply schedule, and dummies are
limited to 32.18's one per three.

## Rationale

Case 32.11 says that, of the supply rules, only those in §32 apply, and
24.9 is a supply rule: a dump is a place to keep loose ammunition and fuel,
and under §32 there is no loose supply, only supply units that are already
dump counters. A real dump would hold nothing. Option 2 would let a player
buy unlimited dummies for 2 CP each, which makes 32.18's cap pointless.
Option 3 invents a split rule the text never gives; supply units in one
hex may already rearrange their contents in the organisation phase, which
covers what a split would be for. Option 1 is the printed
reading and the simplest for an engine: it builds no dumps under §32. The
Logistics Game keeps 24.9 and [R-018](R-018.md) unchanged.
