# R-020 — How often may water be drawn from one well in an Operations Stage?

## Problem

Drawing water (52.13) is a per-unit action: a unit in a well hex pays one
CP, rolls, and reads the Water Availability Table (52.7) for the points
drawn and whether the well is now depleted. Nothing in 52.1x says how many
times a well may be drawn from in one Operations Stage. Read literally, every
unit in the hex — and every unit that passes through it — may pay its CP and
roll, so a village well can be drawn a dozen times in a stage until a roll
depletes it; the depletion column then does very little to limit water in a
busy hex. NJHarman's notes from the Discord game treat a well as yielding one
draw per Operations Stage.[^nj-discord]

[^nj-discord]: NJHarman, private notes from the Discord game, 2026-09
  (paraphrased; not part of his published house rules and not under the
  CC-BY-SA grant, so not quoted).

## Options

1. **One draw per well per Operations Stage** (NJHarman's practice). The
   first unit to pay the CP rolls; the well is then closed for the rest of
   the stage, depleted or not. Consequence: the table's yield is a
   per-stage yield, which is what its "per source" figures look like; a
   stack that needs more than one draw's worth must move on or wait.
2. **Any number of draws, each by a different unit.** Each draw is a
   separate CP, roll and depletion check. Consequence: the literal reading;
   a well in a crowded hex empties fast on the depletion column, and the
   secret-depletion rule (52.14) is what stops the opponent from knowing the
   well is dry. Water in coastal villages becomes abundant for whoever
   holds them with many small units.
3. **One draw per unit per Operations Stage, any number of units.** The
   middle reading: a unit may not draw twice in a stage, but each unit
   present may draw once. Consequence: the same as option 2 for stacks and
   limits only the single-unit case.

## Decision

None yet — proposed.

## Rationale

The Water Availability Table is written per source, not per unit, and 52.14
treats the draw that depletes a well as a single event by a single player,
which sits more easily with one draw per well per stage than with many.
Option 1 is the reading the table supports and the one that makes depletion
matter; it is also the one already used at the table. Option 2 is what the
text literally allows and is the reading a rules lawyer would defend; it
should be rejected on the record rather than left open, because it is the
default anyone reads first. This is a Logistics Game rule (§52); the Land
Game abstraction ignores water entirely
([§32](../rules/95-abstract-logistics-and-air.md)), so the ruling has no
effect on the restated Land Game prose beyond the CP cost table's well
entries ([Capability points — costs](../rules/30-capability-points.md)).
