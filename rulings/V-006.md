# V-006 — Maintenance in a scenario's first Operations Stage

## What the printed rules say

No plane may be maintained in a scenario's first Operations Stage
([Reading a scenario](../rules/scenarios/00-reading-scenarios.md#air)).

## The change

NJHarman's house rule:[^nj]

> CHANGE  Ignore [59.36] and [60.32] maintenance may be performed first
> OpStage after flight.
> Designer intention seemed to be, you have only these aircraft ready. Perhaps
> an artifact from when maintenance was done before missions?

[^nj]: NJHarman, *CfNA House Rules & Interpretations*, § Scenario Notes, 64.0 The Campaign For North Africa,
  <https://friendorfoe.com/war/cfna/houserules/#the-campaign-for-north-africa>, CC-BY-SA 4.0 (tagged CHANGE). See `ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## Effect on play

Planes that fly in the first Operations Stage may be maintained after their
mission, as in any later stage.

## Why it is recorded as a variant

59.36 states the restriction plainly; lifting it is a change, not a reading.
