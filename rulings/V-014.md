# V-014 — Order of air steps after air-to-air combat

## What the printed rules say

After air combat, flak and missions are resolved in the printed order of the air phase.

## The change

NJHarman's house rule:[^nj]

> Resolve flak vs aircraft flying Recon missions
>
> Resolve Recon missions
>
> Roll for semi-sighted aborts
>
> Check for friendly fire
>
> Resolve flak vs aircraft flying non-Recon missions
>
> Resolve non-Recon missions

[^nj]: NJHarman, *CfNA House Rules & Interpretations*, § Air War, Changes to Sequence after Air-to-Air,
  <https://friendorfoe.com/war/cfna/houserules/#changes-to-sequence-after-air-to-air>, CC-BY-SA 4.0 (tagged CHANGE ADDITION, for the whole Air War section). See `ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## Effect on play

Recon and its flak are resolved first, then the sighting and friendly-fire checks of V-016 and V-017, then flak and the other missions.

## Why it is recorded as a variant

It reorders the printed sequence and adds steps that exist only in other variants.
