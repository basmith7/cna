# R-169 — Set-up and schedule aircraft the characteristics chart has no row for

## Problem

Every plane a scenario sets up, and every arrival on the air reinforcement
schedules, needs its ratings from the Aircraft Characteristics Charts
(34.6). The set-up pages and schedules name types in their own shorthand,
and most names find their row once spelling is set aside (`CR 42` is the
C.R. 42 Falco, `Potez 63/11` the 63.11, `Me Bf109F` the Bf. 109F). Some do
not:

- **Marks the chart never prints.** The Last Chance gives the Commonwealth
  Hurricane IIE (63.33), Wellington II (63.33), Wellington V (Malta,
  63.37), and Baltimore II and III (63.33, 63.37). The chart has one
  Baltimore (the A-30 Baltimore I), Wellingtons Mk. I and Mk. IV, and
  Hurricanes up to the IID.
- **A mark printed under another letter.** Crusader and The Last Chance
  field the Beaufighter IC (62.36, 63.33, 63.37); the chart's Mk. I row is
  the IF.
- **Names printed without a mark where the chart has several.** Crusader's
  "Beaufighters" (62.33: IF or VIF), The Last Chance's "Baltimores" at Burg
  el Arab (63.33), its Crete "Ju88's" (63.43: the chart's Ju. 88C night
  fighter or Ju. 88D bomber), and the Commonwealth schedule's March 1941
  "Wellington" (34.84: Mk. I or Mk. IV).

## Options

1. **The nearest row stands in.** Each name takes the row of the chart's
   closest type, listed below, and plays exactly as that row.
2. **Leave them out.** A plane with no row of its own does not enter play.
   The Last Chance's Commonwealth air force loses 16 Hurricanes, 64
   Baltimores, 21 Wellingtons and its Beaufighter ICs.
3. **Add rows.** Invent ratings for the missing marks from the neighbouring
   rows.

## Decision

Option 1. The stand-ins:

| Printed | Row |
|---|---|
| Hurricane IIE | `aircraft:cw:hurricane-mk-iid` |
| Wellington II, Wellington V, Wellington IC, unmarked Wellington | `aircraft:cw:wellington-mk-i` |
| Baltimore II, Baltimore III, unmarked Baltimore | `aircraft:cw:a-30-baltimore-i` |
| Beaufighter IC, Crusader's unmarked Beaufighter | `aircraft:cw:beaufighter-mk-if` |
| The Last Chance's Crete Ju88 | `aircraft:de:ju-88d` |

Each entry's `aircraft_id` names its row; `type` keeps the printed name.

## Rationale

The set-up pages plainly mean these planes to fly, so option 2 strips a
scenario of forces its designers placed, and option 3 puts numbers in the
game that SPI never printed. Option 1 keeps both the printed order of
battle and the printed ratings. The picks follow the airframe: the
Wellington II and the IC are one airframe with different engines, and the
chart's Mk. I row is the IC the Commonwealth flew from 1940 (the Mk. V
never served in the desert; the nearest Wellington is the one the order
of battle already holds). The Baltimore II and III differ from the I in
defensive guns, not in range or load, and the chart has only the one
Baltimore. The Hurricane IIE was the ground-attack Hurricane that became
the Mk. IV; the IID is the chart's ground-attack Hurricane. The Beaufighter
IC is the Mk. I fitted for Coastal Command, so the Mk. I row. An unmarked
name takes the mark in service at the scenario's or entry's date: in
November 1941 the Western Desert's Beaufighters were Mk. Is, the chart's
VIF arriving later on the schedule; in March 1941 the Wellingtons were ICs.
The Crete Ju88s fly with He 111s as a bomber force, which is the Ju. 88D
row (the Ju. 88C is a night fighter). Names whose chart row is the only
one of that type (Boston, Lysander, Gladiator, the plain Z 1007 against
the chart's Z.1007bis, the Bf 110C against the Bf. 110) are not stand-ins
and need no ruling.
