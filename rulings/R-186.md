# R-186 — Points places' supply conditions under abstract supply

## Problem

Three points scenarios let a place score only for a unit that meets a
supply condition written for the Logistics Game. Operation Crusader (62.8)
wants the holding unit to have supplies to last a month and ammunition for
a week; The Long Retreat (63.92) wants every combat unit in the place to
have a Game-Turn's stores, ammunition to fire every weapon twice and fuel
to move 20 CP; the Italian Campaign (60.82) wants a trace of no more than
one Game-Turn to a dump or city linked back to Cairo or Tripoli. Under
abstract supply (§32) there are no stores, no unit-held stocks and no
dumps (R-131), only supply units drawn on within half a unit's CPA
(32.16). cna-engine plays §32 and asked what each condition becomes.

## Options

1. **Stocks become a draw, lines become a route.** Crusader and The Long
   Retreat ask what a unit has on hand, so their test is 32.16's: at the
   end, every holding combat unit must be within half its CPA of a friendly
   supply unit, traced as 32.16 traces it. The Italian Campaign asks for a
   line home, so its test is R-110's route, of any length, from the
   holding unit to Nofilia for the Italians (R-185) or to any hex of Cairo
   for the Commonwealth.
2. **A route everywhere.** All three use R-110's route; Crusader and The
   Long Retreat to each side's map-edge source.
3. **A draw everywhere.** All three use 32.16's draw.
4. **No test** under §32: holding is enough.

## Decision

Option 1. Under abstract supply, Crusader's and The Long Retreat's holders must be able to draw on a friendly supply unit at the end (32.16), and the Italian Campaign's must have R-110's route to Nofilia (Italian) or Cairo (Commonwealth).

## Rationale

The printed tests are of two kinds. A month's supplies and a week's
ammunition, or a Game-Turn of stores and fuel for 20 CP, are stocks the
unit holds; §32 keeps no stocks on units, and the nearest thing it has is
a supply unit the unit can draw on, which is what 32.16 calls being in
supply. The Italian Campaign's test is a trace "back to" a home base, the
same kind as Graziani's convoy line in the same scenario group, which
R-110 already reads as a route of any length; R-185 uses that route for
the same scenario's cut-off test, so the holders use it too. The
one-Game-Turn limit and the dump-or-city waypoint measure convoy time and
Logistics Game dumps, neither of which §32 has, and drop out as R-110's
convoy wording did. Option 2 needs sources neither Crusader nor The Long
Retreat names; option 3 turns a line test into a local one; option 4
drops a condition the text has.

In the data `victory.points_supply` gives each scoring side's test:
`{"draw": true}` or `{"trace": {...}}`. Played with the Logistics Game, the
printed conditions stand.
