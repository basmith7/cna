# R-060 — The Pasta Point: how often is it due, and how long does missing it bite?

## Problem

52.6 ties the Italian Pasta Point to the moment a battalion "receives its
Stores", and says a battalion that misses it may not exceed its CPA "that
Turn". Stores are normally distributed once per Game-Turn (51.1), but a
unit may also be topped up later in the turn, and an Italian battalion that
sits in an oasis (52.3) draws stores without any distribution at all. Water
usage everywhere else in §52 is per Operations Stage. So the case leaves open
whether the extra Water Point is due once per Game-Turn or at every
delivery of stores, and whether the CPA penalty lasts the whole Game-Turn
or only until the point is received. The Disorganisation effect for units
at −10 or worse is explicitly lifted "as soon as" the point arrives; the CPA
limit has no such wording.

## Options

1. **Once per Game-Turn, with the normal stores distribution; the CPA limit
   lasts the whole Game-Turn.** Consequence: the literal reading of "that
   Turn"; a battalion watered late still cannot force-march that turn,
   while its Cohesion recovers at once. Simple to track: one extra point per
   Italian battalion on the turn's stores list.
2. **Once per Game-Turn, and both effects end the moment the point
   arrives.** Consequence: treats the two penalties alike; a late delivery
   restores the unit fully, which rewards pushing water forward mid-turn.
3. **With every delivery of stores.** Consequence: an Italian battalion
   topped up twice in a turn needs two Pasta Points; heavier on water and
   harder to track, and not supported by the per-turn stores rhythm of 51.1.

## Decision

None yet — proposed.

## Rationale

Option 1 is the plain reading and the easiest to play; option 2 is a
reasonable harmonisation but changes printed wording. Option 3 multiplies
book-keeping for no evident design purpose. Units in an oasis get all the
stores and water they need (52.3), so under any option they never miss the
point.
