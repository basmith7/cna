# R-171 — Supply units and motorisation points as air targets under abstract logistics (§47)

## Problem

With the Air Game and abstract logistics (§47), §32 supplies the ground
game: there are no supply dumps and no truck points, only supply units
(regular and depot, real and dummy) and motorisation points. The Air Game's
bombing (41.32, 41.35) and strafing (40.62, 40.64) targets are written for
the full game's dumps and trucks. 47.51 says only that motorisation points
"may be attacked as though they are trucks" under 40.5–40.6 and 41.2–41.5.
cna-engine asked, for slice 2 of its Air Game:

- Do supply units stand in for supply dumps, and what does a percentage
  result take from one?
- Which motorisation points are **trucks in convoy** (B-TC, strafed one
  column right), and which are a unit's attached **first-line trucks**
  (bombed only by D-capable aircraft, strafed by naming the parent)?

## Options

1. **Supply units are dumps; motorisation points are trucks by what they
   are attached to.** Every supply unit (regular or depot, real or dummy) is
   a supply dump for B-SD and dump strafing. A bombing result applies to
   every supply unit in the hex; each strafing aircraft hits one supply
   unit (40.53). A percentage takes that share of the unit's ammunition and
   of its fuel, each rounded up. A dummy attacked is revealed and removed
   (54.12). Motorisation points attached to a combat unit are its
   first-line trucks; all others in a hex (carrying a supply unit, or
   detached) are trucks in convoy. A truck loss removes motorisation
   points and nothing else.
2. **Supply units are dumps, but a percentage takes whole supply units**
   (10 % of a hex's supply units, rounded as the convoy results of 47.65
   round). Motorisation points as in option 1.
3. **No dump target.** Supply units are not dumps; only motorisation
   points can be hit (47.51 names them alone). The B-SD mission and dump
   strafing are unavailable under §47.

## Decision

Option 1. Every supply unit is a supply dump, a percentage takes that share
of each unit's ammunition and fuel (rounded up), and motorisation points
are first-line trucks when attached to a combat unit and trucks in convoy
otherwise.

## Rationale

32.11 shows a supply unit with a supply dump counter and records its
contents on the supply dump log, and [R-149](R-149.md) already treats it as
a dump for a desert raider's raid; reading it as a dump for bombing and
strafing is the same reading. Option 3 leaves the Air Game's land support
with no way to hit supplies, which nothing in §47 asks for: 47.51 adds
motorisation points to the targets, it does not take dumps away. Option 2
turns a 10 % result into nothing on any hex with fewer than ten units, and
47.65's rounding is written for a convoy's cargo, not a dump.

The per-unit rule follows the printed results: 41.35 and 40.64 destroy a
percentage of every kind of supply in the dump, and a supply unit holds two
kinds. Rounding up matches R-149 and keeps a hit on a nearly empty unit
from doing nothing; a unit left empty is removed (32.11), and a depot left
with 60 fuel or less becomes a regular unit (47.14). A B-SD mission names a
hex, and in the full game a hex holds one dump, so all the supply units
there are that dump; strafing is per aircraft (40.53), so the attacker
shares his aircraft among them. 54.12 reveals a dummy dump that is bombed
or strafed, and a dummy supply unit is a dummy dump's stand-in.

For trucks, §32 attaches motorisation points either to a combat unit (to
motorise it) or to a supply unit (to move it, 32.32), and lets them be
detached. Points riding with a combat unit are what the full game calls
first-line trucks; everything else is a truck convoy, the target 41.32
and 40.62 call trucks in convoy, including the "unattached trucks" of
41.35. A truck loss spread "over cargoes" (41.32, 40.62) has no cargo to
spread over: a supply unit's contents are on its own log, not per point,
so the loss takes points only, and a supply unit left with fewer than it
needs to move (30 regular, 6 dummy, 50 depot) stays put until made up in an
organisation phase (32.32, 47.31). A combat unit left with fewer points
than its TOE strength is no longer motorised ([R-123](R-123.md)), and the
trucks 40.61 loses with strafed infantry are one motorisation point per
TOE strength point lost, R-123's rate. This keeps every printed restriction
(D capability for first-line trucks, the major-city limits, the column
shift) and needs no new state.
