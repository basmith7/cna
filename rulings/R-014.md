# R-014 — What may capture a destroyed-tank marker

## Problem

14.52 gives the capture decision to an enemy *player* who enters a hex with a
destroyed-tank marker and no friendly combat unit. Players do not enter
hexes; units do, and the case does not say what kind. A truck convoy, a
lone engineer company or a patrol could be argued to qualify. The sister
rule for broken-down vehicles (21.52) is explicit: it takes a combat unit of
at least battalion size. NJHarman:[^nj-com]

> A battalion sized (1 SP of units) or larger combat unit is required to
> capture Destroyed Tanks [14.52]. *Rule says "Player enters a hex", Players
> aren't units in the game! Similar to Broken Down vehicle capture[21.52].*

[^nj-com]: NJHarman, *CfNA House Rules & Interpretations*, § Combat,
  <https://friendorfoe.com/war/cfna/houserules/#combat>, CC-BY-SA 4.0
  (tagged CLARIFICATION). The quoted item is the third CLARIFICATION under
  that heading. See `ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## Options

1. **A combat unit of battalion size or larger, as for broken-down
   vehicles** (NJHarman). Consequence: the two capture rules use one
   standard; recce companies, trucks and engineers alone cannot claim tanks.
2. **Any combat unit.** Consequence: matches the "no other friendly combat
   units in the hex" condition on the other side — if a company can hold
   the marker, a company can take it — but diverges from 21.52.
3. **Any unit at all.** Consequence: literal reading of "player"; a supply
   column could capture a tank park, which no other capture rule allows.

## Decision

Option 1. Only a combat unit of battalion size or larger that enters the hex can capture a destroyed-tank marker, the same standard 21.52 sets for broken-down vehicles.

## Rationale

Option 1 or 2; option 3 has nothing behind it but the loose word. The
case's own defensive condition speaks of *combat* units, so the entering
unit should be one too. Whether it must also be battalion-sized turns on
whether 21.52 is a precedent or a deliberate difference; 21.52's explicit
size threshold, written for the closely analogous situation, is the best
evidence of the designers' intent and gives an engine one rule to implement.
The restated text presently says "enemy units enter" without a size
([Combat — Capturing destroyed tanks](../rules/60-combat.md#capturing-destroyed-tanks)).
The printed text names no unit type, so neither 1 nor 2 is a literal
reading; 1 is chosen because it matches the one explicit analogue and lets an
engine share a single captor test between the two capture rules. The two
rules still differ in how capture happens (entering the hex here, moving
adjacent there). No engine probe covers this ruling.

Decided under Brian's delegation of 2026-09-26; open to dispute like any
ruling (see [rulings/README.md](README.md)).
