# R-097 — Axis convoys before the first Operations Stage of Group One

## Problem

Group One starts in Game-Turn 1, OpStage 1, so the turn's opening stages
(convoy planning and resolution among them) are not played. 60.37 has the
Italian player plan convoys for the rest of September 1940 before play,
but does not say when the first of them arrives, or whether the
Commonwealth may attack them in Game-Turn 1. NJHarman:[^nj]

> CLARIFICATION  Prior to OpStage I, Axis player must plan Naval Convoy’s for
> GT1 and GT2. The first stuff will unload in OpStage I.
> Yes, allies are denied Naval Convoy Bombing on GT1. Such are the effects of
> “surprise invasion”.

[^nj]: NJHarman, *CfNA House Rules & Interpretations*, § Scenario Notes, 64.0 The Campaign For North Africa,
  <https://friendorfoe.com/war/cfna/houserules/#the-campaign-for-north-africa>, CC-BY-SA 4.0 (tagged CLARIFICATION). See `ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## Options

1. **As NJHarman.** Plan Game-Turns 1 and 2 before play; the Game-Turn 1
   convoy unloads in OpStage 1 and cannot be bombed.
2. **Next turn.** Plan before play, but the first convoy arrives with
   Game-Turn 2's convoy phase, attacked normally.

## Decision

Option 1. The convoys planned before play for Game-Turns 1 and 2 sail as planned; the Game-Turn 1 convoy unloads in OpStage 1 and cannot be bombed.

## Rationale

60.37 has the Italian player plan convoys "for the rest of September" before
play, which only makes sense if some of them sail in the opening Game-Turn;
holding the first one back to Game-Turn 2 (option 2) would leave that
planning with nothing to do. The scenario starts at OpStage 1, after the
turn's naval convoy bombing phase, so the Game-Turn 1 convoy has already
run the gauntlet when play begins: no bombing follows from the sequence
itself, not from a special exception. This is also NJHarman's reading.

Decided under Brian's delegation of 2026-09-26; open to dispute and
