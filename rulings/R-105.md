# R-105 — A counter placed twice in the Crusader set-up

## Problem

The Crusader set-up detaches the 2nd South African Division's armoured
cars and places them with another group, and also places the 7th South
African reconnaissance battalion at Giarabub. The OA sheets have one
counter that fits both entries.

## Options

1. **Giarabub.** The counter starts at Giarabub; the other entry is the
   same unit listed where it was detached from.
2. **With the group.** The counter starts with the group; the Giarabub
   entry is a slip.
