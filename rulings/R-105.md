# R-105 — A counter placed twice in the Crusader set-up

## Problem

The Crusader set-up detaches the 2nd South African Division's armoured
cars and places them with another group, and also places the 7th South
African reconnaissance battalion at Giarabub. The OA sheets have one
counter that fits both entries.

## Options

1. **Giarabub.** The counter starts at Giarabub; the other entry is the
   same unit listed where it was detached from.
2. **With the group.** The counter starts with the group; the Giarabub
   entry is a slip.

## Decision

Option 1. The 7th South African armoured cars (the 7th SA Recce Bn counter) start at Giarabub; the division's "Det" entry only says they are not with it.

## Rationale

By 59.2 a unit marked *Det* belongs to the sheet but starts elsewhere and is
listed again where it starts, so the two entries are one placement and its
cross-reference, not two placements. The only place the set-up lists it
again is Giarabub, which is also where the 7th SA reconnaissance battalion
historically screened the southern flank in November 1941. Option 2 would
need a second listing that does not exist. The data already places it at
Giarabub only; the counter identification ("7 SA Armored Car" as the 7th SA
Recce Bn, the only such counter) stands as read.

Decided under Brian's delegation of 2026-09-26; open to dispute and
supersession like any ruling (see [rulings/README.md](README.md)).
