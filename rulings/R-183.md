# R-183 — What The Last Chance's tactical count of TOE points includes

## Problem

63.91 B: when the Axis has not reached Alexandria, the Axis totals its TOE
strength points "of any type" on map E and the Commonwealth its TOE points
on map D, Layforce and the SAS left out, and the larger total wins a
tactical victory. The case does not say whether an HQ's own TOE points
count, whether gun points count beside infantry and armour, whether
broken-down tank points waiting for repair count, or what equal totals
give. cna-engine asked for the count as data it can run.

## Options

1. **Every unit's TOE, nothing loose.** Count every TOE strength point of
   every friendly land unit in a hex of the named sheet at the end:
   infantry, armour, guns of every kind, and an HQ's own points alike.
   Broken-down tank points, which belong to no unit until repaired, do not
   count. Layforce ("A" Battalion, Special Service Brigade) and the SAS
   Brigade are left out. Equal totals give no victory.
2. **Broken-down points too.** As option 1, but tank points lying broken
   down on the sheet count for the side that owns them.
3. **Combat points only.** As option 1, but only infantry and armour
   points count; guns and HQs do not.

## Decision

Option 1. Each side counts every TOE strength point of its land units on the enemy's sheet at the end (Axis on E, Commonwealth on D, Layforce and the SAS excepted), guns and HQs included and broken-down points not; the larger total wins a tactical victory and equal totals are no victory.

## Rationale

"Any type" rules out option 3: the printed count takes every kind of TOE
point, and an HQ holding attached guns (70-organisation) holds TOE points
like any other unit. A broken-down tank point is not strength until it is
repaired: it sits on the map unassigned, cannot move or fight, and the
printed count is of strength a side has forward, so option 2 would reward a
side for the wrecks it left behind. "The player with the most TOE points"
names no winner when the totals are equal, so equal totals give no
victory, as a zero margin does in Operation Crusader (62.8). Layforce is
our `unit:cw:a` and the SAS Brigade `unit:cw:sas` (the two units 20.3's
commando replacement row names). The comparison applies only when the Axis
strategic level is not reached.

In the data, `victory.toe_on_sheet` of `scenario:the-last-chance` carries
the sheets, the excluded units, the three inclusions and the tie, so another
reading is one edit.
