# R-062 — Where must water be for a unit to count as watered?

## Problem

52.41–52.42 say how much water each unit needs per Operations Stage, and
52.5 says what happens to a unit "without water", but §52 never says where
the water has to be, or when in the stage it is spent. Fuel has an explicit
rule (49.15–49.16: it must be in the same hex as the unit using it, drawn at
the start of the Movement Segment). Water has none: a unit might be watered
from its own carried stock, from a dump or truck in its hex, from a well it
stands on, or — on a looser reading — from any friendly source within
reach, as stores are at 51.1.

## Options

1. **Same hex, as for fuel.** The water must be in the unit's hex (carried,
   on first-line trucks, in a dump, or drawn from a well there) at some
   point in the stage, and is deducted then. Consequence: consistent with
   49.15; forces real water logistics, which is the purpose of the section.
2. **Same hex, checked at a fixed moment** (for example the end of the
   Operations Stage). Consequence: the same as option 1 but removes the
   question of mid-stage timing; a unit that moves away from its water
   before the check goes without.
3. **Any friendly source the unit could reach**, by analogy with stores.
   Consequence: much lighter book-keeping, but water shortage then almost
   never happens, which makes 52.5 nearly dead text.

## Decision

None yet — proposed.

## Rationale

Option 1 follows the one explicit model the Logistics Game gives for a
consumed commodity (fuel) and keeps the effects of 52.5 meaningful. Option 2
is option 1 with a timing rule added and may be worth adopting for play
convenience. Option 3 contradicts the design note that water is "a
nuisance" players must attend to.
