# R-073 — Are the Tunis/Tripoli boxes supply dumps?

## Problem

The Supply Dump Capacity Chart (54.13; headed 54.12 on the chart sheet)
gives the Tunis/Tripoli row unlimited capacity for every supply type, and
its key says each of the four Axis off-map boxes may hold that much. The
same key then says "the Tunis/Tripoli/In Transit boxes may not be used as
supply dumps".

## Options

1. **The boxes hold unlimited supplies but are not dumps.** Supplies may
   sit there without a dump counter and without the non-dump limits; dump
   rules (blowing, dummies, dump construction) do not apply there.
   Consequence: both sentences of the chart are kept; the row is a storage
   allowance, not a dump.
2. **Only the In Transit box is barred; Tunis/Tripoli boxes are ordinary
   unlimited dumps.** Consequence: the Axis may build, blow and fake dumps
   in the off-map boxes; the key's list is read as a misprint.

## Decision

Option 1. The Tunis/Tripoli boxes hold unlimited supplies but are not dumps, and the In Transit box is not a dump either.

## Rationale

The chart key says two things: each Axis off-map box may hold unlimited
supplies, and the Tunis/Tripoli/In Transit boxes may not serve as dumps.
Both can be true at once if the Tunis/Tripoli row is read as a storage
allowance rather than a dump; that keeps every printed word and needs no
misprint. Supplies in those boxes need no dump counter and are free of the
no-dump hex limits, but the dump rules (building, blowing, dummies,
capture as a dump) do not reach them, which also suits boxes that are off
the map and cannot be attacked. Option 2 has to call the key's list a
misprint with nothing to show it. The as-printed chart cells are kept; the
note in `data/tables/supply-dump-capacity.json` now records this reading.
See [Trucks and dumps](../rules/logistics/40-trucks-and-dumps.md).

supersession like any ruling (see [rulings/README.md](README.md)).
