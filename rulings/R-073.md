# R-073 — Are the Tunis/Tripoli boxes supply dumps?

## Problem

The Supply Dump Capacity Chart (54.13; headed 54.12 on the chart sheet)
gives the Tunis/Tripoli row unlimited capacity for every supply type, and
its key says each of the four Axis off-map boxes may hold that much. The
same key then says "the Tunis/Tripoli/In Transit boxes may not be used as
supply dumps".

## Options

1. **The boxes hold unlimited supplies but are not dumps.** Supplies may
   sit there without a dump counter and without the non-dump limits; dump
   rules (blowing, dummies, dump construction) do not apply there.
   Consequence: both sentences of the chart are kept; the row is a storage
   allowance, not a dump.
2. **Only the In Transit box is barred; Tunis/Tripoli boxes are ordinary
   unlimited dumps.** Consequence: the Axis may build, blow and fake dumps
   in the off-map boxes; the key's list is read as a misprint.

## Decision

Open.
