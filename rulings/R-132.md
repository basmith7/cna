# R-132 — The Axis prisoner departure point: the Tripolitania box

## Problem

Case 28.16 sends Axis-held prisoners to a departure point it calls "the
Sirte box on game-map A", where they leave play. Neither scan of the map,
the VASSAL module's map, nor the off-map rules (8.8x, the 8.89 chart) has
a Sirte box: the boxes off map A's west edge are Tripolitania, Tripoli,
Gabes and Tunis. Where do Axis prisoners leave play? cna-engine asked, and
meanwhile uses hex A2802, the hex units leave by for Tripolitania.

## Options

1. **The Tripolitania box.** Prisoners leave play on entering it, by the
   ordinary exit from A2802 (8.85).
2. **Hex A2802.** Prisoners leave play on reaching the exit hex itself,
   without entering a box.
3. **The Tripoli box.** The nearest box with a port, where prisoners would
   historically have been shipped from.

## Decision

Option 1. The Axis departure point is the Tripolitania box: Prisoner
Points leave play for good as soon as they enter it, by the same exit from
A2802 that land units use.

## Rationale

Sirte lies on the coast road west of the map's edge, inside the region the
Tripolitania box stands for, and that box is the first one off map A, as
28.16's "on game-map A" suggests; the printed name matches nothing the
game provides, and Tripolitania is the box it can mean. Option 1 keeps
the printed meaning (a box beside map A, reached by leaving the map) with
the box that exists. Option 2 would make the departure point a map hex,
which 28.16 does not say, and spare the captor the off-map stages (CPA 8
is the chart's slowest band). Option 3 sends prisoners one box farther than the rule asks. For an
engine: prisoners use the off-map movement rules like any Axis land unit,
and are removed when they enter the Tripolitania box.

Decided under Brian's delegation of 2026-09-26; open to dispute and
supersession like any ruling (see [rulings/README.md](README.md)).
