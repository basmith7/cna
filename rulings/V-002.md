# V-002 — Fuel in vehicles' tanks does not evaporate

## What the printed rules say

Case 49.3 reduces every fuel holding on the map by a fixed percentage each
Game-Turn (more in hot weather, and more for the Commonwealth until the
jerry can arrives), wherever the fuel is kept. The restated rule is in the
Logistics Game's fuel file (`rules/logistics/10-fuel.md`).

## The change

NJHarman's house rule:[^nj]

> Fuel in vehicle “fuel tanks” is not subject to Evaporation.
> A rule that may be implicit but never stated. Either way this is a massive
> tracking effort reduction win.

[^nj]: NJHarman, *CfNA House Rules & Interpretations*, § Leaky Gas Tanks,
  <https://friendorfoe.com/war/cfna/houserules/#leaky-gas-tanks>, CC-BY-SA 4.0 (tagged CLARIFICATION/CHANGE?). See `ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## Effect on play

- Only fuel in dumps, trucks, trains and ships evaporates; fuel already
  issued to a unit's vehicles is exempt.
- Far less bookkeeping each Stores Expenditure Stage, and a real incentive to
  keep formations fuelled up rather than holding fuel in dumps.

## Why it is recorded as a variant

The printed case applies evaporation to fuel wherever it is kept, and the
author himself tags the item as a possible change. Exempting fuel in tanks
alters a printed rate's reach rather than resolving an ambiguity, so under
the [rulings policy](README.md) it is a variant.
