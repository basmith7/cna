# R-024 — Morale Modifier Table, cohesion −4: reading 56

## Problem

On the Morale Modifier Table (17.4) the −4 cohesion row prints its −2 cell
as 42–55 and its −3 cell as 61–66, so a reading of 56 has no printed
result. Both scans agree and the September 1979 errata is silent. The gap
is declared in `known_gaps` of `data/tables/morale-modifier.json`. The
cna-engine probe finds that 2.8 % of the rolls on this row read 56, so it
comes up in play.

## Options

1. **The cell to the left: −2.** Treat the −2 cell as 42–56. Consequence:
   the cell ends on a 6, the way the −2 cells of the −5 row (46) and the −6
   row (36) do.
2. **The cell to the right: −3.** Treat the −3 cell as 56–66. Consequence:
   the −3 cell starts on a 6, which no −3 cell nearby does (−5 row: 51,
   −6 row: 41).
3. **No change (0).** A 56 leaves basic morale alone. Consequence: a high
   roll, which elsewhere on the row is worse for the unit, becomes the
   best result on the row after 11–33.

## Decision

Option 1. At cohesion −4, a reading of 56 gives a −2 morale modifier (the −2 cell reads 42–56).

## Rationale

No printed text settles it, so the choice rests on the table's own shape.
Along a row the modifier worsens as the reading rises, so option 3 breaks
the row. Down the −2 column the cell's end moves one step of ten per row:
36 at −6, 46 at −5, so 56 at −4; and the −3 cell's start moves the same
way, 41 at −6, 51 at −5, 61 at −4. The printed 61 fits that pattern and the
printed 55 does not, so the misprint is the −2 cell's end, and the gap
belongs to −2. The probe
([`probes/chart-oddities.json`](https://github.com/basmith7/cna-engine/blob/15a12384f3bd7aca3f815ad4f39ef8d5a52ecca2/probes/chart-oddities.json))
shows one roll in 36 on this row lands on the gap. The table data stays as
printed, with the gap in `known_gaps`; an engine applies this ruling on
top, as it applies errata.

Decided under Brian's delegation of 2026-09-26; open to dispute and
supersession like any ruling (see [rulings/README.md](README.md)).
