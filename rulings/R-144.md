# R-144 — What the Commonwealth learns of a bombed Axis convoy

## Problem

Under abstract supply the Axis names the lanes it is using and the
Commonwealth attacks each one (32.63); the result is a
percentage of the lane's supply units, motorisation points and replacement
points, with at least one supply unit lost on a 20 % or worse result
(32.64–32.65). Nothing says whether the Commonwealth then learns how many
supply units sailed, or how many sank. In the Logistics Game the lane's
flak is its tonnage over 1,000 (56.16), fired at the bombers as a total.
cna-engine asked which reading to enforce.

## Options

1. **Lanes, roll and percentage only.** The Commonwealth learns the lanes
   in use (32.63), its own bomb points, the dice and the percentage result,
   and in the Logistics Game the convoy's flak total as it is fired. The
   cargo, and what the percentage destroyed, stay with the Axis player.
2. **Counts sunk.** As 1, and the Axis player announces how many supply
   units (and points) were destroyed.
3. **Full manifest.** The Axis player shows what the lane carried, so the
   Commonwealth can check the arithmetic.

## Decision

Option 1. The Commonwealth learns the lanes, the roll and the percentage,
never what sailed or what sank.

## Rationale

32.63 grants the lanes and nothing else, and 3.62 keeps replacement-point
numbers secret. Supply units reach the map with dummies, counted from the
real units that arrive ([R-128](R-128.md)); telling the Commonwealth how
many real units sailed and sank would hand it the count the dummies exist
to hide. The flak total is a total, which 3.6 says is revealed in combat;
it hints at tonnage, as it would at a table. Options 2 and 3 add grants no
rule makes.

Decided under Brian's delegation of 2026-09-26; open to dispute and
supersession like any ruling (see [rulings/README.md](README.md)).
