# R-104 — The Desert Fox campaign scenario

## Problem

61.1 offers a second scenario: a full campaign from the Rommel's Arrival
set-up. Section 61 gives it no end, initiative rule or victory conditions.

## Options

1. **Campaign rules from Group Five.** Play from the Group Two set-up to the
   campaign game's end, judged by the campaign game's victory points (64.7).
2. **Open-ended.** Players agree an end; no victory conditions.
