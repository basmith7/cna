# R-104 — The Desert Fox campaign scenario

## Problem

61.1 offers a second scenario: a full campaign from the Rommel's Arrival
set-up. Section 61 gives it no end, initiative rule or victory conditions.

## Options

1. **Campaign rules from Group Five.** Play from the Group Two set-up to the
   campaign game's end, judged by the campaign game's victory points (64.7).
2. **Open-ended.** Players agree an end; no victory conditions.

## Decision

Option 1. The Desert Fox campaign runs from the Rommel's Arrival set-up to the campaign game's end (Game-Turn 111) and is judged by the campaign game's victory rules (64.7), with initiative as Rommel's Arrival sets it and by the normal rules after.

## Rationale

61.1 calls it a full campaign from the Group Two set-up, so the rules SPI
wrote for full campaigns are the natural fill: Group Five's end and victory
points, which already score every place a campaign from 1941 can reach. An
open-ended game (option 2) gives an engine nothing to decide a winner with.
Starting later than the campaign game changes nothing in 64.7: its places,
replacement and withdrawal points all count from whatever the game holds at
the end. Any open point in 64.7 itself is R-109's. The data now carries the
campaign game's victory block.

Decided under Brian's delegation of 2026-09-26; open to dispute and
