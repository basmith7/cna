# R-103 — The 3rd Armoured Brigade's tanks in Group Two

## Problem

61.32 gives tank points to the 3rd Hussars, to 5 RTR and to 6 RTR of the
3rd Armoured Brigade. The set-up (61.31) places the brigade with 6 RTR and
the 3rd Hussars assigned, but never places 5 RTR, whose OA sheet puts it in
that brigade. 61.38's list of the 7th Armoured Division also names an
"ARTR" that matches no counter.

## Options

1. **5 RTR is with its brigade.** It starts at A2022 with the brigade and
   the tanks 61.32 gives it; the "ARTR" is a misprint with no effect.
2. **5 RTR is not in play.** Its tanks go to the brigade's other
   battalions or are lost.
