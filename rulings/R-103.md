# R-103 — The 3rd Armoured Brigade's tanks in Group Two

## Problem

61.32 gives tank points to the 3rd Hussars, to 5 RTR and to 6 RTR of the
3rd Armoured Brigade. The set-up (61.31) places the brigade with 6 RTR and
the 3rd Hussars assigned, but never places 5 RTR, whose OA sheet puts it in
that brigade. 61.38's list of the 7th Armoured Division also names an
"ARTR" that matches no counter.

## Options

1. **5 RTR is with its brigade.** It starts at A2022 with the brigade and
   the tanks 61.32 gives it; the "ARTR" is a misprint with no effect.
2. **5 RTR is not in play.** Its tanks go to the brigade's other
   battalions or are lost.

## Decision

Option 1. 5 RTR starts at A2022 assigned to the 3rd Armoured Brigade with the tanks 61.32 gives it (1 point of A9, 4 of A13); the "ARTR" in 61.38 is a misprint with no effect.

## Rationale

61.32 gives 5 RTR tank strengths, which only matter if it is in play; its
OA sheet puts it in the 3rd Armoured Brigade, which 61.31 places at A2022.
The set-up line simply omits one of three battalions whose strengths the
very next case lists, and dropping it (option 2) would throw away printed
figures to explain a missing name, the same reasoning as R-106. In 61.38
"ARTR" stands where a tank regiment's abbreviation belongs and names no
counter; the 7th Armoured Division's composition does not depend on it.
The data now assigns 5 RTR with the brigade.
