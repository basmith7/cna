# R-053 — Prisoner stores: per stage within a per-turn distribution

## Problem

The general rule of §51 hands out stores once, at the start of the
Game-Turn. Case 51.12 charges prisoners one Stores Point per five Prisoner
Points in every Operations Stage, fed before anyone else, while 51.17
charges guards per Game-Turn but lets them draw from the nearest dump as
prisoners do. It is not said when in each stage the prisoner charge is
paid, or whether a group of fewer than five Prisoner Points costs anything.

## Options

1. **Pay at the start of each stage, part-groups round up.** The charge
   falls with the stores distribution in the first stage and at the start
   of the second and third; a part-group counts as a full five.
   Consequence: simple and literal; prisoners are charged three times a
   turn, at three bookkeeping moments.
2. **Pay once per turn for all three stages.** Charge three stages' worth
   at the Game-Turn distribution, rounding the total up. Consequence: one
   bookkeeping moment per turn, in line with the §51 general rule;
   slightly cheaper for small groups.

Open.
