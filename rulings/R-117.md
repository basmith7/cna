# R-117 — What a swamp hex costs a unit that enters it by railroad

## Problem

The Terrain Effects Chart (8.37) gives swamp no CP cost: its row reads "May
enter only on road or railroad". It gives the railroad no rate either: the
Railroad row reads "Same as terrain in hex for all purposes". A unit entering
a swamp hex along a railroad therefore finds the permission but no price on
either row. A road does have a rate (1 CP non-motorised, ½ CP motorised,
breakdown ½), and notes 10 and 11 say a railroad is "treated as a road" for
two purposes only (crossing a wadi in a rainstorm, crossing a major river).
Case 8.33 grants the road and track rate to units moving from road/track hex
to road/track hex, and 8.47 says nothing more about railroads in movement.
The map has railroad through swamp hexes (E3211, E3312, E3412, E3413 and
E3414 on map E), so the case arises in play.

## Options

1. **The road rate.** The railroad is the road for the purpose of the swamp
   hex: 1 CP non-motorised, ½ CP motorised, ½ breakdown.
2. **The clear rate.** The swamp hex is priced as the cheapest ordinary
   ground, clear: 2 CP either way, breakdown 4.
3. **Free.** The railroad hex costs nothing, as rail movement (8.7) does.

## Decision

Option 1. A unit moving from a constructed railroad hex to an adjacent
constructed railroad hex across a railroad hexside, into a swamp hex, pays
the road rate for the hex: 1 CP non-motorised, ½ CP motorised, breakdown ½.

## Rationale

The chart lets a unit into swamp by "road or railroad" in one breath, as two
equal keys to the same door, and gives only the road a price; pricing the
railroad as the road is the only reading that uses a figure the chart
prints. Notes 10 and 11 already treat a railroad as a road where the chart
needs a figure for it. Option 2 invents a rate for swamp that the chart
withholds and would make the rail line through a swamp dearer than a road
through the same hex for no printed reason. Option 3 confuses this with
Commonwealth rail movement (8.7), which is a separate, CP-free move that
needs no hex price at all. The Railroad row's "same as terrain in hex"
still governs every other terrain: a railroad through clear costs 2. The
ruling prices the swamp hex only; hexside features on the same step are
charged as for any unit that is not on a road (the railroad cancels none of
them, apart from what notes 10 and 11 print), and the railroad must be
constructed (8.47 ignores an unconstructed railroad, so swamp stays closed
there unless a road runs through it). For an engine: the number the engine
already charged, with the entry condition above.
