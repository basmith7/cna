# R-185 — The Italian Campaign's levels and its cut-off test

## Problem

60.82 scores the Italian Campaign in points, "most points wins", and calls
every result a plain "Victory" except one: if no Italian combat unit on the
map can trace a supply line, the Commonwealth wins a strategic victory. It
names no level for the plain victory, nothing for equal totals, no moment
for the cut-off test, and its "supply line" is a Logistics Game idea (a
trace to a dump or city linked back to Tripoli). cna-engine plays §32 and
asked for the level and the test as data.

## Options

For the plain victory:

1. **Tactical**, the lowest level, as unnamed victories are elsewhere
   (Rommel's Arrival, R-184); equal totals are a draw.
2. **Decisive**, between the plain win and the strategic cut-off.

For the cut-off test, judged at the end of the scenario:

1. **R-110's route.** An Italian combat unit is supplied if a medium truck
   could travel, any distance, from its hex to Nofilia (A2703), the map end
   of the off-map line to Tripoli (8.89), without entering a hex holding an enemy
   combat unit or an enemy ZOC with no friendly combat unit in it (R-119).
   No Commonwealth unit may enter the boxes, so Nofilia stands for Tripoli.
2. **32.16's draw.** An Italian combat unit is supplied if it is within
   half its CPA of a friendly supply unit.
3. **64.73's end-of-game stocks test**, as the campaign game uses.

## Decision

Option 1 for both. The plain victory is tactical and equal totals a draw; at the end, if no Italian combat unit on the map has an R-110 route to Nofilia, the Commonwealth wins a strategic victory whatever the points.

## Rationale

The plain victory is the only win the points give, and recording an
unnamed win as the lowest level keeps one convention across the
scenarios (R-184). A "supply line" traced "back to Tripoli" is a line of
communication, the same kind of test as Graziani's convoy line in the same
scenario group, and R-110 already turned that into a route of any length
for §32 play; using it here keeps one tracing rule. 32.16's draw tests a
unit's local stocks, not a line home, and 64.73 belongs to the campaign
game and tests stores and water §32 does not keep. The text has no timing,
and victory conditions are read at the end; 64.72's every-stage test is
the campaign game's and is not borrowed.

In the data `victory.cut_off` carries the side tested, the winner, the
level and the trace; the margins are `{min 1: tactical}`, `{min 0: draw}`.
