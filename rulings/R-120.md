# R-120 — Abstract supply: a unit that cannot pay its fuel or ammunition

## Problem

Under abstract supply (§32) a unit pays its fuel and ammunition from a
friendly supply unit within half its CPA. Section 32 says what each move
and each combat costs, but not what happens when no supply unit in range
holds enough. Can a fuel-using unit with no fuel move at all: by land, by
rail or sea, reacting, retreating before assault, or in a forced retreat?
Does a defender with no ammunition fight at full strength, at reduced
strength, or not at all? cna-engine refuses the land move and the attack
and lets the defence stand until this is ruled.

## Options

1. **The land rules for "out of supply" apply.** A unit that cannot pay its
   fuel makes no move that costs fuel: no land movement, reaction or retreat
   before assault. Rail and sea moves cost no fuel and are allowed. A forced
   retreat it cannot make is paid for by the 10 % loss per hex not retreated.
   A unit that cannot pay its ammunition is out of ammunition: it cannot
   barrage, assault or defend, and a hex whose every defender is out of
   ammunition surrenders when assaulted.
2. **Full strength without supply.** The unit moves and fights as usual,
   and the missing points are simply not paid.
3. **Half strength.** A unit that cannot pay moves at half its CPA and
   fights at half its ratings.

## Decision

Option 1. Without the fuel it owes, a unit makes no move that costs fuel
(rail and sea moves cost none). A forced retreat it cannot make costs 10 %
per hex not retreated. Without the ammunition it owes, a unit is out of
ammunition: it cannot barrage, assault or defend, and if every defender in
an assaulted hex is out of ammunition, the hex surrenders.

## Rationale

32.23 makes fuel the price of a land move ("the required Fuel Points are
expended" the first time the unit moves in the stage), and 32.21 makes
ammunition the price of taking part in a combat. If the price cannot be
paid, the move or the combat cannot happen, which is how the Logistics Game
treats a dry tank (§49) or an empty hex (§50). The Land Game already says
what an unarmed defender does: a unit out of ammunition cannot assault or
defend, and a hex with only such units surrenders when assaulted (15.15,
[combat](../rules/60-combat.md)). Under §32 "out of ammunition" can only
mean "cannot pay 32.21's cost", so the rule applies as written. Retreat
before assault is ordinary movement paid in CP and fuel (13.2, R-010).
A forced retreat also pays CP "as for any move", and the Land Game already
prices a retreat a unit cannot make at 10 % per hex. Option 2 would make
§32's bookkeeping pointless, because a player could ignore his supply units.
Option 3 invents a rule that appears nowhere in the printed text. The other
Logistics Game penalties (R-050's loss of ZOC and surrender in an enemy ZOC)
belong to §50 and are not imported, because §32 replaces §50 rather than
adding to it. For an engine: before a land move, reaction or retreat before
assault, require the fuel from a supply unit in range, or refuse the move;
before a combat, charge each unit's ammunition, and mark any unit that
cannot pay as out of ammunition for that combat.
