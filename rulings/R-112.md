# R-112 — Refit additions: by the aircraft's nation or the SGSU's?

## Problem

38.35 adds 2 to the refit roll when the aircraft being refitted are
Italian and 1 when they are German, whichever player owns them. The
Aircraft Refit Table's own key words the same additions by the SGSU doing
the work: an Italian SGSU adds 2, a German one 1. The two differ whenever
an SGSU of one nation refits another nation's aircraft (a German SGSU on an
Italian airfield working on Italian fighters, or a captured aircraft).

## Options

1. **By the aircraft.** As 38.35: the aircraft's nation sets the addition.
2. **By the SGSU.** As the table's key: the nation of the SGSU doing the
   work sets it.
3. **Both.** Add for the aircraft and for the SGSU, cumulatively.

## Decision

Option 1. The aircraft's nation sets the addition (Italian +2, German +1), as 38.35 says, whatever SGSU does the work.

## Rationale

The rules text is explicit and the chart's key is a summary of it. In
ordinary play the two never differ: German and Italian aircraft never share
a squadron (35.28), and the +1 for another squadron's SGSU already covers
the cost of being serviced away from home. They part company only when a
German SGSU works on Italian aircraft or either refits captured ones, and
there the text's reading keeps the penalty with the aircraft type, which
is what the nation figures stand for (spares and serviceability). Option 3
would double-count a single cause. Reading by the aircraft also needs no
lookup of the working SGSU's nation, so it is simpler for an engine.

Decided under Brian's delegation of 2026-09-26; open to dispute and
supersession like any ruling (see [rulings/README.md](README.md)).
