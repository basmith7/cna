# R-102 — Group Two abstractions: references and motorisation points

## Problem

61.72 says its supply set-ups replace the cases for Axis and Commonwealth
trucks (61.43, 61.35), though their content is supply (61.44, 61.36). 61.73
points to a case 61.62 that does not exist, and, unlike 61.72, gives no
starting unattached motorisation points when only the Logistics Game is
left out.

## Options

1. **Read the supply cases, and carry 61.72's motorisation over.** The
   replacements apply to 61.44 and 61.36; 61.73's reference is to 61.72;
   61.73 games start with 61.72 B's motorisation points.
2. **Supply cases, motorisation from 59.63.** As option 1, but count the
   motorisation points from the listed trucks by 59.63 (medium and heavy
   trucks, air-facility trucks included since the Air Game is played).

## Decision

Option 2. The references are read as the supply cases (61.44, 61.36) and 61.72; with the Air Game played and the Logistics Game left out, starting motorisation points are counted from the listed trucks by 59.63, air-facility trucks included.

## Rationale

59.63 is the general rule for initial motorisation points and applies
"unless the scenario's own instructions differ"; 61.73 gives none, so the
general rule is what the printed text leaves in force. 61.72's figures are
for the Land Game alone, where 61.71 has already cut the trucks for the
missing Air Game; carrying them into a game that plays the Air Game (option
1) would start both sides short by the trucks the Air Game keeps. The
reference corrections are the same in both options: the cases named hold
trucks, the cases meant hold supply.
