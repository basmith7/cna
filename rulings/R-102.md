# R-102 — Group Two abstractions: references and motorisation points

## Problem

61.72 says its supply set-ups replace the cases for Axis and Commonwealth
trucks (61.43, 61.35), though their content is supply (61.44, 61.36). 61.73
points to a case 61.62 that does not exist, and, unlike 61.72, gives no
starting unattached motorisation points when only the Logistics Game is
left out.

## Options

1. **Read the supply cases, and carry 61.72's motorisation over.** The
   replacements apply to 61.44 and 61.36; 61.73's reference is to 61.72;
   61.73 games start with 61.72 B's motorisation points.
2. **Supply cases, motorisation from 59.63.** As option 1, but count the
   motorisation points from the listed trucks by 59.63 (medium and heavy
   trucks, air-facility trucks included since the Air Game is played).
