# R-165 — What Malta's starting capacity counts

## Problem

44.13 leaves each Maltese base's starting capacity to the scenario, and
44.14 measures that capacity in levels: a level serves up to 18 aircraft
of any type, and Malta uses no SGSUs. The scenarios state it three ways.
Graziani's Offensive gives the bases "five SGSUs" between them and lists
an SGSU count beside each group of planes, two for the 15 Hurricanes and
one each for the rest, five in all (60.46). Rommel's Arrival gives a
"total air facility capacity of 8" to divide (61.34). Crusader gives "14
squadrons" (62.36). The Last Chance has every base at full capacity
(63.37). Are these the same unit, and what are Graziani's SGSUs?

## Options

1. **All are levels.** Each figure is a total of 44.14 levels the
   Commonwealth divides among the Maltese facilities, none above its
   standard level. Graziani's SGSUs and Crusader's squadrons are the
   levels, under the squadron grouping 44.14 asks for; Graziani's
   per-group SGSU column says how many levels each group fills. No SGSU
   counters go to Malta.
2. **Graziani's SGSUs are real.** Five SGSU counters start on Malta, and
   the capacity follows from them. This contradicts 44.14 outright, and
   leaves Graziani's Malta with no stated capacity at all.
3. **Squadrons are counted as on the mainland.** A level serves one
   squadron of whatever size, as on an African airfield (36.11), so
   Crusader's 14 squadrons can hold any number of planes. This drops
   44.14's 18-aircraft limit, the one rule that sets Malta's facilities
   apart.

## Decision

Option 1. Every scenario's Malta capacity is a total of levels of 18
aircraft to divide among the Maltese facilities; Graziani's SGSU figures
are those levels, and no SGSUs are used on Malta.

## Rationale

44.14 is the only rule that says what a Maltese level is, and it says
SGSUs are neither needed nor used there; the scenario wording varies
because the mainland vocabulary (an SGSU serves a squadron, a squadron
takes a level) was carried over to the island. The figures bear this
out. Graziani's SGSU column sums to its five, and each group fits its
share at 18 aircraft a level: 15 Hurricanes in two levels, 12 Swordfish
in one. Rommel's Arrival puts 55 planes in 8 levels (room for 144) and
Crusader 74 in 14 (room for 252). Option 2 contradicts 44.14 and option
3 discards it; option 1 keeps every printed number and needs one unit
for the engine. The Maltese facilities and their standard levels are in
`data/tables/fixed-air-facilities.json`; each scenario's total is
`sides.cw.air.malta.facility_levels` (`standard` for The Last Chance).
