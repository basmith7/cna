# V-023 — Strategic air stages merged with the convoy stage

## What the printed rules say

The Strategic Air Planning Stage and Naval Convoy Stage are separate, and strategic-mission aircraft stay out until the Strategic Air Recovery Stage at the end of the Game-Turn.

## The change

NJHarman's house rule:[^nj]

> Combine and reorder steps of II Strategic Air Planning Stage and III Naval
> Convoy Stage.
> Facilitate asynchronous internet play. Both sides can do more independently.
>
> Move Stores Expenditure to start of Game Turn.
> Facilitate asynchronous internet play. Both sides can do more independently.
>
> Move VIII Strategic Air Recovery Stage to end of combined II/III Stage.
> It’s confusing to leave strategic mission aircraft “out” through all the
> OpStages. In practice no one does that. Suspect designers meant to
> emphasize aircraft flown on strategic mission can’t also be flown during
> Land Support Air Phases.

[^nj]: NJHarman, *CfNA House Rules & Interpretations*, § Sequence of Play,
  <https://friendorfoe.com/war/cfna/houserules/#sequence-of-play>, CC-BY-SA 4.0 (tagged CHANGE). See `ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## Effect on play

Strategic air planning and convoy resolution become one stage, and strategic-mission aircraft return and are maintained at its end instead of at the end of the turn.

## Why it is recorded as a variant

It changes the printed sequence of play.
