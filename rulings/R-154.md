# R-154 — What leaves with a named withdrawal

## Problem

The Commonwealth schedule withdraws units by name, most of them division or
brigade headquarters (the 6th Australian Division HQ at Game-Turn 27
OpStage 2, for one). 20.81 does not say whether such a row takes the HQ
counter alone or the formation under it. The schedule's key prints "HQ" for
"the headquarters counter only" and "Less" for "without", and puts the
parent formation in brackets, but it does not say whose units a formation
is made of once play has moved them about (19.x assignment and
attachment). One row (Game-Turn 97 OpStage 2) also takes "any three
infantry battalions" with the 22nd Guards Brigade HQ.

## Options

1. **The OA sheet's tree.** A named counter takes every unit printed under
   it on its OA sheet that is in play, wherever it is now assigned or
   attached, less those the row lists after "Less"; a row printed "HQ" takes
   the counter alone. Units attached to it from other formations stay.
2. **The current chain of command.** A named counter takes whatever is
   assigned or attached to it at the moment of withdrawal.
3. **The HQ counter alone,** always.

## Decision

Option 1. A named withdrawal takes the counter and every unit under it on
its OA sheet still in play, except the row's `less` units; an `hq_only` unit
goes alone; a `pick` adds that many units of the named slot kind, chosen by
the Commonwealth player.

## Rationale

The key's "HQ" mark only makes sense if an unmarked HQ means more than the
counter, so Option 3 contradicts the chart. The schedule's "Less" lists name
exactly the brigades that withdrew earlier from the same sheet (the 6th
Australian Division less its 16th and 17th Brigades, which left at 26/1 and
28/2), and the 15/2 row says "Less (obviously) the 5th & 7th Bdes", so the
chart counts a formation by its OA sheet, which is also how a named arrival
and a scenario set-up read (59.2, R-100). Option 2 would let the player
empty a division before its date by reassigning its units, and an engine
would have to chase attachments; Option 1 is a fixed list. Units already
eliminated or withdrawn are simply not there. For the pick, the slot kinds of
[R-142](R-142.md) (`fills`) already say which counters are infantry
battalions; each chosen battalion meets 20.82 on its own
([R-156](R-156.md)). The rows carry `hq_only`, `less` and `pick` in
`data/tables/reinforcement-schedule.json`; returning rows carry the same
marks where the chart prints them.
