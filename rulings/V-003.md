# V-003 — No off-road breakdown penalty for light trucks

## What the printed rules say

By NJHarman's account, the Truck Characteristics Chart (54.2) carries a note
that light trucks suffer extra breakdown when they move off roads (the chart
itself is transcribed in Task 7 of the Logistics Game plan). The restated chart and rule
are in the Logistics Game's trucks file
(`rules/logistics/40-trucks-and-dumps.md`).

## The change

NJHarman's house rule:[^nj]

> Ignore [54.2] Truck Characteristics Chart’s note about light trucks
> earning extra breakdown if not moving along road.

[^nj]: NJHarman, *CfNA House Rules & Interpretations*, § Logistics,
  <https://friendorfoe.com/war/cfna/houserules/#logistics>, CC-BY-SA 4.0 (tagged CHANGE). See `ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## Effect on play

- Every truck class breaks down at the same rate off road.
- One fewer breakdown case to track per truck column.

## Why it is recorded as a variant

The chart's note is printed and unambiguous; the author doubts its intent
(he thinks heavy trucks were meant) but drops it for bookkeeping. That is a
change, recorded as a variant under the [rulings policy](README.md).
