# R-156 — The 75 % test for a withdrawing brigade or division

## Problem

20.82 and 20.85 require a withdrawing unit to be at 75 % of its maximum TOE
strength, brought up with replacements or replaced by a like unit at 75 %.
Most withdrawals are brigades or divisions ([R-154](R-154.md)), whose HQ has
a token TOE or none. Is the test made on the HQ, on each unit that goes, or
on the formation as a whole?

## Options

1. **The formation as a whole.** The TOE strength points of every unit that
   goes, added up, against the sum of their maxima; 75 % or more passes.
2. **Each unit that goes,** separately; one weak battalion fails the lot.
3. **The named counter only.**

## Decision

Option 1. A named withdrawal passes when the units that go together hold at
least 75 % of their combined maximum TOE strength points; a unit picked by
type (20.82) is tested on its own, and the strongest of its type serves when
none is at 75 %.

## Rationale

20.82 speaks of "the unit", and for a named division the unit is the
division: Option 1 reads it at the size the row names. Option 2 would
eliminate a whole division because one of its battalions lost a point in the
last stage, a punishment out of all proportion to the rule's stated purpose
of stopping procrastination (20.83). Option 3 tests a figure (an HQ's one
tank point, or nothing) that says nothing about the formation's state. Units
no longer in play count in neither sum. A substitute under 20.85 is a unit
of the same `fills` kind, or for a formation one whose HQ has the same
`organisation` row ([R-142](R-142.md), #253), at 75 % by the same test;
substitutes and picks are made by the Commonwealth player. A withdrawal
that fails at the check of [R-155](R-155.md) is eliminated, every unit in
it, and none of them returns (20.83).

Decided under Brian's delegation of 2026-09-26; open to dispute and
supersession like any ruling (see [rulings/README.md](README.md)).
