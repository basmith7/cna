# V-001 — The *San Giorgio* as a live gun battery

## What the printed rules say

Case 30.17 gives the Italians one ship, the *San Giorgio*, half-sunk in
Tobruk harbour. It acts as an immobile artillery unit until enemy engineers
remove or sink it; 30.39 lets it fire at Commonwealth ships as coastal guns
do. In the Logistics Game the wreck costs Tobruk three port efficiency
levels (55.25), and both campaign set-ups (60.7, 61.6) start Tobruk at
level 7 for that reason. The restated rule is in [Special rules — The
Mediterranean Fleet](../rules/90-special.md#the-mediterranean-fleet).

## The change

NJHarman's house rule, first proposed on BoardGameGeek and carried on his
house-rules page:[^nj-sg]

> [30.17] […] It has been parked in
> the harbor of Tobruk to serve as a stationary gun battery. It is immune to
> torpedoes, may Barrage adjacent hexes and may fire at Commonwealth ships in
> adjacent hexes through sea hexsides. If Tobruk is taken, the San Giorgio is
> scuttled in the harbor in the same OpStage and removed from the game. The
> ship may be undocked from the harbor, though it requires 3 Op-Stages to
> prepare and to take each action, instead of the usual 2, due to the Italian
> supply situation. If this happens, as the ship has been uninstalled from its
> fortified position, it loses its immunity to torpedoes and its AA rating
> drops from 6 to a 4.
>
> San Giorgio may not combine Barrage with any other unit.
>
> [55.25] is rescinded. San Giorgio never reduces efficiency whether afloat
> or scuttled.
>
> [60.7, 61.6] Remove references to Tobruk being below efficiency maximum.
> San Giorgio is in the harbor in both campaigns.

and his own addition:[^nj-sg]

> CW Fleet may attack San Giorgio. Only from same hex, only BB and CA. Any
> anti-ship fire of San Giorgio and coastal batteries is applied before
> calculating CW barrage strength.

[^nj-sg]: NJHarman, *CfNA House Rules & Interpretations*, § The San Giorgio,
  <https://friendorfoe.com/war/cfna/houserules/#the-san-giorgio>, CC-BY-SA 4.0
  (tagged CHANGE and CORRECTION; originally
  <https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2025665/proposal-rule-change-san-giorgio>).
  The elided opening sentence repeats the printed 30.17 wording and is not
  quoted here. See `ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## Effect on play

- The ship becomes a deliberate Italian asset rather than an obstacle: an
  emplaced battery with torpedo immunity, its own AA rating and a barrage
  into adjacent hexes, which the Commonwealth can only silence by taking
  Tobruk or (under the addition) by bringing battleships or heavy cruisers
  into its hex.
- Tobruk's port efficiency is no longer reduced by the wreck, which changes
  the Logistics Game's supply picture for whichever side holds the port.
- Sallying the ship is allowed but slow and exposed; the author's own view is
  that no one should.

## Why it is recorded as a variant

The first paragraph is a historical correction — the *San Giorgio* really was
an armoured cruiser moored at Tobruk as a floating battery — but the rule as
proposed changes what the counter does (torpedo immunity, an AA rating,
optional sortie, scuttling) and rescinds a Logistics Game case, rather than
resolving an ambiguity in the printed text. Under the [rulings
policy](README.md) a change of that kind is a variant, not a ruling: we
record it, cite it from the affected prose, and leave the printed rule in
place. It can be reopened as a ruling if the discussion concludes that 30.17
is a misprint rather than a design choice.
