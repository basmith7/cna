# V-008 — Mussolini's requirements

## What the printed rules say

The Italian player may conduct the 1940 campaign as he likes; only the
victory conditions judge him ([Group One](../rules/scenarios/10-the-italians.md#victory)).

## The change

NJHarman's house rule:[^nj]

> ADDITION   Ala OCS DAKII. With god’s eye view and hindsight we are way
> better operational commanders than the Italians. It’s too easy to craft
> opening moves that produce success outside historical reasonableness.

[^nj]: NJHarman, *CfNA House Rules & Interpretations*, § Mussolini Requirements,
  <https://friendorfoe.com/war/cfna/houserules/#mussolini-requirements>, CC-BY-SA 4.0 (tagged ADDITION). See `ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## Effect on play

The house rule obliges the Italians to commit set numbers of divisions and
other strength points to Egypt by early in Game-Turn 1, and some of them past
a line east of Sidi Barrani by Game-Turn 5, until the Commonwealth retakes
Sidi Barrani or enters Libya. It forces the historical advance rather than
an optimised one. The full lists are on his page.

## Why it is recorded as a variant

Nothing in the printed rules constrains Italian deployment this way.
