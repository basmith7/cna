# R-179 — Machine-gun and heavy-weapons units are known by their counter symbol

## Problem

The Maximum Attachment Chart's key (19.5) says its "infantry" limit covers
every infantry type except machine-gun and heavy-weapons units. The
Replacement Point Conversion Chart (20.3) prices those two types apart from
other infantry, and 15.4 names them among the units that support tanks in an
assault. Nothing in the data marked them: every one sits on an "Infantry"
row of the Unit Characteristics Chart (4.46), and a counter's `fills` names
the 19.3 slot it takes, not what it is (the German 2nd and 8th MG
battalions fill an infantry battalion slot). cna-engine asked for a
per-unit field before building the chart's exception.

Two sources could decide the type: the unit's name on the OA sheet, or the
symbol on its counter. They mostly agree, but not always: the Italian
Bersaglieri "Mitrg" battalions and companies are named as machine-gun units
and drawn as motorised (or motorcycle) infantry, and the Italian garrisons of
El Grein, Fort Maddalena, Mechili and Sceferzen and the Ramcke Brigade's
battalions carry the heavy-weapons symbol without the name.

## Options

1. **The counter symbol decides.** A unit is a machine-gun unit when its
   counter carries the machine-gun symbol (motorised or not), and a
   heavy-weapons unit when it carries the heavy-weapons symbol; the name
   decides only where the counter reading conflicts with the unit's own ID
   code row.
2. **The OA name decides.** Whatever the sheet calls a machine-gun or heavy
   weapons unit is one.
3. **Both must agree.**

## Decision

Option 1. The type is the counter's symbol, recorded as `infantry_type`
(`machine-gun` or `heavy-weapons`) on each such OA unit; units without the
field are neither.

## Rationale

The charts speak of unit types as the counters show them: 20.3's
machine-gun row covers units "with or without" the motorised symbol, and its
heavy-weapons row excepts the Ramcke Brigade's units, which only makes sense
if those battalions carry the heavy-weapons symbol, as their counters do.
The OA names are descriptive (a Bersaglieri *mitraglieri* battalion's
counter and ID code are the Bersaglieri's), so option 2 would put the Bersaglieri
under two types, and options 2 and 3 would both drop the Ramcke battalions
that the 20.3 key presupposes.

The symbols were read from the VASSAL module's counter art (CNA v2.1.0),
which draws the machine-gun symbol as an infantry cross with an upward
arrow and heavy weapons as a box half filled in. 54 OA units carry the
field: 37 machine-gun (12 Commonwealth, 3 German, 22 Italian) and 17 heavy
weapons (8 German, 9 Italian). Every Commonwealth `u` and German `n` unit
is a machine-gun unit and every German `h` unit (Ramcke) heavy weapons, so
the reading agrees with the ID codes where they are exclusive. The one
conflict is the Italian 40/9 Bersaglieri battalion: the VASSAL counter
draws motorised infantry, but the OA sheet calls it a heavy-weapons
battalion and gives it ID code `p`, the row of the two other Italian
heavy-weapons battalions (III/65 and the 101st), so it is recorded as heavy
weapons and the conflict is queued for a check against the printed counter.

A unit may be of two kinds at once for other charts: the Ramcke battalions
are heavy weapons for 19.5 and 15.4 and still pay the paratroop price in
20.3, as that chart says. Under this ruling heavy-weapons units do not count
against a parent's one-infantry attachment limit; they still count toward
its total of battalion-equivalents.
