# R-110 — Graziani's Offensive victory supply without the Logistics Game

## Problem

60.81 asks Italian holding units to be "suppliable by convoy" from Tobruk
(or, for the strategic level, from map D) and Commonwealth holding units to
have a truck-convoy route to Cairo or Alexandria. Both are Logistics Game
ideas. 60.92 lets the scenario be played with the Land Game alone, where
there are no truck convoys or dumps, only the supply units of §32, and
nothing says what the test becomes. cna-engine asked for one meaning.

## Options

1. **A route, not a load.** The holding unit needs an unbroken route from
   its hex to the source (any hex of Tobruk; any hex of map D; any hex of
   Cairo or Alexandria), of any length, along which a medium truck could
   move: no hex or hexside impassable to a medium truck, no hex holding an
   enemy unit, and no hex in an enemy ZOC unless a friendly unit is in it
   (the tracing standard of 32.17). The source hex itself must not hold an
   enemy unit. No supply unit is needed.
2. **§32 supply as well.** Option 1, and the holding unit must also be
   within half its CPA of a friendly supply unit (32.17).
3. **No supply test** in Land-only play: holding is enough.

## Decision

Option 1. A holding unit qualifies when a medium truck could travel, any distance, from its hex to the named source without crossing enemy units or unoccupied enemy ZOC.

## Rationale

The printed test is whether supply *could* reach the unit by convoy, not
whether a dump is near it, and it sets no length; a route is the part of
that test that survives without trucks and dumps. Borrowing 32.17's way of
tracing (as a medium truck, blocked by enemy ZOC unless friendly-occupied)
keeps one tracing rule in the Land-only game. Option 2 adds a condition the
printed text does not have, and option 3 drops one it does: an Italian
column that has outrun its line to Tobruk would win without it. In the data
each level of `scenario:grazianis-offensive` now carries `supply_trace`
naming the source.

Other scenarios' victory supply tests (truck Movement Point limits, dumps)
are open for their own rulings; R-109 covers the campaign game's.
