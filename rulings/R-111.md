# R-111 — Is the Flak printed on the map heavy?

## Problem

Some map hexes (Tripoli among them) carry Flak printed on the map rather
than on a counter. 40.74 bars fighters from flying Flak Suppression against
heavy flak, but the rules do not say whether printed map Flak is heavy or
light, so it is unclear whether such hexes can be suppressed. NJHarman:[^nj]

> INTERPRETATION  On map printed Flak such as in Tripoli is considered heavy
> flak. Affects Fighter Flak Suppression missions (which can’t be flown
> against heavy flak [40.74]).

[^nj]: NJHarman, *CfNA House Rules & Interpretations*, § Map,
  <https://friendorfoe.com/war/cfna/houserules/#map>, CC-BY-SA 4.0 (tagged INTERPRETATION). See `ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## Options

1. **As NJHarman.** Printed map Flak counts as heavy: fighters may not fly
   Flak Suppression against it.
2. **Light unless stated.** Printed map Flak is treated as light, so 40.74
   does not stop fighters suppressing it.
3. **By the counters' rule.** Classify printed Flak with whatever rule
   (46.1) classifies Flak counters, reading the map symbol the same way.
