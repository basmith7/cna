# R-019 — The fleet's western limit: 100 sea hexes, or hex row xx29?

## Problem

30.15 states the Mediterranean Fleet's reach two ways at once: a range of
100 sea hexes from Alexandria, and, as a gloss, a westernmost hex row of
xx29 on map B. The two do not agree if the hexes are counted. 30.23's own
example has battleships bombarding Derna, which lies west of row xx29, and
24.5 explains the Derna bypass road as built to keep traffic out of range of
naval guns — both of which assume the ships reach Derna. NJHarman:[^nj-fleet]

> [30.15] says 100 sea hexes is Bxx29. But that's wrong. Counting hexes,
> crossing med, the correct hex row is Bxx24. *The [30.23] example describes
> bombardment of Derna, which is 5 hexes beyond Bxx29. Also [24.5] mentions
> Derna bypass road was built to avoid coastal shelling!*

[^nj-fleet]: NJHarman, *CfNA House Rules & Interpretations*, § Commonwealth
  Fleet (in *Just the Corrections…*),
  <https://friendorfoe.com/war/cfna/houserules/#commonwealth-fleet>,
  CC-BY-SA 4.0 (tagged CORRECTION). See `ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## Options

1. **The 100-hex range governs; the row number is a misprint** (NJHarman).
   Ships may be placed in any sea hex within 100 sea hexes of Alexandria,
   which on the printed map reaches row xx24 on map B and includes Derna.
   Consequence: the 30.23 example and the 24.5 commentary become consistent
   with the rule; a redrawn map must carry the count, not the row.
2. **The row number governs; "100" is approximate.** Ships stop at row xx29.
   Consequence: Derna is out of naval range, the printed example is wrong,
   and the bypass road has no rationale — but the limit is the one a player
   can read off the map without counting.

## Decision

None yet — proposed.

## Rationale

Option 1. The parenthesis in 30.15 is offered as a restatement of the
100-hex range, not as an independent limit; when a gloss and the figure it
glosses disagree, the figure stands. Two other places in the rules (30.23,
24.5) presuppose Derna in range, which fits row xx24 and not xx29. The
restated rule presently repeats both figures ([Special rules — The
Mediterranean Fleet](../rules/90-special.md#the-mediterranean-fleet)) and
will need the row corrected — or dropped in favour of the count — if this
ruling is accepted; the hex count itself should be verified on the map
before that. The September 1979 errata does not touch 30.15.
