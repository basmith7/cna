# R-025 — When does coastal shipping move?

## Problem

The Logistics Game sequence of play (48.0) puts the carriage of cargo
between African ports in a *Tactical Shipping Segment* of the shared
Organization Phase, before either player's movement. Case 56.32 says Axis
coastal ships move in the *Truck Convoy Phase*, which comes after each
player's Movement and Combat Phase. The two cannot both hold: a coastal
ship either sails before or after the fighting of the stage. NJHarman flags
the conflict:[^nj-coast]

> SoP says coastal shipping and port transfers occur during “Tactical
> Shipping Segment.” Rules says coastal ships move in “Truck Convoy Phase”.

[^nj-coast]: NJHarman, *CfNA House Rules & Interpretations*, § Coastal
  Shipping, <https://friendorfoe.com/war/cfna/houserules/#coastal-shipping>, CC-BY-SA 4.0 (tagged
  CORRECTION/CLARIFICATION). See `ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## Options

1. **The Truck Convoy Phase** (56.32; NJHarman's leaning). He adds: [^nj-coast]

   > Grouping all supply movement into one phase is easier to handle track.

   Consequence: Axis coastal ships sail after combat, with the second- and
   third-line trucks, and each player's shipping happens in his own half of
   the stage. Allied port-to-port transfers would need the same move for
   consistency.
2. **The Tactical Shipping Segment** (48.0). Consequence: both players ship
   in the shared Organization Phase, before either moves; the loading cost
   and timing in 56.34 (ships load as the Truck Convoy Phase opens)
   must be read as referring to that segment instead.
3. **Both, by kind.** Allied port-to-port transfers (no counters) in the
   Tactical Shipping Segment; Axis coastal ship counters in the Truck Convoy
   Phase. Consequence: each rule is kept literally for the case it names, at
   the cost of the two sides shipping at different points of the stage.
