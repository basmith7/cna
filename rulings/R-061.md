# R-061 — Drawing water from a broken pipeline

## Problem

Case 52.25 limits a cut pipeline to the last hex still joined to the
line, counting outward from its source. Read literally, only one hex of the surviving
line — the end nearest the break — still yields water, and every hex between
it and the major city stops working. Read as intended by most players, every
hex still joined to the source keeps working and only the hexes beyond the
break are cut off; "farthest connected hex" then just marks where the water
stops. The case does not say which.

## Options

1. **Every hex still joined to the source keeps working; hexes beyond the
   break are dry.** Consequence: a break shortens the line to the break
   point; the line behaves like the unlimited source of 52.23 up to there.
2. **Only the last joined hex yields water.** Consequence: the literal
   reading; a single raid turns a long pipeline into one watering point,
   and units along the line must walk to its end. Hard to justify
   physically, since water passes through those hexes to reach the end.

## Decision

None yet — proposed.

## Rationale

Option 1 matches 52.23 (every pipeline hex is a source like a major city)
and the physical picture; option 2 is the literal wording. The rules prose
currently states option 1's sense ("water still flows up to the last hex
that remains joined to the source") and carries this open question.
