# R-040 — Does fuel in a unit's own tanks evaporate?

## Problem

Case 49.3 has fuel lost to evaporation and spillage wherever it is kept,
and cuts "all fuel levels on the game-map". Case 49.14 gives each vehicle
Strength Point a fuel capacity, the fuel in its own tanks. The Land Game
hot-weather rule (§29.3, restated in `rules/90-special.md`) exempts what is
in tanks and radiators from its 5 % loss. So it is unclear whether the 6 %
(or 9 %) per-turn loss and the 5 % hot-weather loss cut the fuel each unit
carries, or only dumps and truck loads.

## Options

1. **Tanks included.** Every fuel stock on the map, including each unit's
   own tanks, loses the percentage. Consequence: heavy bookkeeping (one
   calculation per Strength Point, each rounded down, so small tanks often
   lose nothing), and units idle far from dumps slowly dry out.
2. **Tanks excluded** (NJHarman's house rule, recorded as the variant
   [V-002](V-002.md); adopting this option would make it the rule). Only dumps, trucks, trains and other stocks lose
   fuel; fuel already in a unit's tanks does not. Consequence: matches the
   §29.3 exclusion, far less bookkeeping; players gain by filling tanks
   before the Stores Expenditure Stage.
3. **Tanks excluded from the hot-weather loss only.** Consequence: follows
   each printed text literally, at the cost of two different scopes for
   two losses in the same case.

## Decision

Open.
