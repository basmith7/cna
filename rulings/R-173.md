# R-173 — SGSU upkeep with the Air Game and abstract logistics

## Problem

Case 35.14 makes every SGSU spend, for its own upkeep, 1 fuel and 1 water
each Operations Stage and 1 stores each Game-Turn, and an SGSU without
them cannot repair its aircraft. With the Air Game on and the Logistics
Game off, 47.0 keeps §32 for everything §47 does not change. §32's supply
units carry only ammunition and fuel, and 32.11 sets aside every rule on
using or spending ammunition, fuel, stores and water except §32's own.
47.2 lists the Air Game's abstract costs (flak, rearming, refuelling) and
says nothing of an SGSU's upkeep. cna-engine asked what an SGSU pays each
stage and turn, and when it counts as lacking its supplies.

## Options

1. **No upkeep; supplied means in reach of a source.** An SGSU spends
   nothing on its own upkeep. It counts as having its supplies when it can
   draw them under 47.11–47.13: it is at an off-map air facility, or (a
   Commonwealth SGSU) in or within reach of Cairo or Alexandria, or within
   15 CP of a friendly supply unit (a depot included), traced as 32.16.
   An SGSU with none of these cannot repair aircraft. Consequence: no new
   charge, one supply test an engine already needs for refuelling.
2. **Fuel from a supply unit; water and stores dropped.** Each SGSU draws
   1 fuel a stage from a supply unit within 15 CP (none at an unlimited
   source); one that cannot draw it cannot repair. Consequence: a running
   drain of 3 fuel a turn per SGSU that no case of §32 or §47 sets.
3. **No upkeep and no sanction.** An SGSU never lacks its own supplies.
   Consequence: an SGSU cut off from every source still repairs aircraft.

## Decision

Option 1. With the Air Game and abstract logistics an SGSU pays no fuel,
water or stores for its own upkeep; it has its supplies, and may repair
aircraft, only while it can draw supplies under 47.11–47.13.

## Rationale

The printed text decides the cost. 32.11 sets aside all use and
expenditure of the four supply types except what §32 itself spends, and
47.0 keeps §32 with only §47's changes; §47's list of expenditures (47.2)
names flak, rearming and refuelling and no upkeep. 35.14's charge is
therefore one of the references 32.11 ignores, as construction's supply
costs are ([R-133](R-133.md)) and a desert raider's calls at dumps and
wells ([R-148](R-148.md)). Option 2 keeps the one supply type a supply
unit happens to hold and spends it on a purpose §32 and §47 never name,
the reasoning R-133 turned down. Water and stores have no holder at all
under §32, so no option can charge them.

35.14's sanction is a different matter: it ties an SGSU's work to being
supplied, and §47 says exactly how an SGSU is supplied, by drawing from an
off-map facility, Cairo or Alexandria, or a supply unit within 15 CP
(47.11–47.13). Option 1 keeps that link; option 3 drops it and lets an
SGSU deep behind enemy lines go on repairing, which nothing in §47
suggests. The test in option 1 is the same one an engine runs before it
refuels a squadron from a supply unit (47.23), so it adds no new state.
[R-174](R-174.md) applies the same test to refits and repairs.
