# R-051 — Rounding of captured ammunition and stores

## Problem

Case 50.16 lets a captor use one-third of captured ammunition, rounded up,
and a player who recaptures a dump use one-third of what is left, with no
rounding stated for the recapture. Case 51.16 lets a captor use half of
captured stores and states no rounding at all. With odd or small stocks the
answer changes what survives (one captured Stores Point yields 1 or 0).

## Options

1. **Round up throughout.** Apply 50.16's stated rounding to the recapture
   and to stores as well. Consequence: one rule for every capture; a tiny
   stock is never wholly lost.
2. **Round up only where stated; round down elsewhere.** Consequence:
   follows the letter of each case; recaptures and captured stores lose the
   odd point, and a single captured point is lost.

## Decision

Option 1. Every captured or recaptured share of ammunition or stores is rounded up.

## Rationale

Case 50.16 states round-up for the captor's one-third and is silent on the
recapture that follows in the same case; 51.16 gives half of captured
stores with no rounding at all. Nothing in the printed text points to
rounding down, and the only rounding the capture rules ever state is up, so
we extend it to the silent cases. One rule for every capture is also the
simplest for an engine, and it avoids the odd result of a single captured
point vanishing entirely. The existing example in [Ammunition and stores
— Captured ammunition](../rules/logistics/20-ammunition-and-stores.md)
already rounds the recapture up.

supersession like any ruling (see [rulings/README.md](README.md)).
