# R-125 — Breakdown: which reconnaissance units are armoured cars

## Problem

21.14 gives armoured cars a breakdown adjustment of one column left and
armoured recce none, and 21.13 checks each BAR separately. The Axis
characteristics charts say which rows are armoured cars (German ee and ff,
Italian vv and ww, all "Breakdown Adjustment Rating 1 Left"). The
Commonwealth chart lists eleven reconnaissance rows, hh to tt, and says
nothing: its ll row alone holds the 11th Hussars, the 13th Lancers, three
infantry divisions' Recce Regiments and four divisional cavalry regiments.
The Italian chart's other reconnaissance rows are camels, motorcycle
machine-gunners and desert patrols "treated as mounted in medium trucks",
which 21.11 neither lists nor exempts by name. cna-engine asked for a
vehicle class per unit so it can check breakdown for these points.

## Options

1. **Per unit, by what the counter is.** The Axis rows follow their chart
   notes. A Commonwealth unit is an armoured car unit when its title or OA
   note says armoured car, or it is a cavalry or yeomanry regiment fighting
   as one (Hussars, Lancers, Royals, King's Dragoon Guards, Derbyshire
   Yeomanry, the Guides, the Duke of York's, the Free French column); a
   division's Recce Regiment, divisional cavalry, and the Indian and Polish
   horse regiments that fought on carriers are armoured recce. Camel and
   motorcycle units never break down; the Italian desert patrols break
   down as trucks.
2. **Every Commonwealth reconnaissance unit is an armoured car unit.**
3. **Every Commonwealth reconnaissance unit is armoured recce.**

## Decision

Option 1. Each reconnaissance unit's class is set per unit in the OA data,
following the Axis chart notes and, for the Commonwealth, what the unit
was.

## Rationale

The rulebook draws the line by unit, not by row: its unit descriptions say
recce units carry light cars and carriers while armoured car units are
"virtually light tank battalions" with some tanks and heavier cars, and
that the British counted recce as infantry and armoured cars as armour.
The Commonwealth rows cannot carry that split (ll mixes both), so options
2 and 3 would misclass either the 11th Hussars or the 51st Recce Regiment.
20.51's bar on upgrading infantry divisions' recce units points the same
way: those are the light, carrier-mounted units.

21.11 makes vehicles break down and exempts motorcycles; camels have no
vehicles, so both are `none`. The desert patrols' OA note puts them in
medium trucks, and nothing exempts those trucks, so they use the truck
rating. A light tank point added to a recce unit under 20.52 is a tank
point and checks with the tanks at its own model's BAR, as 21.13 checks
per type and per BAR.

No unit's TOE points are its own trucks. Truck points are always separate
(8.91), even when a unit's counter shows them; a unit motorised by trucks
checks its truck points as trucks and its TOE points by their own class.

For an engine: every OA unit on a Recce or Reconnaissance row of
`data/tables/unit-characteristics.json` carries `vehicle`
(`armoured-car`, `armoured-recce`, `truck` or `none`), keyed into
`data/tables/breakdown-adjustments.json`.
