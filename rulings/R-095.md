# R-095 — The Commonwealth fleet at the start of Group One

## Problem

60.45 gives the Commonwealth fleet twice and the two do not agree. Its
opening sentence counts 1 battleship, 3 cruisers and 7 destroyers in all,
placed by the Fleet Schedule (30.59). The lists that follow
put two battleships, two cruisers, four anti-aircraft cruisers and six
destroyers in Alexandria, and one cruiser, one anti-aircraft cruiser and
two destroyers at Valletta. The case also bars movement until the first
OpStage of Game-Turn 2 in one sentence and until a September stage in
another.

## Options

1. **The lists.** Start the ships the lists name, where they name them; no
   ship moves before Game-Turn 2, OpStage 1.
2. **The total and the Fleet Schedule.** Start what the schedule shows for
   September 1940, up to the stated total; ignore the lists.
