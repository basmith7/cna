# R-095 — The Commonwealth fleet at the start of Group One

## Problem

60.45 gives the Commonwealth fleet twice and the two do not agree. Its
opening sentence counts 1 battleship, 3 cruisers and 7 destroyers in all,
placed by the Fleet Schedule (30.59). The lists that follow
put two battleships, two cruisers, four anti-aircraft cruisers and six
destroyers in Alexandria, and one cruiser, one anti-aircraft cruiser and
two destroyers at Valletta. The case also bars movement until the first
OpStage of Game-Turn 2 in one sentence and until a September stage in
another.

## Options

1. **The lists.** Start the ships the lists name, where they name them; no
   ship moves before Game-Turn 2, OpStage 1.
2. **The total and the Fleet Schedule.** Start what the schedule shows for
   September 1940, up to the stated total; ignore the lists.

## Decision

Option 2. The Commonwealth fleet is the opening paragraph's total (one battleship, three cruisers, seven destroyers), deployed by the Fleet Schedule (30.59); the lists of ships by port are ignored, and no ship moves before Game-Turn 2, OpStage 1.

## Rationale

SPI's own errata (60.45, "important correction") says only the first
paragraph of the case is correct and that everything from "The following
units are available..." on is to be ignored. That is the printed text
settling the question, so it outranks the reasoning this ruling first gave
for the lists. The schedule agrees with the paragraph: it deploys exactly
one battleship, three cruisers and seven destroyers on Game-Turn 1,
OpStage 1, all in Alexandria, and lets one cruiser and two destroyers start
at Valletta instead, which is also where the old lists put them. The
movement ban the first paragraph states (not before Game-Turn 2, OpStage 1)
is the one kept; the calendar-style ban in the ignored lists goes with them.
For an engine the fleet is now the schedule's own rows plus a total, with
nothing to reconcile.

*History.* First decided for Option 1 (the lists) before the errata item
was noticed; reopened and reversed on the strength of it.

Decided under Brian's delegation of 2026-09-26; open to dispute and
supersession like any ruling (see [rulings/README.md](README.md)).
