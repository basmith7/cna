# R-145 — Who knows the numbers when prisoners escape

## Problem

3.62 keeps the numbers of prisoners, camps and guards secret. But prisoners
left without enough guards escape (28.17, 28.23); escapees within eight CP
of a friendly unit rejoin it and come back a month later as replacement
points; the rest walk towards their own lines, losing 10 % a stage without
water, and are recaptured if an enemy unit enters their hex (28.24–28.25).
Someone must move them and count their thirst losses. cna-engine asked
whether escape and liberation numbers are known to both sides.

## Options

1. **Known to both at the hand-overs, private in between.** When prisoner
   points escape, both sides learn how many: the captor already held them,
   and their own side now owns and moves the escapee marker. From then on
   the escapees are their own side's secret (thirst losses, and the count
   that rejoins and later returns as replacement points). On recapture the
   recapturing side learns the number it takes, as it does with any capture.
2. **Known to both throughout.** Escapees' numbers are public until they
   rejoin or are recaptured.
3. **Secret from their own side.** The captor keeps the count and tells the
   other side only when the escapees arrive. Leaves nobody able to move them.

## Decision

Option 1. Both sides learn the count when prisoners escape and when
escapees are recaptured; between those moments the escapees' own side alone
knows their strength.

## Rationale

The 3.62 secret is the captor's: an enemy may not count the prisoners he
holds. At an escape the captor loses that secret to the men's own side,
who must move them (28.24) and so must know their strength; the captor
knew it already. Once they are their own side's marker, 3.61's default
makes their state secret, and replacement points are secret by 3.62.
Option 2 grants status of an opposing counter no rule grants; option 3
breaks 28.24's movement.

Decided under Brian's delegation of 2026-09-26; open to dispute and
supersession like any ruling (see [rulings/README.md](README.md)).
