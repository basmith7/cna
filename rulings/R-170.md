# R-170 — Crusader's second Blenheim IVF entry

## Problem

Operation Crusader's Commonwealth North African air force (62.33) lists the
Blenheim IVF twice, on separate lines with separate counts: 24 (16
refitted) and, further down, 142 (100 refitted). The list otherwise names
each type once. Whether the second line is more of the same fighter or a
different Blenheim decides what 142 planes can do: the IVF is a fighter
(34.6's fighter table, no bombs), the Blenheim IV a bomber (6 bomb points).

## Options

1. **The second line is the Blenheim IV bomber.** 24 IVF fighters and 142
   IV bombers.
2. **Both lines are the IVF, as printed.** 166 IVF fighters, and no
   Blenheim bombers.

## Decision

Option 1. The second entry keeps its transcribed name in `type`
("Blenheim IV F (second line)") and takes
`aircraft:cw:blenheim-mk-iv` as its `aircraft_id`.

## Rationale

A set-up list with one line per type does not repeat a type with a second
count unless the second line is a different type; read as printed, the
list would make the 142 a duplicate the page never explains. The
Commonwealth reinforcement schedule (34.84) settles which type it is:
before Crusader's first Game-Turn (57) it delivers 185 Blenheim IV
bombers and only 42 IVF fighters, so 24 IVFs fits the first line and a
force of well over a hundred Blenheims can only be the bombers. Option 2
would also leave the Commonwealth's largest bomber type out of the battle
and give it more Blenheim fighters than the schedule ever sends.

Decided under Brian's delegation of 2026-09-26; open to dispute and
supersession like any ruling (see [rulings/README.md](README.md)).
