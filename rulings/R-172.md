# R-172 — Ports and the water pipeline as air targets under abstract logistics (§47)

## Problem

Ports (41.39, 40.66, torpedoes, mining by plane) lose efficiency levels,
and the water pipeline (40.67) is cut by strafing. Both look like Logistics
Game installations: §32 ignores water outright (32.11), and §47's convoys
have no tonnage limit (47.61). Yet §47 keeps every Air Game rule it does
not change, and the Land Game played alone still uses port efficiency:
each scenario sets its ports' levels, 32.36 lets the Commonwealth ship a
supply unit only into a port at 50 % or better, and the Axis may divert an
arrival to Benghazi only at level 3 or better. The §32 errata also let a
desert raider cut pipeline (27.32). cna-engine asked whether these targets
exist with abstract logistics, and what a hit does.

## Options

1. **Both stay, with their printed effects.** Ports are bombed, strafed,
   torpedoed and mined as printed; the current level counts from the Port
   Capacity Chart's maximum and the scenario's start, recovers under 55.18,
   and mines or blocked levels are cleared under 55.26 with no supplies
   spent ([R-133](R-133.md)). The level limits what the Land Game and §32
   tie to it, never an abstract Axis convoy (47.61). The pipeline may be
   strafed and cut as printed, but with water ignored a cut has no effect.
2. **Ports stay; the pipeline is not a target.** As option 1 for ports;
   the pipeline strafing mission is unavailable under §47.
3. **Neither exists.** Port efficiency and the pipeline belong to the
   Logistics Game; both missions are unavailable, and Tripoli raids (41.81)
   may hit only grounded aircraft.

## Decision

Option 1. Ports and the pipeline stay targets as printed; a port's
efficiency level limits only what the Land Game and §32 already tie to it,
never a §47 convoy, and a cut pipeline has no effect while water is
ignored.

## Rationale

47.0 keeps every rule it does not modify, and §47 modifies neither port
bombing nor pipeline strafing. Port efficiency is not a Logistics Game
number alone: the scenarios set it for the Land Game, and 32.36 and the
Benghazi diversion read it, so a raid that lowers it has real, if smaller,
effect. 55.18's recovery costs nothing, so it works unchanged. Clearing a
mine or a blocked level is an engineer task on the Construction Chart, and
[R-133](R-133.md) already has construction spend no supplies under §32.
47.61 states that abstract convoys have no tonnage limit, so a damaged port
never holds one back; reading the level into the convoy would add a limit
the designer removed. Option 3 deletes rules §47 leaves standing and
contradicts §32's own use of efficiency.

For the pipeline, the printed result (a 2 or more cuts it in the hex) can
be kept at no cost: the §32 errata already treat pipeline as map state a
raider can cut, so the engine tracks it either way. Nothing in §32 reads
it, so a cut changes nothing, which is 40.67's own warning that strafing
the pipeline is a poor use of fighters. Option 2 would make the same hex
cuttable by a raider but not by a fighter for no textual reason.

Decided under Brian's delegation of 2026-09-26; open to dispute and
supersession like any ruling (see [rulings/README.md](README.md)).
