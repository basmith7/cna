# R-128 — Dummy supply units: counted after convoy bombing

## Problem

32.18 allows one dummy supply unit for every three real supply units a
player receives or starts a scenario with. Axis supply units reach Africa
by convoy, and a convoy attack of 20 % or more destroys at least one of
them (32.65). cna-engine asked whether the Axis counts the supply units it
planned to ship, or only those that survive the bombing.

## Options

1. **After bombing.** Only supply units that arrive count toward dummies.
2. **Before bombing.** Every supply unit loaded on a convoy counts, sunk or
   not.

## Decision

Option 1. A real supply unit counts toward dummies when it is received:
for the Axis, when its convoy arrives, after any bombing; units sunk at sea
never count.

## Rationale

32.18 counts units a player "receives", and a unit sunk on the way is
never received: 32.64 and 32.65 destroy it before it arrives. Counting it
would also let the Axis turn bomb losses into decoys, so the Commonwealth's
successful attack would add counters to the map it has to search. Option 1
asks nothing new of an engine: it counts arrivals, the same event that
places the real unit. As 32.18 lumps starting and received units together,
the count is a running total through the game: a player may have placed
one dummy for each full three real units received or started with so far.
