# R-151 — The *San Giorgio*'s values, and when it is on the map

## Problem

30.17 makes the half-sunk *San Giorgio* an immobile artillery unit in
Tobruk that fires into adjacent hexes and, under 30.39, at Commonwealth
ships through sea hexsides. Its counter prints only a type and two ratings
(CL 2/6 in the VASSAL module, gun and AA as on the Commonwealth ships), no
vulnerability, CPA or TOE, and its OA entry says land units cannot attack
it. An engine needs: its barrage and AA points, a vulnerability (or a
reason it needs none), how many bomb points it puts on a ship (30.35
halves the raw barrage points of mobile artillery but not of emplaced
guns), which scenarios start with it, and when it leaves.

## Options

1. **The counter's two ratings and nothing more.** 2 barrage points and
   6 AA points, both fixed. It has no vulnerability: it is never a target
   of land fire (barrage, assault or anti-armour), so nothing reads one,
   and 30.39 lets it fire at ships without 30.35's vulnerability-6 test.
   Against a ship its 2 points enter the Air Bombardment Table whole, as an
   emplaced gun's do, since it cannot move. It is in Tobruk's hex (C4807)
   at the start of the scenarios that begin on Game-Turn 1 (Graziani's
   Offensive and the two that extend it), and fires only while an Axis
   land unit is in that hex. It is removed at the end of any Operations
   Stage in which the hex holds a Commonwealth engineer unit (an E unit,
   23.1) and no Axis unit.
2. **As option 1, but give it an Italian artillery vulnerability** (for
   instance the 7 of a towed medium gun) so it can be hit by barrage and
   lost to forward-gun losses like any other gun.
3. **As option 1, but halve its points against ships** (30.35's rule for
   mobile artillery), so it puts 1 bomb point on a ship.

## Decision

Option 1. The *San Giorgio* fires 2 barrage points and 6 AA points, has no
vulnerability because nothing on land may attack it, puts its full 2 points
on a ship, starts only in the Game-Turn 1 scenarios, and goes when
Commonwealth engineers hold Tobruk alone.

## Rationale

The two figures are the counter's, the same gun/AA pair the Commonwealth
ships print (30.12), and NJHarman's house rule ([V-001](V-001.md)) quotes
the same AA 6, a second witness to the module's counter. The OA sheet's
note that land units cannot attack the ship makes a vulnerability moot:
the rating only ever measures exposure to enemy land fire and gates 30.35,
which 30.39 replaces for this ship, so option 2 invents a number to feed
rules that never reach it. 30.39 says it fires using its gun rating as
bombardment points; it is a fixed gun that can never move, which is the
case 30.35 exempts from halving, so option 3 reads against both. Every
scenario that starts after Game-Turn 1 starts on or after Game-Turn 26,
when Tobruk has been in Commonwealth hands since January 1941; their notes
keep only the wreck's lost port levels ([R-082](R-082.md)). 30.17 says it
lasts until enemy engineers remove or sink it but gives no cost or step;
removing it when a Commonwealth engineer unit holds the hex is the
plainest reading, and keeping it silent while no Axis unit shares its
hex stops an Italian gun firing from a Commonwealth-held town in the
stages before the engineers arrive. Ammunition and the rest of the
artillery rules apply to it unchanged; this ruling does not touch them.
