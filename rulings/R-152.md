# R-152 — Captured guns and tanks become the captor's replacement points at once

## Problem

28.3 lets captured equipment be used against its former owner once it has
been repaired, and says no more. Guns and tanks are captured in several ways:
as the captured share of close-assault losses (15.86), by surrender of any
kind (17.51, which treats every surrender as a capture), by the marsh rule
for stranded guns, and, separately, as destroyed-tank markers (14.5) and
broken-down vehicles (21.5). §22 repairs only broken-down vehicles and
destroyed tanks; nothing in the rules repairs a gun, and a tank taken in an
assault is neither broken down nor a destroyed-tank marker. What does
"repaired" ask of captured points that are not wrecks, which units may take
them, how do they count once taken, and do captured wrecks qualify?

## Options

1. **Replacement points at once.** Every captured TOE point of a gun or
   vehicle class becomes, at the moment of capture, the captor's
   replacement points of its class (17.51), with no repair roll and no
   training (as a repaired vehicle, 22.5). 28.3's repair condition is met by
   §22 for the only captured equipment that is damaged: destroyed-tank
   markers (22.4, facility only) and broken-down vehicles (22.2x field or
   22.3x facility), which become replacement points when repaired, as 22.5
   says.
2. **Captured vehicles must be repaired as broken-down vehicles.** Captured
   tank and armoured-recce points become broken-down vehicles of the captor,
   usable only after a field or facility repair roll; guns, which no table
   repairs, are usable at once. Two paths for one rule, and a repair roll on
   equipment the rules never damaged.
3. **Captured tanks are destroyed-tank markers.** As 2, but facility repair
   only (22.4). Most captured tanks would never be used: few captors can tow
   them to a facility.

## Decision

Option 1. Captured gun, tank and armoured-recce TOE points become the
captor's replacement points of their class the moment they are captured, to
be absorbed like any other, without repair or training; only destroyed-tank
markers and broken-down vehicles need §22 repair first.

## Rationale

17.51 says outright what a surrender, voluntary or not, does with tanks and
guns: it turns them into replacements. 15.86 lets the captor use the assault
share "as they see fit", which is the same thing in looser words. The only
equipment the rules damage is a destroyed tank or a broken-down vehicle, and
§22 already says how each is repaired and that a repaired vehicle is a
replacement point of its type (22.5); 28.3's "once repaired" reads naturally
as a reminder of that path, not as a second repair step. Options 2 and 3
invent a repair for equipment nothing damaged, and still leave guns, which
no rule repairs, with no way to qualify; that is the contradiction they would
have to paper over. Option 1 is also one path for an engine: a capture adds
replacement points to the captor's side, and everything after that is the
replacement rules it already has.

What follows, in detail, for an engine:

- **What is captured.** A captured TOE point of an infantry-type unit
  (every 20.3 row costed in infantry points, heavy weapons included) is a
  prisoner point (§28). A captured point of an armoured-recce, armoured-car,
  tank, artillery, anti-tank or anti-air unit gives the replacement points
  20.3 charges to rebuild one TOE point of that unit type, first
  alternative: one point of its class, or two armoured-recce points for an
  armoured car.
- **Where they start.** In the hex of the capturing unit; where several
  enemy units made the capture, the captor picks one of their hexes. They
  are earmarked at once for a unit of the captor's side (20.41) and are
  replacement counters from then on.
- **Who may take them.** Any unit of the captor's side, German or Italian
  alike, that 19.6 would let rebuild with that class: tank points any tank
  unit, artillery points any artillery unit, and so on. Two printed bars
  stand: captured anti-tank guns never form an Axis ad hoc battery (19.86),
  and under §32 the Commonwealth removes captured Axis anti-air points at
  once (32.81).
- **How they count.** Absorbed points are ordinary TOE points of the unit
  that takes them: its ratings, its cohesion and state (20.4x), and its
  printed maximum TOE as the cap. They change no 19.3 slot, which units
  fill, not points. A point stops being "captured" when it is absorbed;
  until then 22.14's separate repair batch and the German Mobile Tank Repair
  Squad's bar apply to it.
- **Wrecks.** A destroyed-tank marker captured under 14.5 qualifies only
  after a 22.4 facility repair; a broken-down vehicle captured under 21.5
  after a 22.2x or 22.3x repair. Each then becomes a replacement point under
  22.5 and follows the points above. Guns lost to vulnerability (15.84) and
  every eliminated point outside the captured share are gone, not captured.
