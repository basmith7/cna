# R-030 — Order of the Logistics organisation segments

## Problem

The Logistics sequence (48.0) lets the seven organisation segments run in any order. Two of them interact: attrition reduces units left short of water or stores, and water distribution hands out water. If attrition may come before water distribution in the same stage, a unit can be reduced for want of water that is sitting in its hex. Supply distribution (sharing out supplies in a hex, loading trucks) interacts the same way with water distribution. The printed text does not say whether attrition judges the unit's state before or after this stage's distribution.

## Options

1. **Any order, as printed.** Consequence: players choose; an engine must let the owner pick the order, and careless order costs strength. Attrition in practice checks whatever was received when it is run.
2. **Fixed order: supply distribution, then water distribution, then attrition; the rest in any order.** Consequence: no unit suffers attrition for supply that was in reach this stage; an engine has one deterministic order.
3. **Attrition judges the previous stage's supply, whenever it is run.** Consequence: the order no longer matters for attrition, but water given now counts only from next stage.

## Decision

Option 1. The seven organisation segments run in whatever order the players choose, and attrition judges each unit's water and stores as they stand at the moment that segment is run.

## Rationale

The printed sequence (48.0, organisation phase) says in plain words that its
segments may be taken in any order, and nothing in the attrition segment asks
for a look back to an earlier moment. Option 1 is therefore the reading the
text supports. It opens no exploit: the only player hurt by a careless order
is the one who chose it, and a sensible player simply distributes water and
supplies before running attrition. Option 2 would add a sequence the rules do
not print, and option 3 would invent a memory of last stage's supply that the
game never records. For an engine the cost is small: offer the owner the
remaining segments to pick from, and evaluate attrition on current state.

Decided under Brian's delegation of 2026-09-26; open to dispute and supersession like any ruling (see [rulings/README.md](README.md)).
