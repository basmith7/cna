# R-134 — Where minefields may be laid: 24.35's terrain or the chart's

## Problem

Case 24.35 lets a minefield be laid in clear, sand/gravel or rough hexes,
never in a major city. The Construction Chart (24.17) gives the sites for
both real and dummy minefields as clear, sand/gravel and **salt marsh**.
Both scans of the chart agree, and the September 1979 errata touch
neither. Rough and salt marsh are both common on the map (655 and 266
hexes), so the difference matters. cna-engine asked which governs, and
meanwhile follows the chart.

## Options

1. **The text (24.35) governs**: clear, sand/gravel or rough.
2. **The chart governs**: clear, sand/gravel or salt marsh.
3. **Either**: clear, sand/gravel, rough or salt marsh.
4. **Both**: only where they agree, clear or sand/gravel.

## Decision

Option 1. Minefields, real or dummy, may be laid in clear, sand/gravel or
rough hexes, as 24.35 says; the chart's salt marsh is read as a misprint.

## Rationale

Here one case of rules text stands against one chart cell, so
[R-018](R-018.md)'s reason for following the charts (two charts agreeing
against one sentence) does not apply. The case is the fuller statement:
it names the sites, adds the major-city exclusion and one field per hex,
and the chart's own key says its entries are described in the cases
(24.13). The repair-facility figures, where the chart and the text also
disagree, already follow the text ([Construction](../rules/80-engineering.md#construction)).
Option 1 also makes sense on the map: a field exists to stop vehicles,
and most vehicles may not enter salt marsh off a road or track at all
([movement](../rules/40-movement.md)), so a field there would bar little
that the marsh does not, while rough ground is open to them. Options 3 and 4 invent a list
neither source prints. For an engine every option is one terrain list;
`data/tables/construction.json` keeps the chart's cell as printed, with a
note pointing here.

Decided under Brian's delegation of 2026-09-26; open to dispute and
supersession like any ruling (see [rulings/README.md](README.md)).
