# R-147 — Desert raiders are counters of their own; a raider a set-up places starts formed

## Problem

27.11 gives the Commonwealth two Long Range Desert Group counters and 27.2
the Axis one Sonderkommando Almasy (DSA) counter. The Commonwealth counter
sheet prints "LRDG 1" and "LRDG 2", but no OA sheet lists any of the three,
so they have no ID code, no characteristics row and no arrival. 27.13 makes
a raider only by removing a recce or armoured-car TOE point from a unit at
maximum morale. Rommel's Arrival nevertheless sets up "one unit of the LRDG"
at Jalo. Does that raider cost a TOE point, and from which unit?

## Options

1. **Pieces of their own, and a set-up's raider is free.** The three
   counters carry their 27.14 values in place of a characteristics row. A
   raider a scenario places starts formed, and no unit loses a TOE point for
   it; 27.13 governs only raiders formed during play.
2. **A set-up's raider is paid for.** The Commonwealth player removes one
   recce or armoured-car TOE point from a set-up unit at maximum morale, as
   27.13 would.
3. **No raider at set-up.** The Jalo line is read as where an LRDG may be
   formed, and none starts in play.

## Decision

Option 1. `unit:cw:lrdg-1`, `unit:cw:lrdg-2` and `unit:de:dsa` are pieces
with `raider` values (CPA 50, no combat ratings, no stacking points, never
break down, moving as a recce unit) in place of a characteristics row, and
a raider a set-up places is formed already, at no TOE cost.

## Rationale

A scenario's set-up lists the counters as they stand at its start, and
every other unit in it is placed as it is, not built from something else;
the Jalo line is the same kind of entry, so the LRDG there is simply in
play. Option 2 adds a cost the scenario does not print, and no unit in that
scenario's Commonwealth set-up is shown as trained to maximum morale, so it
could not be paid as 27.12 requires. Option 3 discards a printed placement.
Forming in play is unchanged: a raider is made from a recce or armoured-car
TOE point (in the data, a unit whose `vehicle` is `armoured-car` or
`armoured-recce`) of the raider's own nation, the DSA from German units
only (27.2b), and at most two LRDG and one DSA are ever in play. The
counter-sheet names give the LRDG abbreviations; the DSA's is the rules'.
As with Rommel ([R-135](R-135.md)), an engine reads each as one more
counter whose `raider` values stand in for a characteristics row.
