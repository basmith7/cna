# R-159 — What Rommel's Arrival's release schedule counts

## Problem

61.38 holds every unit in Cairo and Alexandria in the opening OpStage, then
releases "one battalion" a stage; the 7th Armoured Division releases one
tank battalion a stage once 75 % repaired and "one more nontank battalion"
a stage, both from the first OpStage of April 1941; its HQs go at any time.
It does not say what counts as a battalion (an artillery regiment, a
company, an HQ of another formation), nor whether the 7th Armoured's
releases come out of the general one a stage or on top of it.

## Options

1. **Counters, HQs free, 7th Armoured on top.** Every land counter other
   than an HQ counts as one battalion, whatever its size; the 22nd Guards
   Brigade with its attached battalions counts as one; every HQ leaves
   freely. The 7th Armoured's tank battalion and other battalion a stage
   are its own allowance, over and above the general one.
2. **Battalion-equivalents, 7th Armoured inside the limit.** A company
   counts as half; the 7th Armoured's releases use up the general
   allowance.
3. **Counters, 7th Armoured inside the limit.**

## Decision

Option 1. From Game-Turn 27 OpStage 1 one non-HQ counter a stage leaves
Cairo or Alexandria, and the 7th Armoured Division may also release one
tank battalion (at 75 % repaired) and one other battalion a stage; HQs and
the 18th Australian Brigade (from Game-Turn 27 OpStage 2) leave freely.

## Rationale

The printed text supports it. "In addition" introduces the 7th Armoured's
allowance, and it starts on the same stage as the general one, so it is
read as extra; read as part of the general limit, the "one more nontank
battalion" would have nothing to be more than. 61.38 frees the 7th's HQs
and counts the Guards Brigade as one, which shows the limit is about
fighting counters; freeing every HQ keeps that consistent and costs
nothing, since an HQ alone cannot fight. Counting counters rather than
battalion-equivalents avoids half-units in a one-a-stage limit and is the
simplest for an engine: one count per stage per allowance.
