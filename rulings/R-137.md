# R-137 — Rommel's +5 CPA: which units, and what parting costs

## Problem

Section 31 gives a unit "+5 CPA" when Rommel starts an Operations Stage
with it and stays with it for the whole stage. The printed sentence allows
"a unit(s)", but does not say whether the Axis picks one, when the bonus is
known, or what happens when the two part before the stage ends. Because
disorganisation points are earned the moment a unit spends past its CPA
(6.21), a unit that used the bonus and then lost it has already been
spared points it did not earn. cna-engine asked.

## Options

1. **Every unit in his hex at the stage's start.** Each one's CPA is five
   higher for as long as it and Rommel stay in the same hex. A unit that
   parts from him loses the bonus at once and takes the disorganisation
   points the bonus had spared it (one per CP it has spent past its own
   CPA, at most five).
2. **One unit, named at the stage's start.** The Axis picks a single unit
   in his hex; the rest is as option 1.
3. **Settled at the stage's end.** Every unit that was with him from start
   to end gets the bonus; disorganisation for the five CP above the CPA is
   held back until the stage ends and then charged or waived.

## Decision

Option 1. Every unit stacked with Rommel when an Operations Stage begins
has CPA +5 while it stays in his hex; a unit that parts from him loses the
bonus and at once takes the disorganisation points it had been spared.

## Rationale

The printed "a unit(s)" allows more than one, and nothing in 31.0 asks the
player to name it, so option 2 adds a choice the text does not make. Option
3 contradicts 6.21, under which a point spent over the CPA disorganises
the unit immediately, and it would leave every unit's cohesion level
provisional all stage. Option 1 keeps 6.21's immediacy and the printed
condition: the bonus lasts exactly as long as the two stay together, and
parting undoes it, so a unit can never keep a bonus it did not earn.
"Parting" is anything that leaves them in different hexes: either moving
without the other, a retreat or reaction by one alone, or Rommel's removal
by a raid. A unit joining his hex after the stage starts never qualifies,
and none does while he is away in Germany. Each counter is a unit: a
formation qualifies through its counters, and moving as one body (6.15) it
uses its slowest component's raised CPA. The bonus counts wherever the
rules use the unit's CPA in that stage except two places: the
non-motorised cap of 8.17, which the printed text measures against the
"base CPA", and whether a unit is motorised at all (CPA 10 or less), which
is a property of the unit, not of the stage. For an engine: per unit per
stage, a flag set at the stage's start from Rommel's hex and cleared on
the first move that separates them, with the spared DP charged when it
clears.
