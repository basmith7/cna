# V-020 — Ship flak in port joins the hex

## What the printed rules say

Commonwealth ships fire their flak only as the fleet rules provide.

## The change

NJHarman's house rule:[^nj]

> CHANGE  While in port, ship Flak points are added to any others in that
> hex. Deployed ships may only fire flak at aircraft flying missions against
> their fleet. Ship flak is considered light.
> When sailing out at sea, flak not in range. Assume “in port” means positioned
> so stuff worth bombing is covered. Otherwise little reason to base in Valetta.

[^nj]: NJHarman, *CfNA House Rules & Interpretations*, § Commonwealth Fleet,
  <https://friendorfoe.com/war/cfna/houserules/#commonwealth-fleet-1>, CC-BY-SA 4.0 (tagged CHANGE). See `ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## Effect on play

Ships in port add their flak to the hex as light flak; ships deployed at sea fire only at aircraft attacking their own fleet.

## Why it is recorded as a variant

It adds a use of ship flak that the printed rules do not give.
