# R-100 — When the schedule and an OA sheet give different arrivals

## Problem

A unit's arrival is printed twice: on the reinforcement schedule and in the
Arrives column of its OA sheet. For a few units the two disagree. The 5th
Panzer Regiment's HQ, its two tank battalions and the 2nd Machinegun
Battalion are each one or two Operations Stages earlier on their OA sheet
than on the Axis schedule, and the 22nd Guards Brigade's sheet prints no
arrival at all. Later in the schedule the 102nd Anti-tank
Regiment, the 22nd Armoured Brigade HQ (perhaps a return) and the 78th
Field Regiment differ in the same way. Both charts were read twice from two scans, so the
difference is in print. Where it lies is listed in
`data/tables/reinforcement-schedule.json`.

## Options

1. **The schedule governs.** Arrivals are taken from the schedule; the OA
   column is a summary that may lag behind it.
2. **The OA sheet governs.** The unit's own sheet is more specific.
3. **The earlier of the two.**

## Decision

Option 1. A unit the Reinforcement Schedule names arrives when the schedule says; an OA sheet's Arrives column decides only for units the schedule does not name, which come with their parent unless their own date is later.

## Rationale

20.1 makes the Reinforcement Track the source of arrivals, and 4.42 reads a
sheet's own dates only to hold back members of an arriving parent; the
scenarios say the same ("all reinforcements as per the Reinforcement
Track", 60.37). So the schedule is the primary chart and the OA column a
summary, the same order R-096 sets between a scenario's set-up and the OA
column. Taking the earlier date (option 3) would let a player pick between
charts, and the OA sheet (option 2) is silent for the 22nd Guards Brigade.
The differences are one or two stages, so the choice moves little. The
schedule data already carries the schedule's dates.

supersession like any ruling (see [rulings/README.md](README.md)).
