# V-009 — Air missions flown by squadron

## What the printed rules say

Aircraft fly missions individually or in any grouping their owner likes, and arriving reinforcements enter on the turns the reinforcement schedule (34.84) gives.

## The change

NJHarman's house rule:[^nj]

> Many rules below refer to squadrons. A squadron is all aircraft from same SGSU
> on a particular mission. For the most part missions are flown by squadron.
> Recon, Transport being exceptions.
> Mostly to discourage micro-managing missions to reach next column on Air
> Bombardment table.

> [34.84] Spread aircraft out more evenly but still keep them in squadrons. So,
> the 53xSM79 in squadrons across Turns. But, the 5xZ501 all arriving in same Turn.

[^nj]: NJHarman, *CfNA House Rules & Interpretations*, § Air War,
  <https://friendorfoe.com/war/cfna/houserules/#air-war>, CC-BY-SA 4.0 (tagged CHANGE ADDITION, for the whole Air War section). See `ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## Effect on play

Most missions are flown by whole squadrons (the aircraft of one SGSU on one mission); recon and transport may still split. Large reinforcement batches arrive spread across turns, still grouped as squadrons.

## Why it is recorded as a variant

The printed rules have no squadron unit for missions and set the reinforcement turns; both are changed.
