# R-160 — Where Rommel's Arrival's unattached motorisation points start

## Problem

With the Land Game alone, 61.72 gives the Axis 290 unattached motorisation
points and the Commonwealth 90 "in other locations" (besides 150 in
Alexandria or Cairo), but names no hexes. 59.63 counts unattached points
hex by hex from the second- and third-line trucks, and the scenario prints
no location for those either. Motorisation points sit in hexes (32.5x), so
a game cannot start without one.

## Options

1. **With the supply units.** The owner divides the points freely, at
   set-up, among the hexes its supply units may start in under 61.72: the
   Axis among the twenty coast-road hexes from El Agheila to Nofilia, the
   Commonwealth among Benghazi, Tobruk and the dump sites.
2. **Anywhere.** The owner places them in any hex of its territory (Libya
   for the Axis, sheets A–D for the Commonwealth).
3. **With any friendly unit.** Any hex holding a friendly unit once the
   rest of the side's set-up is placed.

## Decision

Option 1. The Axis 290 go anywhere on the coast road from El Agheila
(A1816) to Nofilia (A2703), and the Commonwealth's 90 in Benghazi, Tobruk or
any of the twelve dump sites, divided as the owner likes.

## Rationale

The printed text gives no place, so the choice turns on what the points are
for. The scenario's own note urges second-line trucks onto supply work, and
59.66 B lets unattached points start carrying supply units only where every
full 30 share a hex with one: points kept apart from the supply units start
idle. The supply units' set-up hexes are already lists, so this reading
gives an engine a finite hex list with no new rule, and it keeps the points
out of the front line, where "anywhere" would let them start as a free
motorised screen. Options 2 and 3 were rejected for those reasons; neither
is what the printed text says either.
