# R-027 — Tobruk's port efficiency level

## Problem

Case 55.12 gives Tobruk as its example of a port's efficiency level and
names the figure 5. The *San Giorgio* costs Tobruk three levels (55.25),
and both campaign set-ups start Tobruk at level 7 because of the wreck
(60.7, 61.6), which implies an unblocked level of 10 on the Port Capacity
Chart (55.3). The printed figures cannot all be right. NJHarman:[^nj-tob]

> The rules, charts, and scenarios claim various values for
> Tobruk port efficiency. It is 5.

[^nj-tob]: NJHarman, *CfNA House Rules & Interpretations*, § Logistics (in
  *Opinionated Additions and Changes*), <https://friendorfoe.com/war/cfna/houserules/#logistics>, CC-BY-SA 4.0
  (tagged CORRECTION). See `ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## Options

1. **The chart governs.** Tobruk's full level is the figure on the Port
   Capacity Chart; 55.12's "5" is an illustration, and the scenarios' 7 is
   that level less the wreck's three. Consequence: the charts, 55.25 and
   the scenarios agree; only an example sentence is set aside.
2. **Level 5** (NJHarman's reading). He adds: [^nj-tob]

   > It’s a wide open harbor, but not equivalent to other 10’s such as
   > Alexandria and Tripoli. The San Giorgio does not block it.

   Consequence: Tobruk is much weaker as a port for whoever holds it; the
   *San Giorgio* reduction is dropped, which is the change recorded as
   [V-001](V-001.md), so this option adopts part of a variant.
3. **Level 5 as the blocked level.** Read 55.12 as describing Tobruk with
   the wreck in place (a full level of 8). Consequence: contradicts the
   scenarios' 7 and whatever the chart prints.

The chart's printed figure for Tobruk, read from the archive.org scan (jp2
109, common chart sheet page 15): **efficiency level 5**, stacking points 1
in and 3 out, 1,700 tons. The dagger's note says Tobruk starts the campaign
below "the listed five" because of the *San Giorgio*. So the chart agrees
with 55.12, and only the scenarios' 7 (which implies a full level of 10)
stands apart. Option 1 therefore now means a full level of 5, blocked to 2
at the start; that contradicts the scenarios, not 55.12.
([data/tables/port-capacity.json](../data/tables/port-capacity.json).)

## Decision

Option 1. The Port Capacity Chart governs: Tobruk's maximum level is 5, and with the San Giorgio's three levels lost it starts the campaigns at level 2, not the 7 the scenarios print.

## Rationale

Three printed sources agree on 5 as Tobruk's full level: the example in
55.12, the Port Capacity Chart (55.3), and the chart's own dagger note,
which says Tobruk begins below that listed level because of the wreck.
Together with 55.25's three-level loss they give a starting level of 2.
Only the campaign set-ups' 7 (60.7, 61.6) stands apart, and it would need a
full level of 10 that nothing prints; it is most likely a figure carried
over from an earlier draft of the chart. Option 2 would also give 5, but
by dropping the wreck's reduction, which adopts part of variant
[V-001](V-001.md) against 55.25 and the dagger; option 3 contradicts both
the chart and the scenarios. The chart row in
`data/tables/port-capacity.json` already holds 5, so an engine needs only
the scenario start level set to 2.

supersession like any ruling (see [rulings/README.md](README.md)).
