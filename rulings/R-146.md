# R-146 — The support group in the turn-92 armoured division

## Problem

The Commonwealth Formation Organisation Chart (19.31) gives the armoured
division a support group through turn 91, and the chart's key says the
support group is the Motor Brigade Group after turn 70. The type IV
armoured division, from turn 92, has no support group: it has an infantry
brigade slot instead, and the chart prints no support group row after turn
91. The O/A sheets give the 1st, 2nd and 7th Armoured Divisions only
support group or motor brigade HQs (1st, 2nd and 7th Support Groups, 7th
Motor Brigade), whose `fills` ([R-142](R-142.md)) named only the support
group slot. From turn 92 those HQs fill nothing, and every armoured
division with one starts The Last Chance (turn 102) a shell (9.26).

## Options

1. **A support group or motor brigade HQ also fills the infantry brigade
   slot.** From turn 92 it is judged, as a brigade, by the infantry brigade
   row: three infantry battalions.
2. **It does not.** The type IV division's infantry brigade slot takes only
   an infantry brigade HQ; a support group or motor brigade from turn 92
   is a counter beyond the chart (19.2) and fills nothing.

## Decision

Option 1. Every Commonwealth support group HQ lists `infantry-brigade` in
`fills`, and from turn 92 the support group is judged by the infantry
brigade's composition.

## Rationale

The key ties the support group to the motor brigade from turn 71, and a
motor brigade is an infantry brigade: three motorised battalions, the
type III support group's own three battalions without the artillery it
gave up. That artillery moves to the division itself in type IV, which
gains three artillery units, so the turn-92 chart reads as the support
group renamed and stripped down, not removed. Option 2 makes every
Commonwealth armoured division with its own motor brigade a shell from
turn 92 onward unless it borrows an infantry brigade from an infantry
division, which no historical order of battle shows and no rule asks for.

The chart prints no support group row from turn 92, so the formation
organisation data adds one for turns 92 on with the infantry brigade's
three battalions, marked `ruling: R-146`. The fill is not limited by
period, as no other `fills` entry is: a support group attached to an
infantry division before turn 92 may fill an infantry brigade slot too.
It is a 2 SP formation of infantry battalions, as an infantry brigade is,
so this gives a player nothing the 19.2 attachment rules do not already
allow.

The reverse does not hold: an infantry brigade HQ does not fill a support
group slot before turn 92, since the key gives the support group artillery,
anti-tank and reconnaissance components no infantry brigade has.
