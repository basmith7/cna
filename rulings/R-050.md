# R-050 — What is a unit "without ammunition", and when does it surrender?

## Problem

Case 50.12 makes a unit with no ammunition useless in combat, strips its
ZOC and has it surrender if it is in an Enemy ZOC or is fired on. Two points
are unclear. First, 50.0 and 50.15 let a unit use any ammunition in its hex
(attached trucks or a dump), and 50.12 speaks of ammunition "of any type",
though the game has only one kind of Ammunition Point; so it is not stated
whether the test is the unit's own carried load or everything in the hex.
Second, the sentence on surrender joins "in an Enemy ZOC" and "fired upon"
with "and/or", and does not say when the surrender is resolved (at once on
entering the ZOC, at the end of the phase, or only when an Enemy unit
attacks it).

## Options

1. **Hex test; surrender when attacked or at phase end.** A unit is without
   ammunition only if neither it nor its hex holds any. It surrenders the
   moment an Enemy unit barrages or assaults it, or at the end of any Enemy
   phase in which it is in an Enemy ZOC. Consequence: dumps and first-line
   trucks protect everything stacked with them; surrender is checked at
   defined moments an engine can hook.
2. **Unit test; surrender at once.** A unit is without ammunition when its
   own carried load is zero, whatever the hex holds, and it surrenders
   immediately on being in an Enemy ZOC or being fired upon. Consequence:
   harsher; every unit that has fired its own load and not been refilled is
   exposed, which makes the refill step critical.

## Decision

Open.
