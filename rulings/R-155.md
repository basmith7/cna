# R-155 — When a withdrawal is checked

## Problem

20.83 gives a unit until its stated Game-Turn or Stage to get to Cairo or
Alexandria, and eliminates it otherwise; 20.84 removes it once there. Neither
says at which instant of the stated Operations Stage the check is made, so an
engine cannot tell whether a unit that arrives in Cairo during that stage is
in time.

## Options

1. **At the end of the stated Operations Stage.** Movement during the stage
   still counts; the check follows the stage's last phase.
2. **At the start of the stated Operations Stage,** in its naval convoy
   arrival phase, when that stage's reinforcements appear.
3. **Whenever the unit reaches Cairo or Alexandria** from the stated
   Game-Turn on, and at the end of the stage for one that has not.

## Decision

Option 1. Each withdrawal due in an Operations Stage is resolved once, after
that stage's last phase: removed if it is in Cairo or Alexandria at the
required strength and with its transport, eliminated otherwise.

## Rationale

"On or before the Stage" includes the stage itself, so a unit that gets
there during it is in time; Option 2 would cut a stage off the printed
deadline. Option 3 matches 20.84's wording but gives a player a choice of
moment that changes nothing except which phase the unit sits out, and makes
the engine track a window. One fixed check at the end of the stage is the
plain reading and a single event for an engine. Until then a unit due to go
is an ordinary unit and may fight or move. The strength test is
[R-156](R-156.md)'s and the transport test [R-157](R-157.md)'s.

Decided under Brian's delegation of 2026-09-26; open to dispute and
supersession like any ruling (see [rulings/README.md](README.md)).
