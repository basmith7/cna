# R-082 — How are the *San Giorgio*'s three levels at Tobruk regained?

## Problem

Case 55.25 says the wreck costs Tobruk three efficiency levels but not how
they come back. Levels lost to bombs return one per stage by themselves
(55.18), but blocking and mines are excluded from that; blocking is undone
by engineers (55.26, with a cheap Tobruk rate); and 30.17 lets enemy
engineers remove or sink the ship itself.

## Options

1. **Treat the three levels as blocking.** They do not recover on their
   own; engineers clear them one level per stage at Tobruk's 55.26 rate.
   Consequence: whoever holds Tobruk can restore the port in three stages
   for a modest supply cost, ship or no ship.
2. **Tied to the ship.** The levels stay lost while the ship is in the
   harbour and all three return when it is removed or sunk under 30.17.
   Consequence: the Commonwealth, once it holds Tobruk, deals with the ship
   once; the Axis can never restore the port while the ship is there.
3. **Both.** The ship's removal restores nothing by itself; after removal
   the levels are cleared as blocking (option 1). Consequence: slowest
   recovery.
