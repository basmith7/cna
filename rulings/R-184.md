# R-184 — What level the Commonwealth wins in The Long Retreat

## Problem

In The Long Retreat only the Axis scores points. 63.92 gives the Axis a
strategic victory for 10 or more points, a "substantive" one for 4 to 9
and a "marginal" one for 1 to 3, and says only that with no points the
Commonwealth "wins", naming no level. Our levels are tactical, decisive
and strategic, and the margins list gives a level to the side ahead, which
at zero points is nobody. cna-engine asked which level the Commonwealth
gets and how the data says it.

## Options

1. **Tactical.** Zero points is a Commonwealth tactical victory, the lowest
   level, as an unnamed "Commonwealth Victory" is in Rommel's Arrival.
2. **Strategic.** Zero points is the best the Commonwealth can do, so it is
   its top level.
3. **Decisive.** A middle reading.

## Decision

Option 1. With no Axis points the Commonwealth wins a tactical victory; in the data a margins row may name the side that wins its band whoever is ahead.

## Rationale

The printed text names a level for every Axis band and none for the
Commonwealth's, and where SPI leaves a win unnamed elsewhere (61.8's plain
"Commonwealth Victory") our data records it as tactical; reading more into
the silence than that would invent a scale the scenario does not have.
The Commonwealth cannot score here, so "the side ahead" does not decide
this band: a margins row now carries an optional `side`, and the row
`{"min": 0, "level": "tactical", "side": "cw"}` gives the zero band to the
Commonwealth. Rows without `side` keep their meaning.

Decided under Brian's delegation of 2026-09-26; open to dispute and
supersession like any ruling (see [rulings/README.md](README.md)).
