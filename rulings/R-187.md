# R-187 — "Any two villages or cities in Egypt" in Rommel's Arrival

## Problem

The Axis strategic victory in Rommel's Arrival (61.8) needs Benghazi,
Tobruk and Bardia "plus any two villages/cities in Egypt". Our map data
marks places as villages, major cities, oases, birs and features, and
splits Alexandria into two one-hex places. The case does not say whether
an oasis counts as a village, or whether Alexandria's two hexes are one
city or two. cna-engine asked for the hold as typed data.

## Options

1. **Villages and cities, Alexandria once.** Any two places in Egypt whose
   kind is village or major city count; oases, birs and features do not;
   Alexandria's two hexes are one city, held if either is held.
2. **Oases too.** As option 1, with Siwa and the other oases counted.
3. **Alexandria twice.** As option 1, but each Alexandria hex counts as a
   place of its own.

## Decision

Option 1. The Axis needs two places in Egypt of kind village or major city, holding each whole; an oasis is not a village, and Alexandria counts once, for either hex.

## Rationale

Our map data, read from the printed map, keeps oases as a kind of their
own beside villages and cities, so option 2 reads "village" more widely
than the map does. Alexandria is one city printed across two hexes; our
data holds it as two one-hex places, and R-181 already treats the pair as
one city, so option 3 would let one city count twice. Holding a place
means what it means elsewhere in the victory data: every hex of it, so
Cairo needs all five.

In the data the objective is `{"hold": {"region": "egypt"}, "count": 2,
"place_kinds": ["village", "major-city"], "counted_once":
[["alexandria-east", "alexandria-west"]]}`.

Decided under Brian's delegation of 2026-09-26; open to dispute and
supersession like any ruling (see [rulings/README.md](README.md)).
