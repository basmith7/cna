# R-164 — A combat unit with no TOE strength points left

## Problem

A unit whose vehicles have all broken down keeps its counter with no TOE
strength points in it: the broken-down points sit under their own marker
(21.42), and 19.62 says only a unit wiped out by combat or attrition, not
by breakdown, is gone for good. Rommel's Arrival starts the 7th Armoured
Division with every tank broken down (61.38), so its tank battalions begin
the game this way. No case says what such a counter is while it stands at
zero. Does it exert a zone of control, can it be barraged, does it hold
off, and what happens when it is left alone next to the enemy, the way
10.29 captures a non-combat unit?

## Options

1. **Still a combat unit.** It keeps every combat unit's duties and
   protections: its stacking points count toward 10.11's ZOC test, it is
   a barrage target, it must be held off, and nothing captures it. With no
   points to lose it can never be destroyed: a row of such counters is a
   screen that barrage cannot hurt and an assault cannot remove.
2. **A non-combat unit while at zero.** Until it absorbs a TOE point it is
   treated like a bare HQ: it exerts no ZOC and its stacking points do not
   count toward 10.11's ZOC test (they still count against the stacking
   limit); it is not a barrage target (its trucks are still exposed to the
   barrage's truck roll); it neither assaults nor holds off; it may not
   voluntarily enter an unoccupied enemy-controlled hex; and if it is alone
   in an enemy ZOC during the enemy's movement or combat phase it is
   captured under 10.29 and removed for good, giving no prisoner points.
   It keeps its place in its formation and its CPA, and absorbs
   replacement points as usual (19.61); with one TOE point it is a combat
   unit again.
3. **Eliminated at zero.** It is removed as soon as it has no points, as
   if destroyed. That is the reading 19.62 rules out by name.

## Decision

Option 2. A combat unit with no TOE strength points is treated as a
non-combat unit until it absorbs one: no ZOC, no barrage target, no
assault or holding-off, and captured under 10.29 when alone in an enemy
ZOC; it keeps its counter, its slot and its CPA, and is rebuilt with
replacement points.

## Rationale

The printed text comes closest in three places. 3.5 makes a counter's
abilities those of its points, so one with none has a CPA and nothing
else; 3.34 says an HQ whose last gun point is lost "reverts" to a
non-combat HQ rather than being destroyed, which is this situation for an
HQ; and 10.29 captures any non-combat unit alone in an enemy ZOC that "has
no strength of any type", the words that describe a counter at zero. The
rest follows from those: 10.15 already denies a ZOC to a hex under ten
defensive close assault points, and 12.22 already excludes HQs without TOE
points from barrage. Option 1 creates the indestructible screen the
Problem describes, and option 3 contradicts 19.62's explicit exception for
breakdown, which exists so that repaired vehicles (returned as replacement
points, 22.5) can rebuild the unit they came from. Capture yields no
prisoner points because prisoners are counted in TOE points (§28) and the
unit has none; 3.36's one point is for an HQ, which this is not.

For an engine, option 2 matches its present behaviour in an assault: when
an assault leaves a side nothing with TOE in the hex, the zero-strength
counters still there are alone in the assaulting units' ZOC during the
enemy's combat phase and are captured, which removes them as the engine
does now. Where the enemy hex exerts no ZOC (10.15), they stay.
Consistent with R-138, where Rommel, a unit with no strength, is captured
alone in an enemy ZOC under 10.29.
