# R-118 — The CP a holding-off failure retreat costs

## Problem

When a unit is left with no way to attack or hold off the enemy hex that
threatens it, 10.36 has it retreat three hexes, says all its CP are played
for that movement, and gives it three disorganisation points besides any the
retreat earns. Two readings of the CP part:

- The unit is charged up to its full CPA, and the retreat's own CP are
  charged on top, so every point of the retreat is overspend.
- The retreat is paid out of the CP the unit has left, and whatever is left
  after the retreat is lost; only a retreat that costs more than the unit has
  left overspends.

## Options

1. **All CP gone, retreat on top.** Charge the unit up to its CPA, then the
   retreat's cost as overspend. The retreat always earns disorganisation
   (any retreat costs CP) and always pushes the unit past its CPA.
2. **The retreat is paid from the CP left; the rest is forfeited.** The
   unit's CP for the stage end at its CPA when the retreat is done. The
   retreat earns disorganisation only if it costs more than the unit had
   left.

## Decision

Option 2. The retreat is the movement the unit's CP are "played" for: it pays
its CP from what the unit has left, and whatever remains is forfeited with no
disorganisation. The three disorganisation points are added whatever the
retreat costs.

## Rationale

The printed phrase ties the CP to the movement itself, so the CP are the
retreat's price, not a second charge beside it. "Any garnered by the
retreat" is also a conditional: under Option 1 it would be a certainty,
because every retreat would overspend, and the sentence would not need
"any". Option 2 also keeps 10.36 in line with 6.2's single rule that
disorganisation comes from CP spent beyond the CPA, and with the case's
evident purpose, which is to take the unit out of the rest of its movement,
not to double its penalty (the three points are already the penalty). A
unit that has already spent its CPA (or more) has nothing left, so every CP
of its retreat is overspend. The retreat still gets a breakdown check for
vehicles, as any involuntary movement does. For an engine: charge the retreat
as ordinary movement; then set `cp_spent` to the CPA if it is below it,
earning nothing for the difference; then add 3 disorganisation points.
