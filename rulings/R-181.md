# R-181 — How the 18th Australian Brigade arrives in Rommel's Arrival

## Problem

61.38 has the 18th Australian Brigade appear in Alexandria in the first
OpStage of April 1941 (Game-Turn 27 OpStage 1) and move from the next
April OpStage. Its row on the Reinforcement Schedule is Game-Turn 14
OpStage 3, before the scenario starts on Game-Turn 26 OpStage 3, and no
set-up block places it. The scenario does not say whether the brigade
brings the first-line trucks its schedule row lists (12 light, 30 medium,
10 heavy), whether "appears" means its whole OA sheet or only the HQ, or
which Alexandria hexes it may enter. cna-engine asked for the landing as
data so it can bring the brigade on.

## Options

1. **The schedule row, moved.** The brigade arrives exactly as its schedule
   row would, its whole OA sheet with the row's trucks, but on Game-Turn 27
   OpStage 1 and in either Alexandria hex, divided as the owner likes. Its
   schedule row does not apply in this scenario.
2. **Without trucks.** As option 1, but the brigade lands with no
   first-line trucks, since the scenario names none.
3. **The HQ alone.** Only the HQ counter appears; its battalions come by
   their own schedule rows, if any.

## Decision

Option 1. The 18th Australian Brigade arrives in the arrival phase of
Game-Turn 27 OpStage 1 with its OA sheet and the 12 light, 30 medium and
10 heavy trucks its schedule row lists, in the Alexandria East or West
hexes as the Commonwealth player divides it; the schedule's Game-Turn 14
row is ignored.

## Rationale

61.38 changes only when and where the brigade arrives; everything else
about an arriving unit comes from the schedule, which the scenario says
is used "except as listed below". A unit named on the schedule stands for
its OA sheet, as in a set-up (59.2), and the brigade's battalions have no
schedule rows of their own, so option 3 would leave them out of the game.
Option 2 reads the scenario's silence as a rule, and would give the one
brigade released outside the 61.38 limit no way to move except by rail or
sea; nothing suggests that was meant. Alexandria is two places in our map
data, and the release block already treats both as one city.

The same shape serves any scenario whose landings differ from the
schedule: `sides.<side>.arrivals` names the units, the OpStage, the
placement and the trucks, and a unit named there does not also arrive by
the schedule in that scenario. Rommel's Arrival is the only one today.
