# R-042 — How the evaporation rates combine

## Problem

Case 49.3 gives a 6 % per-turn loss, a further 5 % in each hot-weather
operations stage, and says the Commonwealth "rate is 9 %" from September
1940 to August 1941. It does not say:

- whether the Commonwealth 9 % stands in for the 6 % or is added to it;
- whether the Commonwealth also takes the 5 % hot-weather loss in that
  period, or whether the 9 % is its whole rate;
- whether the hot-weather loss is rounded down like the 6 % (only the 6 %
  is said to round down);
- whether fuel in convoys at sea, exempt from the 6 %, is also exempt from
  the hot-weather loss;
- whether the 5 % of 49.3 is the same loss as the 5 % of the Land Game
  weather rule (§29.3) or a second one.

## Options

1. **9 % replaces 6 %; hot weather adds 5 % for both sides; all losses
   round down; sea convoys exempt from both; §29.3 and 49.3 describe one
   loss.** Consequence: the reading used in the prose and in
   `data/tables/fuel-evaporation.json` pending this ruling; the smallest
   total loss consistent with the text.
2. **9 % is added to 6 % (15 % per turn).** Consequence: a very heavy
   Commonwealth penalty; does not fit "the rate is 9 %".
3. **9 % is the Commonwealth's whole loss for the turn, hot weather
   included.** Consequence: in hot weather the Commonwealth would lose
   less than the Axis in that period, against the text's intent.
4. **The §29.3 and 49.3 hot-weather losses are separate and both apply.**
   Consequence: 10 % extra per hot stage in the Logistics Game.

## Decision

Option 1. The Commonwealth's 9 % replaces the 6 %, the hot-weather 5 % is one loss (the weather rule's) that both sides take on top, every loss rounds down, and fuel in convoys at sea takes neither.

## Rationale

Each point follows the printed text. Case 49.3 gives the Commonwealth's
"rate" for the period as 9 %: a rate stated in place of another is a
replacement, and a 15 % reading (option 2) needs words the case does not
have. The hot-weather loss is introduced as "in addition" to the per-turn
loss, is triggered by the weather roll rather than by the turn, and is the
same 5 % the weather rules charge in 29.34 at the same moment; counting it
twice (option 4) would give one event two penalties, and treating the 9 % as
covering hot weather too (option 3) would let the Commonwealth lose less in
a heat wave than the Axis. Rounding down is printed only for the per-turn
loss, but a single rounding rule is simpler for an engine and never makes a
loss larger than the text allows. Convoys at sea are off the map, and both
cases speak of stocks on the map, so neither loss reaches them. The scope
of the hot-weather loss (not fuel in units' tanks) is [R-040](R-040.md).
