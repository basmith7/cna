# R-167 — The Last Chance's Axis air facilities

## Problem

63.43 gives the Axis "nine Airfields, eight one Flying Boat Basin and One
Flying Boat Alighting Area", placed anywhere on maps A to D. The "eight"
names nothing, and SPI's errata does not mention it. What does the Axis
start with?

## Options

1. **Eight landing strips.** A noun dropped out: nine airfields, eight
   air landing strips, one basin and one alighting area. Landing strips
   are the one kind the sentence otherwise leaves out.
2. **A stray word.** Nine airfields, one basin, one alighting area and no
   landing strips.
3. **Eight airfields.** "eight" was meant to replace "nine": eight
   airfields, one basin, one alighting area.

## Decision

Option 1. The Axis places nine airfields, eight landing strips, one
flying-boat basin and one alighting area anywhere on maps A to D.

## Rationale

Option 1 keeps every number printed and reads the gap as the one
facility kind missing from the list; every other scenario's set-up gives
the Axis landing strips beside its airfields (Crusader's three, 62.43;
Rommel's Arrival's one, 61.42). Option 2 throws away a printed
number, and option 3 contradicts the "nine" that is printed. Landing
strips carry one squadron each and are lost at capacity zero (36.2), so
the choice adds forward bases rather than servicing capacity: the 48
Axis SGSUs fit on nine airfields with or without them.

Decided under Brian's delegation of 2026-09-26; open to dispute and
supersession like any ruling (see [rulings/README.md](README.md)).
