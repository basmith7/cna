# R-108 — When the El Alamein scenarios start and end

## Problem

63.2's printed Game-Turns are garbled. The start reads like "1OZ" (taken as
Game-Turn 102). The Last Chance's end runs two readings together: after
Game-Turn 102's third stage, and the first stage of Game-Turn 111. The Long
Retreat's end is given as a calendar week in December 1942, not a number;
the data uses Game-Turn 111, OpStage 3 until this is settled.

## Options

1. **One week, then to December.** Both start at Game-Turn 102, OpStage 1;
   The Last Chance ends after Game-Turn 102 (one Game-Turn, as 63.1 says);
   The Long Retreat ends after the Game-Turn the calendar puts at the third
   week of December 1942.
2. **To Game-Turn 111.** The Last Chance runs to Game-Turn 111, OpStage 1.

## Decision

Option 1. Both start at Game-Turn 102, OpStage 1; The Last Chance ends after Game-Turn 102, OpStage 3; The Long Retreat ends after Game-Turn 110, OpStage 3, the fourth week of December 1942.

## Rationale

63.1 says The Last Chance gives the Axis "one week (one Game-Turn)", and an
end after Game-Turn 102 is the only part of the garbled sentence that fits
it; "1st OpStage of Game-Turn 111" reads as a fragment of another scenario's
line (111 is the campaign game's last turn), and option 2's nine-turn Last
Chance contradicts both 63.1 and the "about ten hours" 63.2 gives it. "1OZ"
is 102 with two misset characters. For The Long Retreat, the calendar has
four Game-Turns a month (weeks I to IV, E-024), and the gun charts'
footnotes (4.48, 4.49) fix January 1942's first week at Game-Turn 63 (the 75/27 and
7.5cm guns gain anti-armour then), so Game-Turn 102 is the fourth week of
October 1942 (El Alamein began on 23 October) and December's week IV is
Game-Turn 110. "December 3/IV" is read as its last Game-Turn, week IV.
