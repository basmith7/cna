# R-108 — When the El Alamein scenarios start and end

## Problem

63.2's printed Game-Turns are garbled. The start reads like "1OZ" (taken as
Game-Turn 102); The Last Chance is said to end after the third OpStage of
Game-Turn 102 and also, in the same sentence, at the first OpStage of
Game-Turn 111; The Long Retreat ends "at the end of the December 3/IV,
1942 Game-Turn", a calendar date rather than a number.

## Options

1. **One week, then to December.** Both start at Game-Turn 102, OpStage 1;
   The Last Chance ends after Game-Turn 102 (one Game-Turn, as 63.1 says);
   The Long Retreat ends after the Game-Turn the calendar puts at the third
   week of December 1942.
2. **To Game-Turn 111.** The Last Chance runs to Game-Turn 111, OpStage 1.
