# R-126 — Simplified convoys: no qualifying division far enough west

## Problem

32.63 has the Commonwealth attack every arriving Axis convoy, and 32.64
finds the bomb points by reading down the lane's column of the simplified
convoy bombing chart (32.66) to the row of the westernmost qualifying
Commonwealth division. The chart's first row is 21–40 bomb points, and each
lane's first threshold lies some way into the map (lane 5 at E11, lane 6 at
D11). When every qualifying division is still east of the lane's first
threshold, no row is reached. cna-engine asked whether the convoy is then
not attacked, or attacked on the Air Bombardment Table's 1–20 column.

## Options

1. **Not attacked.** No row is reached, so there are no bomb points and the
   convoy arrives whole.
2. **Attacked on the 1–20 column.** 32.63's "attacks each arriving convoy"
   always holds; a division too far east to reach the chart rolls on the
   weakest column.

## Decision

Option 1. A convoy whose lane's first threshold no qualifying Commonwealth
division has reached is not attacked and arrives whole.

## Rationale

32.64 gives bomb points only from a row of the chart, and the chart prints
no row below 21–40, so with no row reached the attack has nothing to
resolve. The two options also give the same result: on the Air Bombardment
Table (41.51) the Axis naval convoy row of the 1–20 column is no loss on
every roll from 11 to 66 (`data/tables/air-bombardment.json`), so a 1–20
attack could never destroy anything. Option 1 says that directly and saves
an engine a pointless roll.
