# R-113 — Two density shifts for flak

## Problem

The flak procedure (46.0) has the firing player shift his column for the
number of enemy aircraft overhead. Two charts say how much. The notes under
the Anti-Aircraft Combat Results Table shift one column right for every
twelve bomber- or transport-class aircraft in the target group beyond
twelve (24 aircraft, one column). The Flak Adjustment Chart (46.4) counts
all non-fighter aircraft in the hex, in bands of 24 (24–47 one column,
48–71 two, 72 or more three). They agree at 24 but part company above it,
and one counts the target group, the other the hex.

## Options

1. **The Flak Adjustment Chart.** 46.0 names it for this step; count every
   non-fighter aircraft over the hex (day and night apart) and use its
   bands.
2. **The table's notes.** Count the target group's bomber and transport
   aircraft; one column per twelve beyond twelve.
3. **The larger of the two.**

## Decision

Option 1. Shift by the Flak Adjustment Chart: count every non-fighter aircraft over the hex (day and night apart), one column per band of 24, up to three; the notes under the results table are not used.

## Rationale

The rules text (46.0) sends the player to the Flak Adjustment Chart for
this step, and a chart printed for the purpose outranks a note under
another table. The chart is also the one 46.4 restates. The two agree at
the commonest sizes (one shift at 24 aircraft) and differ only for very
large raids, where the note would grow without limit; the chart's cap of
three columns keeps flak from wiping out a big formation regardless of the
guns firing. Option 3 would apply both readings' worst case, which no text
supports. One count per hex (rather than per target group) is also the
simpler state for an engine.

Decided under Brian's delegation of 2026-09-26; open to dispute and
supersession like any ruling (see [rulings/README.md](README.md)).
