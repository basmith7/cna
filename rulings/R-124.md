# R-124 — Abstract supply: which HQs the air lift reads

## Problem

32.37 lets a player fly one supply unit a turn, but only as far east
(Axis) or west (Commonwealth) as the farthest hex row where a
friendly division stands, "shell or full", and only into a hex with a friendly
division or brigade HQ that is not a battle group. Two things are left to
the reader. First, which counters are "brigade" HQs: 9.22 makes Italian and
German regiments brigade equivalents, the Commonwealth's Support Groups and
the Axis garrison and detachment HQs are brigade-sized without the name,
and the Afrika Korps HQ is above a division. Second, where a division
"occupies" a hex row: 9.21 makes a division its HQ plus everything attached,
which need not share one hex. cna-engine needs both to model the air lift,
and asked for each HQ's echelon as data.

## Options

1. **By organisational level, rows from the division HQ.** A destination is
   any HQ whose formation is a division or a brigade equivalent (regiment
   HQs included, 9.22); battle-group HQs (Kampfgruppe and Gruppe counters,
   19.73) and the corps HQ do not qualify. The farthest row is the row of
   the farthest friendly division HQ counter, whatever is attached to it.
2. **By printed title.** As option 1, but only HQs whose counter says
   division or brigade qualify; regiment, Support Group and garrison HQs
   do not.
3. **Rows from any unit of a division.** As option 1, but the farthest row
   is that of any unit assigned and attached to a division, wherever it
   stands.

## Decision

Option 1. Division and brigade-equivalent HQs (regiments included, battle
groups and the corps HQ not) are destinations, and the limit is the row of
the farthest friendly division HQ counter.

## Rationale

The rulebook already tells the reader to judge an HQ by its level, not its
name: 9.22 says counter titles mislead and Italian regiments are brigade
equivalents, so a title-only reading (option 2) would bar every Italian
infantry regiment HQ while letting in nothing the rule meant to exclude.
The one exclusion the text names, the battle group, is a counter class of
its own (19.73), and a corps HQ is neither of the two levels named.

"Shell or full" means the division counts whatever has been stripped from
it; 9.26's shell is a property of the division HQ's formation, and a
division HQ with nothing attached is the emptiest shell. So the HQ counter
is what marks the division's position, and an engine has one counter per
division to look at (against option 3, which would also let one forward
battalion stretch the lift).

For an engine: every HQ in `data/oa/*.json` carries `echelon` (`corps`,
`division`, `brigade` or `battle-group`). A lift's destination must hold a
friendly HQ of echelon `division` or `brigade`; its row limit comes from
friendly HQs of echelon `division` only. The Italian battle groups of 19.73
have no counters and so never qualify.
