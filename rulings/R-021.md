# R-021 — Sweetening a poisoned well: one unit's CPA, or every unit in the hex?

## Problem

Sweetening a poisoned well (52.17) costs a land unit 5 CP per attempt and
succeeds on a 1–3; attempts may be repeated, and the only stated limit is
the attempting unit's own CPA. Poisoning (52.16) is the
mirror image and is explicit that a failed attempt closes that well for the
rest of the Operations Stage. Sweetening has no such sentence. So the
question is whether the CPA limit is a cap on the whole effort — one unit,
as many tries as its CPA buys — or only a cap per unit, so that a stack of
six units can make six or more attempts at one well in a single stage and
all but guarantee the result. NJHarman's Discord-game notes allow one unit
per Operations Stage to attempt sweetening.[^nj-discord]

[^nj-discord]: NJHarman, private notes from the Discord game, 2026-09
  (paraphrased; not part of his published house rules and not under the
  CC-BY-SA grant, so not quoted).

## Options

1. **One unit per well per Operations Stage** (NJHarman's practice). That
   unit may try as often as its CPA allows at 5 CP a time; no other unit may
   try at the same well that stage. Consequence: sweetening a well is a
   one-unit job whose odds are bounded by that unit's CPA, which is the only
   reading under which 52.17's CPA sentence is a real limit.
2. **Any unit, any number of units.** Each unit in the hex may make its own
   attempts up to its own CPA. Consequence: the literal reading; a
   poisoned well next to a large stack is sweetened almost at will, and the
   1-in-6 poisoning roll is worth far less than its 1 CP suggests.
3. **One attempt per well per Operations Stage, by any one unit**, on the
   analogy of 52.16's failed-attempt rule. Consequence: the two mirror
   actions get the same per-stage cadence; but this contradicts 52.17's
   express permission to repeat attempts, the one thing the case is clear on.

## Decision

None yet — proposed.

## Rationale

Option 1. 52.17 goes out of its way to say attempts may be repeated and
then caps them by a unit's CPA; that cap is meaningless unless the effort
belongs to one unit. Option 3 rewrites the case's plain words and is
rejected; option 2 is the literal reading and, as with drawing water
([R-020](./R-020.md)), is the one to reject on the record. Like R-020 this
is a Logistics Game case with no effect on the Land Game abstraction beyond
the CP cost table's well entries
([Capability points — costs](../rules/30-capability-points.md)).
