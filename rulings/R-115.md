# R-115 — Are there rating-5 pilots?

## Problem

40.15 lists four ratings from 1 up, then a 6 reserved for Marseille.
34.83 has pilot reinforcements rated from one to six arrive each month by
the Pilot Arrival Tables (34.88, 34.89). If those tables print a column for
rating 5, the two cases disagree; if they do not, 34.83 is loose wording.
The Pilot Arrival Tables are now data (`data/tables/pilot-arrival.json`).

## Options

1. **Whatever the Pilot Arrival Tables print.** A rating-5 pilot exists if a
   table can produce one; 40.15's list is then incomplete.
2. **No rating 5.** Follow 40.15: a 5 read from a table is an error, and
   such a pilot arrives as a 4.

## Decision

Option 1. Pilots arrive as the Pilot Arrival Tables print, and every table prints ratings 1 to 4 only; with Marseille's 6 that matches 40.15, so there are no rating-5 pilots (outside the optional aces rule, 40.18).

## Rationale

Once the tables were read (Commonwealth, Italian and German, both scans
agreeing), the conflict went away: their columns run One to Four, and the
German table adds Marseille as a 6 in Game-Turn 29. 40.15's list is exact
and 34.83's "one to six" describes the span from the weakest pilot to
Marseille. Following the tables also means an engine needs no rule beyond
the data. Under the optional rule of 40.18 ratings rise by kills without
limit, so a 5 can then be earned; that rule stands apart from arrivals.

Decided under Brian's delegation of 2026-09-26; open to dispute and
supersession like any ruling (see [rulings/README.md](README.md)).
