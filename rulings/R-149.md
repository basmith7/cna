# R-149 — Under abstract supply (§32) a desert raider may raid a supply unit as a supply dump

## Problem

27.55 lets a desert raider raid a supply dump not in a major city: on an
unguarded dump a 1–2 destroys 10 % of its supplies; a guarded dump must
first be got past with two dice against the guards' raw close-assault
defence. 27.56 reveals a dummy dump after the raid. Under §32 there are no
dumps ([R-131](R-131.md)), but each supply unit is "shown by a supply dump
counter" (32.11), and an enemy combat unit entering its hex captures it.
Is a supply unit a dump a raider may raid? cna-engine asked.

## Options

1. **Yes.** A supply unit, real or dummy, is a supply dump for 27.55, 27.56
   and the Desert Raider Raids Table. A successful raid destroys 10 % of its
   ammunition and 10 % of its fuel, each rounded up; a dummy is revealed and
   removed. A raider never captures a supply unit.
2. **No.** 27.55 targets only the dumps of the full game; under §32 a raider
   has nothing to raid but Rommel.
3. **Capture instead.** A raider entering a supply unit's hex with no enemy
   combat unit captures it, as an enemy combat unit would (32.11).

## Decision

Option 1. A supply unit, real or dummy, is a supply dump for 27.5x and the
Desert Raider Raids Table; a success destroys 10 % of its ammunition and
10 % of its fuel, each rounded up, and a raided dummy is revealed and
removed.

## Rationale

32.11 shows a supply unit with a supply dump counter, and 27.55 lets a
raider attack "any dump" outside a major city; read together, the printed
text makes a supply unit a target, and R-131 forbids only building dumps,
not raiding the counters §32 brings. Option 2 strips raiders of the one
target besides Rommel that §32 leaves them. Option 3 contradicts 27.14: a
raider has no combat ratings and is not a combat unit, and 32.11 gives
capture only to combat units. A supply unit holds two kinds of supply, so
10 % is taken of each. Rounding up follows the other percentage losses
decided so far ([R-091](R-091.md), [R-127](R-127.md)), and keeps a raid on
a nearly empty unit from doing nothing; a unit left with no ammunition and
no fuel is removed (32.11). A dummy found by a raid is "found" in 32.18's
sense and comes off the map, which is what 27.56's reveal means for a
dummy dump. The guard check, its chart-versus-text difference and the 6
that eliminates the raider at a guarded dump are unchanged.
