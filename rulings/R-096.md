# R-096 — Units a scenario places before their OA arrival

## Problem

The Group One set-up (60.41) places the 1st Company of the French Motor
Marines at C3926 at the start, but its OA sheet gives it an arrival of
Game-Turn 65. The scenario and the sheet cannot both be right for a game
that starts in Game-Turn 1.

## Options

1. **The scenario wins.** A scenario's set-up overrides the arrival column
   of the OA sheets for the units it places; the counter starts on the map.
2. **The OA sheet wins.** The company is dropped from the set-up and
   arrives when its sheet says.

## Decision

Option 1. A scenario's set-up overrides the OA sheets' arrival column for every unit it places; such a unit starts on the map and does not arrive again.

## Rationale

A scenario's set-up is the specific instruction for that game, and 59.2
already makes it override the OA sheets wherever a unit's composition
differs; treating the arrival column the same way keeps one rule for both.
The same clash recurs in later groups (the 21st Panzer Division's
headquarters in Crusader is placed before its sheet's arrival), so a
general rule serves better than unit-by-unit fixes. For an engine it is
simple: units placed by the scenario are removed from the arrival list.

Decided under Brian's delegation of 2026-09-26; open to dispute and
supersession like any ruling (see [rulings/README.md](README.md)).
